# MUSIC CUE SHEET
## We're From The Future · S01E01 "The Package"

**Total scored:** 9m 40s of a 36m 08s episode — **27%**. Prestige drama norm is
40–55%. The deficit is deliberate.

---

## THE SCORE'S THREE LAWS

1. **Never under a revelation.** The three biggest turns in the episode — the eye,
   "you got an answer," and the knock — play completely dry. Music is for
   *approach* and *aftermath*, never for the blow.
2. **Never over dialogue.** If the score is audible under a line, the score is
   wrong. Duck to -18 dB below dialogue or drop out entirely.
3. **No percussion. No brass. No choir. No trailer risers. No "epic".**

---

## THE PALETTE

| Element | Use |
|---|---|
| **Felt piano** | Two notes. That is the entire melodic content of the episode. |
| **Bowed double bass** | Sub-harmonic, near-inaudible, for dread |
| **A single detuned string** | The double's presence |
| **Processed room tone** | The recorded room tones of this house, pitched down and stretched. **The score is literally made of the house.** |
| **One sine wave** | High, pure, for clause three |
| **Filtered noise** | The countdown pulse |

**The two-note motif.** A falling major second on felt piano, played once every
few bars, unhurried. It is introduced in the teaser as domestic warmth. It returns
in Act Three under the confession, slower, in a minor colour. It returns in Act
Four under "count backwards for me" — **alone, at half speed, unbearably.**

The same two notes mean *home*, then *what he did*, then *her mother.* Nothing
else in the score has a melody at all.

## WHERE THE SCORE DELIBERATELY ISN'T

| Scene | Why |
|---|---|
| The eye (08:55) | A reveal with music is a jump scare. Without it, it is a fact. |
| The double's entire first appearance | If she is scored, she is a monster. Unscored, she is a woman on a screen, and that is far worse. |
| "You got an answer" | The pivot of the episode. Nothing but a refrigerator. |
| The fight | Scoring an argument tells the audience how to feel about a marriage. Don't. |
| The knock | The score has already stopped. There is nothing left to stop. |
| The tag | An institution does not have a theme. |

---

## THE LAST TWO MINUTES

M13 is the only place the score is permitted to build, and it builds to
**nothing.** At 00:00 on the countdown, M14 strips to one low tone. On the knock,
that tone stops.

**There is no music from the knock to the end of the episode.** The final
image — two people shoulder to shoulder, a shape behind frosted glass — plays on
rain and breath alone.

The episode's biggest moment is its quietest. That is the show.
