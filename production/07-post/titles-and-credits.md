# TITLES, CREDITS & END CARD
## We're From The Future · S01E01 "The Package"

---

## MAIN TITLE — 03:20 → 03:38 (18 seconds)

**Black. One sustained low tone, unresolved, more felt than heard.**

Type resolves out of the black **letter by letter**, left to right, over four
seconds. Thin weight, very wide tracking, optically centred:

```
W E ' R E   F R O M   T H E   F U T U R E
```

- Typeface: a neutral grotesque at its thinnest weight. No serifs, no display
  faces, no distressing, no chromatic aberration, no glitch.
- Colour: pure white, no glow, no halation.
- Scale: small. The card should feel too small for the screen.
- **The tone stops before the card does.** Three seconds of a title in total
  silence, then a straight cut.

**No stinger. No whoosh. No sub-drop.** The absence of an impact is the
statement, and it is the first promise the show makes about its own manners.

> **The critical decision:** this typeface and this tracking are **identical to
> the type the leaf displays** in Act Three. Nobody says so and no one shot
> connects them. It means the show's own titles are set in the house style of
> whoever sent the box — which is either a joke or a threat, and the series never
> settles which.

---

## END CARD — 35:20 → 35:32

Black. Silence. Then the same type, same weight, same tracking:

```
W E ' R E   F R O M   T H E   F U T U R E

        END OF EPISODE ONE
```

The second line is 40% of the first's size, sentence-spaced rather than tracked.

**Score:** M15 — the main-title tone, returning, **resolved a semitone lower.**
Twelve seconds. It is the only resolution anywhere in the episode, and it arrives
after the story has stopped, which makes it feel like an apology.

## END CREDITS

Roll on black. White, small, left-aligned at the optical third. Scrolling at
**reading speed, not broadcast speed** — this show is patient about everything.

**No music under the credits.** Room tone only: the recorded tone of the Ward
house living room, played at length, with rain. The audience sits in the empty
house they just left.

Card order:
1. Written by · Directed by
2. Series regulars — Miles, Lauren
3. Guest — the double, the voice at the door, the system voice
4. Director of Photography · Production Designer · Editor · Composer ·
   Supervising Sound Editor · Costume Designer
5. Full crew roll
6. Production company / distributor cards

**The post-credit tag plays after the final card, following four seconds of
black and silence.**

## TYPOGRAPHY SPECIFICATION — the leaf's display

Governed by the same rules as the main title, because it is the same house style.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| `DO NOT GO BACK` | Thin grotesque, wide tracking, pure white, optically centred, ~4% of frame height |
| The countdown | The same face at 55% of that size, directly beneath, monospaced figures so digits do not jitter |
| Motion | **None.** No fade in, no animation, no cursor, no scan. The words are either there or not there. |
| Halation | A very small amount, and only here. The only bloom permitted in the episode. |

**Forbidden absolutely:** typewriter reveals, glitch transitions, terminal green,
scanlines, boot text, progress bars, loading states, any UI chrome whatsoever.

## SUBTITLE STYLE

| | |
|---|---|
| Face | The same grotesque, regular weight |
| Position | Bottom third, centred, two lines maximum, 42 characters per line |
| Speaker ID | Only where a voice is not attributable on screen: `[her voice]`, `[muffled, through the door]`, `[system voice]` |
| **The redaction** | Rendered as `[ - - - ]`. Not `[INAUDIBLE]`, not `[REDACTED]`, not a bleep glyph. A subtitled viewer must be denied the name exactly as a hearing viewer is. |
| SDH | Adds speaker names and authored non-speech captions. **Never describes the mix** — `[silence]`, not `[score drops out]`. |
