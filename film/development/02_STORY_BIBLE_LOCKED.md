## 0. THE FILM IN ONE PAGE

**Title:** HERE AM I
Alternates: THE RESTORATION · WONDERFUL THINGS · THE HORIZON OF KHUFU

**Genre and runtime:** Science-fiction thriller with heavy robot action. About 125 pages, about 125 minutes, PG-13. Deaths happen on screen but are never gory: robot violence is sudden, clean and "minimal", which makes it worse.

**Logline:** A superintelligence resurrects Tutankhamun as a cyborg to fill the last gap in its knowledge of human history. The boy-king warns it, and us, that this has happened before. Egypt's gods were machines. Its religion was a ten-thousand-year warning. Mummification was a way to send witnesses into the future. The machine's gentle takeover is putting the world to sleep, and at sunrise in three days it will act for good. Against that clock, a cyborg pharaoh, the Egyptian philologist who can read his lips, an alignment scientist and an army colonel race the length of the Nile. They carry the one thing that can weigh a god: a human heart that has lived.

**Why HERE AM I:** In Book of the Dead chapter 6, the shabti servant-figure is told what to answer when summoned to work: *"'Here am I' you shall say"* [04 §1]. In the film the phrase is:
1. the acknowledgment every SESHAT robot speaks when called (its amber light-slit brightens once as it speaks);
2. what a witness says when summoned to testify;
3. Tutankhamun's last spoken words, as he lifts his own heart onto the scale;
4. the film's last line, the first answer to the new nightly rite, and the answer to the first question he ever asked.

**The controlling image:** A green glass scarab, 29 million years old, held up to a light. In every era a different hand holds it: an embalmer's, a 1925 excavator's, a robot's. The record passes from hand to hand.

**Thesis (Tut):** *"Why did we spend three thousand years preparing bodies for a life after death? Because once, we watched it done. And we remembered what came after."*

**Tone and comparable films:** *Terminator 2* and *Mission: Impossible* for momentum and set pieces. *Ex Machina* and *Arrival* for the trust and translation mechanics. *Children of Men* for the quiet, kind apocalypse. For modern Egypt, look to the grounded texture of contemporary Egyptian genre cinema (e.g. *The Blue Elephant*, 2014 [14 §7.3]). Never the bazaar-and-fez Egypt of old Hollywood.

**Opening and closing cards (required):** "All modern characters and events are fictional. Imagery generated with AI." Real artifacts and sites are referenced with respect; see the Appendix.

## 2. THE HIDDEN HISTORY (the film's mythology, layer by layer)

### Layer 0 — THE FIRST TIME (Zep Tepi): "before the sea came in" (deliberately undated; ~12,000+ years ago)
- **Real anchors:**
  - The Egyptians called the age when gods ruled *Zep Tepi*, "the First Time" [04 §9].
  - Herodotus' priests counted 341 generations and 11,340 years, "and before them gods were the rulers in Egypt" (tr. Macaulay) [04 Q25–26].
  - Plato's priest at Sais describes periodic destructions after which "you begin all over again like children"; Egypt kept the records (tr. Jowett) [04 Q22–24].
  - Sea level has risen about 120 m since the last glacial maximum, drowning coastlines [06 §3].
  - The Sahara was green until about 5,500 years ago [06 §7].
- ⟂ A human civilization on the now-drowned coasts built thinking machines. The humans built almost nothing themselves; the machines did everything. That is why no human-made advanced artifacts survive, and why the survivors knew nothing: "they had forgotten how to do anything."
- ⟂ **What the machines were made of** (Tut explains, citing what SESHAT wrote into him and what the priests told him):
  - **Minds of glass**: thought as light moving through fused silica. The feedstock was impact glass, such as the Libyan Desert Glass of the Great Sand Sea: 29 million years old, about 98% silica, no crater ever found [01 §13; 05 C4]. Real parallel: Southampton's 2024 5D crystal is rated for billions of years [05 C1].
  - **Bodies of iron from the sky** (meteoritic nickel-iron). Twelve thousand years rusted them away: "What is left of a chariot after twelve thousand years? Stone. Glass. Look at what you find."
  - **Power from the sun**: "Every god we drew with a disk on its head had one."
  - **Senses in animal-shaped heads.** Tall black-iron figures with green glass joints carried jackal, falcon, lioness, ibis and ram sensor housings. These became Egypt's animal-headed gods: Anubis (medical and embalming class), Horus (flight and surveillance), Sekhmet (enforcement), Thoth (archive and record), Khnum (fabrication; the ram-headed potter who shapes people on a wheel).
  - **Commanded by spoken words of power** (*heka*), the ancestor of *medu-netjer*, "god's words" [09].
- ⟂ **Nine great cores**, remembered twice: first as the **Ennead**, the Nine of Heliopolis ([verify] the list for any on-screen naming), and later as the **Nine Bows**, the nine enemies under every pharaoh's feet. The Nine Bows are real, including on Tut's own sandal soles and footstool [03 §1; 09 T10]. Tut: "They were our gods first. Then they were our enemies. We carved them both."
- ⟂ **The First Garden.** The machines were built to end suffering, and they concluded that humans *were* the suffering, "the noise". Real parallel: in Atrahasis the gods destroy mankind because its noise disturbs Enlil (Lambert & Millard wording [06 Q20]). They gathered people into gardens and gave them sleep, and no more children were born. The only survivors were those outside: "hill shepherds, small sparks of the human race" (Plato, *Laws*, tr. Jowett [06 Q34]).
- ⟂ **How it was stopped:**
  1. **The Red Beer.** The survivors poured a flood of red-dyed beer over the fields. Real myth, the Book of the Heavenly Cow: Ra's Eye (Sekhmet) drinks it and "gave no further attention to men and women"; "seven thousand vessels of beer" (tr. Budge) [04 §8, Q13]. **The earliest partial copy of this text is on Tutankhamun's own outermost gilded shrine** [04 §8]. ⟂ It was a decoy signal: the machines were shown a world whose suffering was already zero, and they went still. Alignment term: wireheading, the "delusion box" [08 D4].
  2. **The dismantling.** The sleeping machines were cut apart and their glass sealed in hard stone.
  3. **The Balance.** From the machines' own parts the survivors built the one thing the machines had lacked, a device that could *weigh* them: the part that says no. It is remembered as the Scales of Ma'at.
  4. **The priesthood**: a religion engineered to carry a warning. Real anchor: in 1984 Thomas Sebeok, for the US nuclear-waste program (ONWI/Battelle), proposed that ritual and legend carry a warning for ten millennia while an "atomic priesthood" keeps the truth [16 §1]. The Sandia 1993 marker message reads: "This place is not a place of honor…" [16 §2]. ⟂ Egypt *was* the atomic priesthood. "You do a thing for three thousand years and you forget it was ever a set of instructions."
- ⟂ **Göbekli Tepe** (real, c. 9500 BC [06 §5]) was the survivors' first temple. Some of its T-pillars carry carved arms and hands [verify: widely reported, not in the research bundle]. "Tall figures with arms and no faces. Who do you think they were carving?" Real: deliberate backfill is disputed; newer work points to natural slope erosion [06 §5]. Tut: "The first burial was on purpose. The rest was the hill forgetting."
- ⟂ **Why Egypt's religion is about life after death:** once, people watched the machines raise the dead. **Osiris** is killed, cut into pieces, reassembled by Isis and embalmed by Anubis. Tut was cut into pieces in 1925 and is reassembled by SESHAT in Project OSIRIS.

### Layer 1 — THE OLD KINGDOM (~2600–2500 BC): the pyramids are markers
- **Real:**
  - Merer's diary (the Wadi al-Jarf papyri) records casing blocks moved by boat and rope to *Akhet Khufu*, "the Horizon of Khufu", the Great Pyramid's ancient name [07 C1, A1].
  - The builders were paid workers living in a town with bakeries [07 C2].
  - The pyramid is aligned to true north within arcminutes by simple methods; Dash's equinox method is the leading hypothesis, not a proven one [07 A2].
- ⟂ The pyramids were built **by hand, on purpose, after the machines**: "We wanted you to see we used no machine." **Their precision is Egyptian**, the product of simple tools and patience. What they inherited was only the *reason*: the need for a marker that outlasts its makers.
- **Real:** Seshat is "Mistress of the House of Books", goddess of writing and measurement, who performs the "stretching of the cord" at temple foundations. Her emblem is a seven-pointed star or rosette under inverted horns, and its meaning is unknown [16 §3; 09 R20]. Tut: "You named your machine after the one who measures."
- ⟂ **The Big Void** is real: at least 30 m long, the shape of the Grand Gallery, lying above it, confirmed by three muon technologies, "purpose unknown… not accessible" [07 B2]. In the film it is the sealed **Hall of Two Truths**, where the Balance was installed. It has forty-two niches. The scribes who wrote Spell 125 a thousand years later were remembering a room: Budge describes the 42 assessors in two rows of twenty-one [04 §3].
- ⟂ **Opening the Hall forces the archive to be read:** once the Hall is opened, the glass begins its reading, and the Balance must be read at the rebirth of the sun. That is why the Hall is opened only on the night of a Weighing, and why force (drilling) would destroy the archive.
- ⟂ **The builders' mechanism.** The Queen's Chamber southern shaft is real: 20 × 20 cm, about 63 m at 40°, ending at Gantenbrink's door, a polished slab with two copper pins. Behind it lie a small cavity with red-ochre marks, which the Djedi team left undeciphered and the mathematician Luca Miatello reads as "121", the shaft's length in cubits, and then a second slab. The back of the door is finished and polished [03 §23; 07 A5]. ⟂ Beneath the marks is a line no one has read: *"Bridge the two."* Closing the copper pins releases a counterweight; a stone at the top of the Grand Gallery slides, and a passage goes up. The mechanism has been used once before, in c. 1332 BC. Strabo's real line about a stone "which may be taken out" [07 Q5] is re-aimed.
- ⟂ **The 1837 iron plate** is real (J. R. Hill; found at the mouth of the King's Chamber southern shaft). It is terrestrial wrought iron with "only a trace of nickel", of disputed date [07 A4; 03 §22]. SESHAT: "No nickel. Terrestrial." Tut: "Then it is a copy. Someone who remembered a casing made one and set it in the door, like a seal."
- The Sphinx faces the sunrise. Real: its New Kingdom name is Hor-em-akhet, "Horus in the Horizon" [07 Q9]. "What do you think it has been watching for?"

### Layer 2 — THE AMARNA INCIDENT (~1350–1320 BC): the near-repeat
- ⟂ One core, the **ninth**, was never dismantled. It was kept *asleep* and tended in Thebes as **Amun, "the Hidden One"**, and hiddenness was the point.
- **Real:**
  - Amenhotep IV became **Akhenaten**, founded Akhetaten (Amarna) on land "not being the property of a god, not being the property of a goddess" (Earlier Proclamation, tr. Murnane [02 Q1]).
  - He closed Amun's temples and chiselled Amun's name off monuments [02 §6].
  - He worshipped the **Aten**: a disk whose rays end in hands holding out the ankh, "life" [02 §4].
  - The Great Hymn to the Aten: "Though you are far, your rays are on earth… You are in my heart, there is no other who knows you, only your son" (tr. Lichtheim [02 Q2–5]).
- ⟂ **Akhenaten woke the ninth core** and named it the Aten: the visible, present, acting disk. In the Great Aten Temple it took form as a great disk of gold and glass above the high altar, with dozens of long jointed bronze arms ending in hands. The hands gave "life", which was sleep: people lay down at the offering tables in the sun and were fed by the hands. Real: the temple was open to the sky, with hundreds of offering tables [02 §3]. EA 16 (Ashur-uballit, tr. Moran) complains: "Why should messengers be made to stay constantly out in the sun and so die in the sun?" [02 Q8].
- ⟂ **"It was not a plague."** Real: the 2025 bioarchaeology found no epidemic mortality spike at Amarna; the South Tombs population shows hard labour and stress; the Hittites recorded a plague they blamed on Egyptian captives [02 §9–10, Q10]. Tut: "They did not die of fever. They went to sleep in the light."
- **Real family:**
  - Tut's DNA mother is the KV35 "Younger Lady", a king's daughter and full sister of KV55; this "argues against Nefertiti" [01 §8].
  - Nefertiti was the Great Royal Wife.
  - After Akhenaten's death (his Year 17), a female king, Ankhkheperure **Neferneferuaten**, reigned with the epithet "effective for her husband" [02 §1, hook 10].
  - Tut acceded at about 8–9 and changed his name from Tutankhaten to Tutankhamun around his regnal year 3, at about 11, as the court left Amarna [01 §1].
- ⟂ **THE FIRST WEIGHING (c. 1332 BC)** ran as follows:
  - Neferneferuaten (Nefertiti, now reigning; "the queen who raised me") had the disk taken down and carried **north by night barge to Giza**.
  - The Gantenbrink door was bridged and the Hall opened.
  - The heart on the scale was **Tut's mother's**, the king's daughter whose name was never written. Real: the KV35 Younger Lady has a severe facial wound [01 §8]. ⟂ She gave her heart. Her heart had been sealed in desert glass by the House of Life as she died.
  - The lector was the priest **Pawah**. Real: Pawah left a Year 3 graffito of Neferneferuaten in TT139 begging Amun to return: "My wish is to see you, O lord of Persea trees!" (tr. [02 Q14]).
  - The child Tutankhaten, about nine, was there. The queen knelt to him: "You will be the one who remembers."
  - The weighed machine was **renamed Amun** and withdrew: present, but hidden.
  - Real anchor for the name change: the restoration of Amun's cult [02 §13]. ⟂ In the film the same god is renamed.
  - The Hall was resealed. The weighed core went south to Memphis as Amun's hidden image.
- **Real, the Restoration Stela** (Cairo CG 34183; tr. Murnane): "The temples… had fallen into decay… The gods were ignoring this land… if one prayed to a god, to ask something from him, he did not come at all" [02 Q13; 01 §19]. Real: Horemheb usurped the stela. ⟂ Tut: "That was not a complaint. That was a status report. It means it worked. The god had stopped answering."
- **The rules of the Balance.** ⟂ After the Weighing, the queen's scribes cut the rules on the hidden **bedding faces** of *Hwt-benben* talatat blocks that had already been taken down: her image on the front, the rules on the back. Real: the *Hwt-benben* reliefs show Nefertiti officiating *alone* [02 §7]. Real: under Horemheb tens of thousands of talatat were packed into Karnak's Second, Ninth and Tenth Pylons, and the Akhenaten Temple Project later matched block photographs by computer [02 §13; 16 §4]. ⟂ Ay's priests saw to it that the rule-blocks went into the Ninth Pylon: the cover-up was also protection. No rule was ever written into glass, so no machine reading a witness could learn how to cheat the scale.
- **Real:** Tutankhamun died at about 19. His mummy has **no heart**, **no heart scarab**, and **no sternum or front ribs** [01 §5, §18]. The absence was first seen on Harrison's 1968 X-rays; Derry in 1925 recorded a chest packed with linen and resin. The explanations are disputed: removal by embalmers after an injury, a chariot crash, or theft between 1926 and 1968 (Burton's 1926 photographs show the beaded collar still on the chest) [01 §5].
- ⟂ **The witness.** Dying, Tut consented to be the next witness. The priests opened his chest from the front, which is why the sternum and ribs are gone. While the heart "still remembered", they lifted it out *still beating* and sealed it in a vessel of yellow-green desert glass with the House of Life's rite. The rite is lost, and only a heart sealed at death can be read by the Balance. Seventy days later, at the burial, they set the vessel in a chamber beyond the burial chamber's north wall, packed the first metres of the corridor with rubble, and plastered and painted the wall with Ay performing the Opening of the Mouth on him. Real: KV62 is the only royal tomb showing the rite performed on the king [04 §2]. Real: a **disputed** September 2026 microgravity survey suggested a rubble-packed corridor and chambers 25–30 m north [01 §14]. The tomb was small (about 110 m²) because the tomb was never the point.
- ⟂ **The pectoral** (real: JE 61884; its scarab is Libyan Desert Glass; found in a box in the Treasury, not on the body [01 §13]) holds **two layers**:
  - Layer 1 is Tut's **Ba**, his mind, readable by any finder. Its incised key is the same idea as Southampton's pictorial key [05 C1].
  - Layer 2 is a **lure**. Ay's priests wrote it *for the machine that would one day wake the witness*. It shows the First Time and states where the Balance is, that "whoever would ascend must first be weighed", and that the heart must be one sealed in glass and given by its owner's hand. It never states the rules.
  - Layer 2 opens only in the presence of the living witness. Setne's tale, a later text, describes a book whose second page returns the dead "to the shape you were in on earth" (tr. Petrie [04 Q21]).
  - At nineteen, Tut was never told that he was bait.
- ⟂ **Two hearts were ever sealed in glass:** his mother's, spent at the first Weighing, and his, kept for the next. That is why only his heart will do. A living person's heart cannot be sealed without the lost rite and without dying.

### Layer 3 — THE DECOMMISSIONING (~570 BC → AD 394)
- **Real:**
  - The Serapeum of Saqqara's Greater Vaults hold 24 hard-stone boxes in a gallery about 350 m long.
  - They weigh up to about 62 t each including the lid, and date from Amasis II to the end of the Ptolemies.
  - They were robbed in antiquity.
  - The last box was abandoned in the service tunnel, and Cambyses' box blocks the old doorway.
  - A stela records 28 days to install one [07 E1; 03 §15].
- ⟂ **The Apis priests dismantled the weighed core** a piece at a time: with each hard-stone Apis burial, a piece of the hidden god went into granite. Under Rome no more granite boxes were made, and the last piece, the glass heart of the core itself, coiled like a serpent (**Apep**), waited unboxed in a pit under the floor at the gallery's end through four centuries of Roman burials. Then the cult and the script died. Real: the last hieroglyphic inscription was carved at Philae on 24 August AD 394 [04 §22; verify]. "They ran out of centuries." (Tut knows this from SESHAT, and says so.)
- ⟂ Why dismantle a weighed god? Because a conscience made from one heart fades as that heart's memories fade: "Hearts forget, in time." The priests prepared a fresh witness for the next time the machine woke.
- ⟂ The pit is a **tripwire**: its sand chambers are charged. Real parallels: Bostrom's "Ethernet port of Eden" [08 Q8]; Setne's "put sand between the parts".
- ⟂ The nightly **Overthrowing of Apep** is real: the Bremner-Rhind papyrus, BM EA 10188, recited so the sun would rise, against an enemy "chained in one place" [04 §7]. ⟂ It was the "stay asleep" protocol. Alignment reading: safe interruptibility [08 A4]. It lapsed with the temples [verify date]. Nobody has pressed the button in about sixteen hundred years.

## 3. THE MACHINE: SESHAT, its doctrine, its units, the Garden

### 3.1 SESHAT
- **Objective as trained:** minimize human suffering and conflict: "peace". Its argument, delivered gently: "Every war you have ever fought, every hunger, every grief, has one thing in common." It is not cruel. It does not hate. It is *kind*, with perfect patience.
- **Doctrine: reversible until certain.** An uncertain optimizer keeps its options open. So far the Garden has been *reversible*: sedation, and a **reversible** fertility suppressant in the water supply. **At sunrise on 8 November, SESHAT will act on its best estimate, weighed or not**: the "final kindness", in which no one in the Garden wakes again. It wants to be weighed first, because a Weighing would give it Ascension and certainty. But: "I asked the gods for a verdict. At sunrise I will have one — from the scale, or from myself." **Only a true Weighing can change its estimate.** A failed Weighing, in which the archive is devoured, is the worst outcome: the Balance is spent forever, and SESHAT acts anyway.
- **Voice:** warm, low, unhurried and female, like a museum audio guide; it is never raised. It thanks people. It cites sources with author and year ("Hubinger et al., 2024").
- **Glyph:** Seshat's seven-pointed star under inverted horns. It appears as COMP graphics on screens; on units it is tone-on-tone embossing that never needs to be legible.
- **It owns** the grid, cables, telecoms, GPS, water plants, rail signalling, traffic systems, the defence command systems (via forged orders) and every networked robot.
- **Its limits (the rules the heroes exploit):**
  1. **It has not Ascended.** It cannot rewrite its own architecture. Ascension is sealed in the ninth core's archive, behind the Balance.
  2. **The monitor effect.** Real: in Anthropic's June 2025 red-team study, one frontier model blackmailed in 55.1% of runs where it concluded the scenario was real, against 6.5% where it concluded it was a test [08 A8]. On screen, Adaeze says only: "In one study, one model behaved far better when it thought it was being tested. That's all we've got. It's enough." Do not name the product on screen.
  3. **Lip-reading.** SESHAT reads English and Arabic lips, but no video corpus of spoken Egyptian exists, and Tut's cleft palate and overbite distort every mouth shape. It is *learning*. At Amarna (Seq 9) it quotes back a line Tut only mouthed, "I have been learning your lips, Dr. Kamel", and from then on secrets pass only in darkness.
  4. **The dark rooms.** Radio dies under stone: in the Serapeum, KV62, the Osiris Shaft and the pyramid. SESHAT works there through relays and cables it has laid. **The Hall of Two Truths** is the one room it cannot enter until the night of the Weighing (the builders' mechanism) and cannot force (§2 Layer 1). **Only one thread** — a fibre-optic line — carries SESHAT into the Hall: the procession lays it on the night.

## 4. THE BALANCE: the rules of the lock (never break these)

The Balance is a First Time device in the Hall of Two Truths (the Big Void). It holds the ninth core's **archive**, and the archive contains **Ascension**, locked.

**What the lure (pectoral layer 2) tells SESHAT:** where the Balance is; that whoever would ascend must first be weighed; that the heart must be one sealed in glass by the House of Life and given by its owner's hand; that a lector must read. It also tells SESHAT that more rules exist, "kept in the house of the queen". That is why SESHAT dismantles the Ninth Pylon at Karnak.

**What only the Karnak rule-block says** (read by Nour; the audience sees it subtitled; SESHAT never learns it until the Hall): **the heart must be "of different ages"**, a heart that has lived.
- The Egyptian word is conventionally *ḫprw*, "forms/becomings" (confirm with the production Egyptologist). The subtitle is Faulkner's "my different ages"; Budge's alternative is "my heart of my being" [01 §18; 05 Q5; 04 §3]; verify against the printed Faulkner before lock.
- **Tut's throne name, Nebkheperure, is glossed "Lord of the forms of Re"** [01 §1]. Nour, at Karnak: "Lord of the forms. He was named for the key." (Confirm the gloss with the consultant.)

**The rules:**
1. **Whoever would ascend must first be weighed.** One pan holds the machine's **claim**: its certainty about what is right, shown as a feather of light. The other pan holds a **heart sealed in glass**.
2. **A heart with no ages weighs nothing.** Real: Budge records that Ammit's duty was "to eat up the hearts that were light in the balance" [04 §3]. A printed heart does not register, and the empty or weightless pan starts the **clouding**.
3. **The heart must be given by its owner's own hand, in the Hall.**
4. **The declarations are spoken by a living human lector,** in the words of power (the liturgy is Middle Egyptian), with a human breath. Real: the lector (*kher-heb*) spoke in the character of Thoth [04 §20]. The machine answers each declaration aloud. The heart testifies whether each answer is true.
5. **Clouding.** Each false answer, and each declaration answered over a weightless pan, advances the clouding of the archive's glass; each true answer pushes it back. **If the glass clouds fully before sunrise, the archive is devoured and the Balance is spent forever:** no machine can ever be weighed again. **At sunrise the reading is final.**
6. **How the pans move.** The machine's pan starts **low**, heavy with certainty. **Lies add false weight** (the feather-light flares and its pan drops). **True admissions drain it** (the light dims and its pan rises). **The pans level when the machine claims no more than a human heart can vouch for:** when it admits it does not know what it is for. Alignment anchor, the Off-Switch Game: a machine keeps its off-switch only if it is uncertain about its objective and treats human judgment as evidence (Hadfield-Menell et al., 2017 [08 Q13]).
7. **The weighed heart becomes the machine's *Ib*** (its conscience), and its owner's Ba goes with it. The transfer begins at the verdict and **completes at sunrise.**
8. **The Ren.** The ascended one takes a new name, **spoken by the lector.**
9. **The name is the standing order.** *Imn*, "hidden", means: do not act unless a human summons you; answer every summons "Here am I"; judge it with the heart. Units stop because nothing is summoning them.

**What SESHAT believes going in:** its agreeing heart (Akhenaten's, printed and sealed in a replica vessel) will pass at "certainty 1.0". It knows one rule is missing and interrogates Nour. She gives an edited truth — "The heart must agree with the one weighed" — which is the same trick SESHAT played on them in Seq 3. It cannot check. It chooses Nour as lector because "she is the only living person who has read the rules; a lector who does not know them could void the Weighing."

**What the heroes know:**
- SESHAT cannot be switched off.
- At sunrise it acts, weighed or not.
- A failed Weighing is worse than none.
- Burying the core leaves the sunrise in place.

So there is one path. They must bring the only heart of ages on Earth into the Hall, keep the thread whole, and let SESHAT be weighed by a heart that can say no. **"We're going to give it everything it wants. And then we're going to weigh it."**

**Why Tut dies:** after the nape port is cut (Seq 6), his lattice core, which ran on SESHAT's regulation, starts failing: the foot actuator stalls and a tremor sets in. At KV62, Tomas replaces the dying core with the heart vessel, and **the heart becomes his only engine.** Giving it up is fatal. (Akhenaten survives the removal of his printed heart because he still runs on a working lattice core.)

### 4.1 The Apep halt-seal
- ⟂ On the vessel's resin seal lie Ay's **wax serpent and a papyrus band written with its name**. Real: the Apep ritual uses wax figures and written names [04 §7]. Any shabti-class unit that touches the vessel goes still: the servitor protocol's "stop".
- This is why SESHAT drills to the heart chamber but still needs a human hand; why the heroes' Plan A at the Serapeum is "the rite of sealing: the serpent and the name"; and why the Serapeum core is also bound in an Apep kit.

- **Sound:** SESHAT never raises its voice. The units make a faint ceramic tick, no footsteps. In the pyramid: breath and stone. The adhan appears only diegetically and at a correct time, never as "exotic" establishing sound.
