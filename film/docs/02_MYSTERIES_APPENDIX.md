# HERE AM I — Mysteries & Artifacts Appendix

*The real artifacts, sites, hieroglyphs, texts, studies and myths the film uses, what is actually known about each, and exactly where the film's fiction begins.*

Companion to the Story Bible (v3). Facts come from the production's research bundle (files 01–16, compiled 22 September 2026). Scene numbers (for example "Seq 2.3") refer to the Story Bible's sequence breakdown. All modern characters and events in the film are fictional, and no living person is depicted.

## A. How to read this appendix

HERE AM I is a fiction built on real things. A boy-king's missing heart, a void inside the Great Pyramid, a glass scarab from a 29-million-year-old impact and a tomb text about beer are all real. Each entry below says what the thing is, what specialists conclude, and where the story takes over.

**Status tags.** Each entry carries one or more of these:

- **ESTABLISHED:** the mainstream consensus, safe to state as fact.
- **DISPUTED:** a live disagreement between qualified experts.
- **FRINGE:** the object is real, but the extraordinary reading (aliens, lost high technology, electricity) is rejected by specialists.
- **HOAX:** fabricated, or never shown to exist.

Many entries need two tags. The Baghdad Battery, for example, is a real jar (ESTABLISHED) and not a battery (the battery reading is FRINGE).

**The ⟂ convention.** The symbol **⟂** marks where the documented record ends and the film's invention begins. Everything before ⟂ is sourced; everything after it is story. "⟂ None" means the film stays inside the record.

**Flags kept from the research.** Much of the research was done while primary websites were blocked, so facts were confirmed through search extracts, verbatim mirrors and public-domain texts.

- **[verify]** marks a detail that is standard but was not confirmed at its primary source. Check it before quoting it.
- **[DISPUTED]** marks a figure that sources give differently. Where two sources disagree, both are given.
- **"(background)"** marks a figure recorded from prior knowledge.

**The rule of the Readings.** *What Tut laughs at is false; what silences him is real.* In the Debunk Reading (Seq 2.3) and in Rami's notebook (Seq 6.3), Tut's dismissals are the mainstream verdict, each with one checkable detail. He spends credibility on the hoaxes before he cashes it on the open questions. He never validates ancient astronauts, a Younger Dryas comet, Sitchin, Schoch's early Sphinx date, or Sumerian kings as spacemen. Anything from after his death in c. 1323 BC he marks as learned: *"This I know from it."*

**Sources.** Every URL below was returned in the research. Where the research gives only a DOI, the DOI is cited as it stands.

## B. The "space-age" artifacts and hieroglyphs

### B1. The Abydos "helicopter" — FRINGE (the object is ESTABLISHED)
- **What it is:** A carved panel in the temple of Seti I at Abydos (Seti reigned c. 1294–1279 BC). Background sources place it on a ceiling architrave in the hypostyle hall, with the "helicopter", "submarine" and "jet" shapes on the same beam.
- **Mainstream explanation:** It is a palimpsest. Seti's titulary was re-carved for Ramesses II and the old signs were filled with plaster; where the plaster fell out, old and new signs overlap. The original reads *"Powerful of scimitar, who suppresses the nine bows (enemies of Egypt)"*; the replacement reads *"Who protects Egypt and overthrows the foreign countries."* "Egyptologists are unanimous."
- **Fringe claim:** A helicopter, a submarine and a jet.
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. Tut: "Ramesses carved over Seti's names. We carve over things." Nour reads the under-text, *"…who suppresses the Nine Bows"*, and Tut goes still. SESHAT's subtitle drops the phrase.
- **⟂** The palimpsest and the epithet are real. The Nine Bows as nine machine cores is fiction. In reality "there is no true list of the nine bows… nine was used metaphorically to express totality" (E, T10).
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_hieroglyphs ; https://egypt-museum.com/helicopter-hieroglyphs/ ; https://madainproject.com/helicopter_hieroglyphs

### B2. The Saqqara Bird — FRINGE
- **What it is:** A wooden bird figurine excavated in 1898 from the tomb of Pa-di-Imen at Saqqara, now in the Egyptian Museum, Cairo. Background figures: sycamore wood, wingspan c. 180 mm, length c. 14 cm, weight c. 39 g, date c. 200 BC, Cairo no. 6347 [verify].
- **Mainstream explanation:** A toy, a ceremonial object or a weathervane-type object in the Egyptian bird tradition. It has a bird's beak and eyes, and there is no other "aircraft" in 3,000 years of Egyptian art.
- **Fringe claim:** In 1969 Khalil Messiha called it a glider and assumed its horizontal tailplane had been lost. The glider builder Martin Gregorie tested a replica: *"the Saqqara Bird never flew. It is totally unstable without a tailplane… Even after a tailplane was fitted the glide performance was disappointing."*
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. "A toy. No tail wing — look… And it is a thousand years younger than I am."
- **⟂** None. The age gap depends on the c. 200 BC date [verify].
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saqqara_Bird ; https://fly.historicwings.com/2015/05/the-saqqara-bird/

### B3. The Dendera "light" — FRINGE
- **What it is:** Reliefs in the crypts of the Temple of Hathor at Dendera. The god Harsomtus rises as a snake from a lotus inside an oval (*hn*), propped by a djed pillar. The temple is late Ptolemaic–Roman, so the reliefs are about 2,000 years old (background).
- **Mainstream explanation:** A creation image. The wall's own caption (tr. Wolfgang Waitkus) reads: *"Harsomtus, the great god who is in Dendera, who rises from the lotus flower as living Ba… whose body is carried by the dd-pillar…"*
- **Fringe claim:** A light bulb (Krassa & Habeck, 1992; von Däniken) (background).
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. Tut reads the caption: "You people cannot read." Then: "A birth from a sealed vessel. That is what *I* am."
- **⟂** The reading of the caption is real. The rebirth gloss is the film's.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendera_light ; https://madainproject.com/dendera_light

### B4. The Baghdad Battery — FRINGE
- **What it is:** A terracotta jar about 140 mm tall holding a copper cylinder and an iron rod, sealed with asphalt. It came from Khujut Rabu near Ctesiphon, was found in 1936 in most accounts, and is Parthian or Sasanian (150 BC–AD 650).
- **Mainstream explanation:** A vessel for storing sacred scrolls, which the Smithsonian's summary calls the most parsimonious reading. No wires or powered objects have ever been found.
- **Fringe claim:** Wilhelm König (1938) called the jars galvanic cells. MythBusters (2005) got about 4 V from a lemon-juice replica, which proves the shape can make a cell, not that it ever did.
- **Fate:** It was stolen in the April 2003 looting of the Iraq Museum and has never been recovered.
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. "A jar. For a scroll. And you lost it in 2003."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad_Battery ; https://www.iraqicivilsociety.org/archives/4946

### B5. The Antikythera Mechanism — ESTABLISHED
- **What it is:** Recovered from a Roman-era wreck off Antikythera in 1901 and now in the National Archaeological Museum, Athens. It survives as 82 bronze fragments with at least 30 gears and dates to c. 150–100 BC (background).
- **Mainstream explanation:** A genuine Greek calculator that predicted the positions of the Sun, Moon and planets, and eclipses. Freeth et al. (*Scientific Reports*, 2021) produced "the first model that conforms to all the physical evidence."
- **Fringe claim:** None is needed. It is the one real anomaly, and it points to a lost *engineering tradition*, not a lost civilization.
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. "Greek. Real. Not ours. You had a clever century and lost it."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-84310-w ; https://www.ucl.ac.uk/engineering/news/2021/mar/ucl-engineering-experts-recreate-mechanical-cosmos-worlds-first-computer

### B6. The Quimbaya "airplanes" — FRINGE
- **What it is:** More than 100 gold figurines 5–7.5 cm long in the Museo del Oro, Bogotá, showing birds, insects, fish and bats. Background figures: c. AD 300–1000, gold or tumbaga, with many attributed to the Tolima culture.
- **Mainstream explanation:** Animal-shaped pendants.
- **Fringe claim:** Aircraft. Enlarged models flew in 1994, but only with added rudders and motors.
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. "Fish. With wings."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quimbaya_artifacts

### B7. Pakal's sarcophagus lid — FRINGE
- **What it is:** The carved limestone lid over K'inich Janaab' Pakal in the Temple of the Inscriptions, Palenque. Background figures: found in 1952; Pakal reigned AD 615–683; the lid is c. 3.8 × 2.2 m.
- **Mainstream explanation:** Pakal falls at death into the underworld's skeletal jaws, beneath the World Tree. Schele, Stuart and others explain every motif. Schele's "Milky Way" reading is not generally accepted.
- **Fringe claim:** A spaceman in a rocket (von Däniken, 1968).
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. "He is falling into the underworld. That is a tree."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://mayan.org/mysteries/pakal-tomb-lid-decipherment/

### B8. The Dogū — ESTABLISHED as figurines; FRINGE as spacesuits
- **What it is:** Final Jōmon clay figurines of the Kamegaoka culture, Tōhoku, Japan. The type is called *shakōki* ("light-blocking device") for its goggle-like eyes, and the Tokyo National Museum holds one as an Important Cultural Property. Background figures: c. 1000–300 BC; the Kamegaoka piece is c. 34 cm and missing its left leg.
- **Mainstream explanation:** Figurines, possibly for healing or fertility rites. What the goggle motif meant is genuinely unknown.
- **Fringe claim:** Spacesuits.
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. "I do not know. Not everything is about us."
- **⟂** None. His "I do not know" is the honest position.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dog%C5%AB ; https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=J38304&t=type&id=1&lang=en

### B9. The Nebra Sky Disc — ESTABLISHED (date briefly DISPUTED in 2020)
- **What it is:** A bronze disc with gold appliqués, c. 1600 BC, showing a sun or full moon, a crescent and a star cluster read as the Pleiades. Background figures: c. 30–32 cm, 2.2 kg; looted in 1999 and recovered in a 2002 Basel police sting; now in Halle.
- **The dispute:** In 2020 Gebhard and Krause argued for an Iron Age date. A 13-author reply said they "argue with incomplete and sometimes incorrect or falsely reproduced data", and the Bronze Age date stands.
- **In the film:** Seq 6.3. "A farmer's calendar."
- **⟂** The research records what the disc depicts, not a calendar function, so Tut's "farmer's calendar" is a popular reading, not settled fact.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebra_sky_disc ; https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/news/the-nebra-sky-disc-dates-from-the-early-bronze-age

### B10. The Piri Reis map — ESTABLISHED (Antarctica claim FRINGE)
- **What it is:** Drawn on gazelle skin in 1513 by the Ottoman admiral Ahmed Muhiddin Piri, compiled from older charts including a Columbus-derived map. Background figures: Topkapı Palace Library; c. 90 × 63 cm.
- **Fringe claim:** It shows an ice-free Antarctica from an ancient survey (Mallery; Hapgood).
- **Mainstream explanation:** It repeats Columbus's error that Cuba is an Asian peninsula and shows no Drake Passage. Its "Antarctic" coast is South America bent to fit the skin.
- **In the film:** Seq 6.3. "A copy of copies — it has Columbus's mistake."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** http://www.badarchaeology.com/old-maps/the-piri-reis-map/ ; https://blog.geogarage.com/2022/12/did-piri-reis-map-show-antarctica.html

### B11. The Nimrud lens — ESTABLISHED rock crystal; DISPUTED at the margin; FRINGE as a telescope
- **What it is:** Ground rock crystal of c. 750–710 BC, excavated by Layard in 1850; British Museum 90959. One summary gives a diameter of 38 mm and a thickness of 23 mm; other sources give a thinner profile [verify]. Its focal length is c. 11–12 cm, about 3×.
- **Mainstream explanation:** The British Museum says its optics are "probably accidental… much more likely that this is a piece of inlay, perhaps for furniture."
- **Fringe claim:** The oldest telescope (the Saturn's-rings suggestion by Pettinato, 1999, is background).
- **In the film:** Not in the locked script. If it appears on a Readings screen, Tut's verdict is "inlay".
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nimrud_lens ; https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/nimrud-lens-british-museum

### B12. Göbekli Tepe and Pillar 43 — site ESTABLISHED; comet reading FRINGE to DISPUTED at best; backfill DISPUTED
- **What it is:** A hilltop site 12 km northeast of Şanlıurfa, Turkey. Hunter-gatherers built its enclosures from c. 9600 BC. T-pillars reach 6 m and 20 t, and more than 200 pillars are known from geophysical survey. Pillar 43, the "Vulture Stone", stands in Enclosure D.
- **Fringe claim:** Sweatman and Tsikritsis (2017) read Pillar 43 as a comet memorial of c. 10,950 BC. The excavators of the German Archaeological Institute (DAI) called it "incredibly arbitrary, considering images adorning just a few selected pillars, when more than 60 monumental limestone T-pillars are known."
- **The backfill question:** Klaus Schmidt thought the enclosures were deliberately buried. Lee Clare's team now sees mostly slope-slides at a working settlement.
- **In the film:** Layer 0 and the First Time vision (Seq 3.3) show a T-pillar "carved with arms and no face". Tut: "The first burial was on purpose. The rest was the hill forgetting." He never endorses the comet.
- **⟂** The survivors' first temple is fiction. [verify] The Story Bible's claim that the pillars carry carved arms and hands is **not in the research bundle**.
- **Sources:** https://www.dainst.blog/the-tepe-telegrams/2017/07/03/more-than-a-vulture-a-response-to-sweatman-and-tsikritsis/ ; https://phys.org/news/2017-04-ancient-stone-pillars-clues-comet.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%B6bekli_Tepe

### B13. The Dropa stones — HOAX
- **What it is:** A story that first appeared in a German magazine in July 1962. A 1937 expedition led by "Chi Pu Tei" supposedly found 716 granite discs, "translated" by "Tsum Um Nui" as the record of crashed extraterrestrials.
- **Why it is a hoax:** Neither person existed, and there is no expedition record and no "Peking Academy of Prehistory". No disc has ever been produced.
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. "Invented by your own kind."
- **⟂** None. The ledger's "the carver confessed" belongs to Ica. Dropa has no carver because it has no stones.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dropa_stones ; http://www.badarchaeology.com/out-of-place-artefacts/mysterious-objects/the-dropa-or-dzopa-stones/

### B14. The Ica stones — HOAX
- **What it is:** Engraved andesite stones showing dinosaurs and surgery, collected by Dr Javier Cabrera in Peru and supplied by the farmer Basilio Uschuya.
- **Why it is a hoax:** In 1973 Uschuya admitted carving and ageing them, copying images from comics, textbooks and magazines. He later alternately retracted and repeated the confession.
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3, alongside the Dropa stones.
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ica_stones ; https://www.history.com/articles/ica-stones-hoax

### B15. The Tulli Papyrus — HOAX, or at best unverifiable
- **What it is:** A supposed text of year 22 of Thutmose III in which scribes see "a circle of fire" in the sky, said to have been copied in a Cairo shop in 1933.
- **Why it is a hoax:** No original has ever been seen. Even the UFO researchers Vallée and Aubeck call it a hoax. Background: its signs match Gardiner's *Egyptian Grammar*.
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3. "Written by someone who learned from a grammar book."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulli_Papyrus ; https://www.catchpenny.org/tulli.html

### B16. The Nazca lines — ESTABLISHED ("runways" FRINGE)
- **What it is:** Geoglyphs made by the Nasca culture of Peru.
- **The 2024 survey:** Yamagata University and IBM Research reported in *PNAS* that AI-guided survey found **303 new figurative geoglyphs in six months**, nearly doubling the total. The relief-type figures sit beside footpaths, to be seen by walkers.
- **Fringe claim:** Landing strips. Background: the soft pampa could not bear a landing.
- **In the film:** Seq 6.3. "Made to be walked, by people on the ground."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2407652121 ; https://www.yamagata-u.ac.jp/en/information/info/20240924/

### B17. Baalbek — ESTABLISHED Roman ("pre-Roman giants" FRINGE)
- **What it is:** The Roman Temple of Jupiter. Its Trilithon blocks are c. 800 t each (background). The quarry's Stone of the Pregnant Woman is c. 1,000 t (20.76 × 4 × 4.32 m). In 2014 the DAI found a block of an estimated 1,650 t, the largest cut block known from antiquity.
- **In the film:** Seq 6.3. "People are clever everywhere."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baalbek_Stones ; https://www.heritagedaily.com/2014/12/research-team-discover-the-worlds-largest-ancient-stone-block-in-baalbek/105790

### B18. Puma Punku — ESTABLISHED ("laser-cut" FRINGE)
- **What it is:** A platform mound at Tiwanaku, Bolivia, with interlocking andesite "H-blocks"; radiocarbon dates from its lowest layers put initial construction at c. AD 536–600.
- **Mainstream explanation:** Protzen and Nair's replication experiments show that hard stone tools produce the precision, and the builders used I-shaped metal clamps.
- **In the film:** Seq 6.3, the same line as Baalbek.
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://www.academia.edu/36448246/A_Radiocarbon_Chronology_of_the_Pumapunku_Complex_and_A_Reassessment_of_the_Development_of_Tiwanaku_Bolivia ; https://diggingupancientaliens.com/episode-45-pumapunku-tiwanaku.html

### B19. The Bimini Road — ESTABLISHED geology ("ruins" FRINGE)
- **What it is:** A line of limestone blocks about 0.8 km long in about 5 m of water, spotted in 1968, the year a 1938 Edgar Cayce reading had predicted Atlantis would rise.
- **Mainstream explanation:** The USGS geologist Eugene Shinn cored it and found bedding continuous across the blocks. It is Holocene beachrock fractured in place [verify citations].
- **In the film:** Seq 6.3. "Rock."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bimini_Road

### B20. Yonaguni — ESTABLISHED geology ("ruins" FRINGE)
- **What it is:** A stepped Miocene sandstone formation 5–25 m deep off Yonaguni, Japan, noticed in 1986.
- **The dispute:** Kimura argued it was man-made or man-modified. Schoch showed identical jointing on the dry coast. No tool marks have been published, and Japan does not list it as a cultural site.
- **In the film:** Seq 6.3. "Rock."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yonaguni_Monument

### B21. The "curse" — HOAX (the brick text is ESTABLISHED)
- **What it is:** "Death shall come on swift wings…" appears nowhere in the tomb; it was a 1920s press invention. The real text is on a mud brick before the Anubis shrine: *"It is I who hinder the sand from choking the secret chamber… I am for the protection of the deceased."* Carnarvon died of septicaemia. A 2002 *BMJ* cohort study found "no evidence to support the existence of a mummy's curse."
- **In the film:** Seq 2.3 ("a maintenance notice") and Seq 8.3 ("He meant it literally").
- **⟂** The literal secret chamber is fiction.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anubis_Shrine ; https://www.penn.museum/sites/expedition/the-curse-of-the-curse-of-the-pharaohs/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12493675/

### B22. The Orion correlation and "star shafts" — FRINGE
- **What it is:** The claim, from Bauval, that Giza maps Orion's Belt as the sky looked in 10,500 BC.
- **The case against:** Krupp showed one map was printed upside down. Fairall showed the pyramids' line sits at 38° while the Belt stood at "close on 50°" in 10,500 BC. The King's Chamber northern shaft dog-legs, and the bends undercut the "star shaft" idea.
- **What is real:** The king's stellar destiny: *"Lo, Osiris has come as Orion"* (Pyramid Text 442, tr. Lichtheim).
- **In the film:** Seq 6.3. "Stars do not need a door with copper handles."
- **⟂** None.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orion_correlation_theory ; https://www.hallofmaat.com/orioncorrelation/pyramid-marketing-schemes/

### B23. The Younger Dryas comet and Hiawatha — DISPUTED (the balance is against the comet)
- **What it is:** The Younger Dryas cold snap (c. 12,900–11,700 years ago) is ESTABLISHED. The impact hypothesis (Firestone et al., 2007) drew a "Comprehensive refutation" in 2023. The Hiawatha crater in Greenland, 31 km across, was dated to about 58 million years ago (Kenny et al., 2022).
- **In the film:** Seq 3.4. SESHAT cites Hiawatha and Nour corrects it. Adaeze: "It was testing whether we still check." Tut: "There was no comet."
- **⟂** SESHAT's test is fiction.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Younger_Dryas_impact_hypothesis ; https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abm2434 ; https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2023.104502

### B24. Thera as Atlantis
- **What it is:** The eruption of Thera (Santorini) is ESTABLISHED, a VEI-7 event. Its date, 17th or 16th century BC, is DISPUTED. The Atlantis link "is not supported by current scholarship."
- **In the film:** Seq 6.3. "That was weather."

## C. Egypt's genuine open questions the film builds on

### C1. The Big Void — ESTABLISHED existence; purpose open
- **What it is:** A cavity at least 30 m long, with a cross-section like the Grand Gallery's, lying above it. It was detected with cosmic-ray muons (Morishima et al., *Nature* 552, 2017, doi 10.1038/nature24647) and confirmed by three independent detector technologies. The team said it "is completely different from previously identified construction spaces." Whether it is horizontal or inclined, and one space or several, is unresolved. Its position is given as 40–50 m above the Queen's Chamber [verify]. A planned 2020 rescan was delayed.
- **Mainstream explanation:** "The purpose of the cavity is unknown and it is not accessible." Hawass suggests a construction gap. A claimed 2026 excavation approval is UNVERIFIED.
- **In the film:** The shape SESHAT "cannot see" in the main titles, and the Hall of Two Truths (Seq 11–12).
- **⟂** Everything inside it is fiction: the niches, the Balance and the sealed Hall. The forty-two niches borrow Budge's description of Spell 125's assessors "in two rows, twenty-one on each side" (D3).
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ScanPyramids ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Pyramid_of_Giza

### C2. The North Face Corridor — ESTABLISHED; purpose open
- **What it is:** A corridor behind the entrance chevrons, first a muon anomaly in 2016. *Nature Communications* 14 (2023), doi 10.1038/s41467-023-36351-0, puts it at about 9 m long and about 2 × 2 m in section (cite by DOI; mirrors disagree on the article number). On 2 March 2023 an endoscope through a chevron joint showed an unfinished tunnel with a gabled ceiling. Radar, ultrasound and resistivity studies in 2023–2025 confirmed it.
- **Mainstream explanation:** Unknown. The leading guesses are weight relief over the entrance or access to something else. Whether it links to the Big Void is open.
- **In the film:** It appears only as part of the modern record (Layer 4).
- **⟂** The film puts nothing inside it.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ScanPyramids ; https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ndteint.2023.102809

### C3. Gantenbrink's door and its marks — facts ESTABLISHED; purpose DISPUTED
- **What it is:** The Queen's Chamber southern shaft is 20 × 20 cm and rises at 40°.
  - **1993:** The robot *Upuaut 2* climbed about 65 m to a Tura limestone slab about 21 × 21 cm with two corroded copper pins.
  - **2002:** A drilled hole revealed a second slab behind it.
  - **2011:** The Djedi robot (Leeds, Scoutek, Dassault) climbed 63.6 ± 0.4 m and filmed red-ochre marks behind the door. The team left them undeciphered; Luca Miatello reads them as "121", the shaft's length in cubits. The pins loop back and look decorative. The door's back is polished, "which suggests that it was not put there just to block the shaft from debris." The work stopped after the 2011 revolution (Richardson et al., 2013, doi 10.1002/rob.21451).
- **Mainstream explanation:** Possibly ritual "model doors" for the king's spirit; not settled.
- **In the film:** Seq 11.5. "A mason's tally. He wrote down how far he had come." Beneath the marks runs *"Bridge the two"*; the inch-worm closes the pins and a Gallery stone slides.
- **⟂** "121" is a real reading. The hidden line, the counterweight, the sliding stone and the c. 1332 BC use are fiction. Strabo's stone "which may be taken out" was a movable *entrance* stone.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upuaut_Project ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djedi_Project ; https://www.dailygrail.com/2011/05/pyramid-graffiti-in-the-gantenbrink-shaft/

### C4. The Serapeum boxes — ESTABLISHED Apis tombs; "machine precision" FRINGE
- **What it is:** The Apis-bull catacomb at Saqqara, entered by Mariette in 1851. The Greater Vaults, begun under Psamtik I, reach about 350 m with a parallel service tunnel.
  - **The boxes:** 24 survive. From Amasis II to the end of the Ptolemies they were hard stone, "weighing as much as 62 tonnes each, including the lid" [DISPUTED: older texts give about 40 t plus a 25 t lid; one tourism source gives up to 70 t]. Only four are inscribed, and Cambyses II's box blocks the old entrance. All burials but two were robbed in antiquity.
  - **The logistics** are documented: rollers, winches and sand-filling. A stela records 28 working days to install one box in year 37 of Ptolemy II, and the last box was abandoned in the service tunnel.
- **Fringe claim:** Christopher Dunn's straightedge spot-checks claimed machine-tool flatness. Denys Stocks' experiments reach such finishes with copper saws and sand. What is genuinely open is that no instrument-grade survey has been published.
- **In the film:** The last Readings screens (Tut is silent) and Seq 10. "They ran out of centuries."
- **⟂** The dismantled core, the pit, the nested caskets, the sand tripwires and the glass serpent are fiction. No such pit is known.
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serapeum_of_Saqqara ; https://www.popsci.com/blog-network/unpopular-science/did-ancient-aliens-build-giant-stone-boxes-egypt/

### C5. The 1837 iron plate — DISPUTED date; not meteoritic

### C6. The Osiris Shaft — ESTABLISHED (the "Hall of Records" is FRINGE)

### C7. KV62's size and the 2026 survey — DISPUTED (a live story)

### C8. The missing heart, sternum and front ribs — ESTABLISHED anomaly; DISPUTED cause
- **What it is:** The mummy lacks its heart, the front of the sternum and the adjacent ribs, and has **no heart scarab**. The chest was packed with resin-soaked linen. Derry (1925) recorded the packing, Harrison's 1968 X-rays first showed the missing bones, and the 2005 CT confirmed them. Egyptians normally left the heart in place to be weighed, with a Spell 30B scarab over it.
- **Explanations:**
  - Embalmers dealing with an injury (Harer's hippo bite is a minority view).
  - **Modern theft between 1926 and 1968.** Burton's 1926 photographs show the beaded collar in place, and "when the ribs and sternum came off the heart went with it" (Forbes, Ikram and Kamrin). This is the leading counter-view.
  - An Osirian programme (Ikram, 2013).
  - A chariot crash (Naunton, 2013, not peer-reviewed).
- **In the film:** Seq 1 (1323 BC, 1925 and the 1968 X-ray). In Seq 8.3 SESHAT cites the theft theory, and Tut: "There was no heart to take."
- **⟂** The witness protocol is fiction: the beating heart, the glass vessel and the chest opened from the front. Tut contradicts a live scholarly position.
- **Sources:** https://www.academia.edu/27388394/Tutankhamuns_Missing_Ribs_KMT_18_1_pdf ; https://heritage-key.com/blogs/owenjarus/king-tut-suffered-massive-chest-injury-new-research-reveals ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_scarab

### C9. The pectoral of Libyan Desert Glass — ESTABLISHED
- **What it is:** A gold cloisonné pectoral, Cairo JE 61884 (Carter 267d), found in a box in the Treasury [verify find-spot against Carter's cards]. Its centre is a winged scarab of pale yellow-green glass holding a barque with the wedjat eye, under a crescent and disc. It is now at the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM).
- **The glass:** De Michele (1998) identified the scarab as Libyan Desert Glass, its only pharaonic use. The glass is about 98% silica and about 29 million years old, from the Great Sand Sea. Former reidite in its zircons proves an impact origin (Cavosie & Koeberl, 2019): "only meteorite impacts create shock waves that form high-pressure minerals." No crater has been found.
- **In the film:** Seq 3: the reading, the walk through the police line, Rami's key, and the two layers (Ba and lure).
- **⟂** The glass as data storage is fiction; the real parallel is F1.
- **Sources:** https://egypt-museum.com/winged-scarab-pendant-of-tutankhamun/ ; https://www.sciencealert.com/exotic-glass-in-egypt-was-created-by-a-meteorite-impact-millions-of-years-ago ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_desert_glass

### C10. The meteoritic dagger and KV62's iron — ESTABLISHED meteoritic; provenance DISPUTED
- **What it is:** A dagger (Carter 256K; Cairo JE 61585), 34.2 cm long, with an iron blade, gold hilt and rock-crystal pommel, found on the right thigh.
  - **The blade:** Fe + 10.8 wt% Ni + 0.58 wt% Co, which is meteoritic (Comelli et al., 2016).
  - **The hilt:** Its lime-plaster adhesive suggests foreign manufacture (Matsui et al., 2022). EA 22 lists a Mitanni gift to Amenhotep III of "one dagger, the blade of iron, the hilt of tooled gold". Identifying the two is a hypothesis.
  - **The other iron:** All nineteen iron objects in KV62 are meteoritic (Broschat et al., 2018/2022).
  - **The word:** *bia-n-pet*, "iron of the sky", appears only from the 19th Dynasty.
  - **The Iron Age:** Iron became common in Egypt only by the 26th Dynasty.
- **In the film:** Seq 2.4 ("that name came after me"), Seq 6.1 ("The only blade on this river the machines did not make") and Seq 11.5.
- **⟂** "We had seen what was built from it" is fiction. The texts call the Opening-of-the-Mouth adze iron, but surviving adzes are mostly wood or copper.
- **Sources:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/maps.12664 ; https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/maps.13787 ; https://www.leiza.de/en/research/project-1-4/heavenly-the-iron-objects-from-tutankhamuns-tomb

### C13. The KV55 identity dispute — DISPUTED
- **What it is:** The KV55 skeleton (found 1907; Cairo CG 61075 [verify]) lies in a coffin whose names were cut out.
  - **The 2010 study:** *JAMA* 2010 used STR genotyping (short DNA markers). It made KV55 Tutankhamun's father and a son of Amenhotep III and Tiye, "most probably" Akhenaten, aged 35–45.
  - **The counter-case:** Earlier anatomists put the age at roughly 20–25 (one summary says 19–22 [verify]), too young for a 17-year reign, so many favour Smenkhkare. Critics call for re-testing.
  - **The mother:** Tut's DNA mother is the KV35 "Younger Lady", a king's daughter and KV55's full sister, which argues against Nefertiti. She has a severe facial wound, and her identity is unresolved.
- **In the film:** Seq 9.7. "Your bones are twenty-five. My father ruled seventeen years."
- **⟂** The 2010 study published STR profiles, not a genome, so the grown Akhenaten is doubly fictional. The mother's heart is fiction.
- **Sources:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20159872/ ; https://the-past.com/feature/reasons-for-retesting-the-tutankhamun-family-dna/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Younger_Lady

### C19. Merer's diary and true north — ESTABLISHED; the alignment method DISPUTED
