## 0. HOW TO USE THIS FILE

1. **LONG and SHORT forms are fixed wording.** Paste them verbatim.
   - Use the LONG form when the prop is the subject of the shot (inserts, hand shots, reference stills).
   - Use the SHORT form when the prop is carried, worn or in the background.
2. **State add-ons** (dust, damage, glow) are appended verbatim after the lock, separated by a comma.
3. **Heritage objects** (pectoral, dagger, trumpets, the wreath) are AI approximations or licensed images (bible §14.7). Prompts describe them and never name the museum number, the king or the finder. The number goes in `Refs:` and notes only.
4. **Readable text is always COMP** (bible §14.5; spec 03b): the pendant's signs, the index cards, the notebook, the block's hidden face, the papyrus band, the tablet image. In the plate, text reads as "tiny illegible signs" or "dense handwriting". Supply the legible version in the shot's `Comp:` line.
5. **Remains and organs rule (bible §3.4):**
   - The heart is only ever "a dark linen bundle" and then "a dark shape behind thick yellow-green glass".
   - Vocabulary: "linen bundle", "sealed vessel", "chest port".
   - Never "organ", "blood", "open chest" or "severed".
6. **Weapons (dagger):** never pointed at the lens. They are used only on machines, threads and objects.
7. **Continuity** follows the bible §12 state board. Each entry lists where the prop is by sequence.

## 1. PROP_PECTORAL — the Libyan Desert Glass scarab pectoral (Cairo JE 61884)

**Real (01 §13):**
- A cloisonné pectoral found by Carter in a box in the **Treasury** of KV62, not on the body. Now in the GEM Tutankhamun galleries.
- Centre: a **winged scarab of pale yellow-green glass** gripping a **lotus and a papyrus**, flanked by **uraei** (cobras).
- A slim **solar/lunar boat** rests on the scarab's forelegs. It carries a **wedjat eye** topped by a **gold crescent and silver disc** bearing images of the gods.
- **Pendants of lotus, papyrus and poppy heads** hang below.
- The scarab is **Libyan Desert Glass**, identified by Vincenzo de Michele (1998) as the only known pharaonic use of the material. LDG is about 98% silica and about 29 million years old, from the Great Sand Sea. Impact origin was confirmed by reidite (Cavosie & Koeberl 2019), and no crater has been found.
- **Dimensions:** about 15 cm across. The commonly quoted figure is about 14.9 × 14.5 cm [verify].
- **Inlays:** gold cloisonné with coloured stones and glass. The exact list (lapis lazuli, carnelian, turquoise, calcite, glass) is [verify].

⟂ **Fiction:**
- The scarab holds two layers: Layer 1 is Tut's Ba, and Layer 2 is the lure.
- The carved details on the scarab's back are the "incised key", matching the pictorial key of Southampton's 5D crystal [05 C1].
- *Controlling image:* "a green glass scarab held up to a light", passed from hand to hand across the eras.

**LONG:** an ancient gold pectoral jewel about fifteen centimetres across: at its centre a translucent pale yellow-green glass scarab with outspread inlaid wings of blue, red and gold, flanked by two gold cobras and gripping lotus and papyrus blossoms; above it a small boat carries a sacred eye crowned by a gold crescent and a silver disc; a fringe of tiny flower pendants hangs below

**SHORT:** an ancient gold pectoral with a translucent pale yellow-green glass winged scarab at its centre, flanked by gold cobras, with a fringe of flower pendants

## 2. PROP_DAGGER — the meteoritic iron dagger (Cairo JE 61585; Carter 256k)

**Real (01 §12; 15 §1.2; 02 §8):**
- **Total length 34.2 cm.** An **iron blade**, a **gold hilt with granulation and cloisonné**, a **rock-crystal pommel**, and a **gold sheath with a feather/lily pattern**.
- It was found in 1925 inside the wrappings on the **right thigh**.
- The blade is Fe + **10.8 wt% Ni** + 0.58 wt% Co, which is meteoritic (Comelli et al. 2016).
- A distorted **Widmanstätten pattern** and troilite inclusions show low-temperature forging. The hilt adhesive is lime plaster, so the hilt was probably made abroad (Matsui et al. 2022).
- It is possibly the Mitanni gift listed in **EA 22**: "one dagger, the blade of iron, the hilt of tooled gold… the pommel of ḫiliba-stone".
- **Verify:** the sheath's terminal (often described as a jackal's head); the blade's current appearance (commonly described as bright and nearly rust-free); the blade length on its own.

⟂ **Fiction:** "The only blade on this river the machines did not make." Iron from the sky, reserved for waking rites.

**LONG:** an ancient royal dagger about thirty-four centimetres long: a slim, pale silver-grey iron blade with a faint crystalline sheen and a few dark spots, a gold hilt banded with fine granulation and coloured cloisonné inlay, capped by a round knob of clear rock crystal; it slides into a gold sheath chased with a feather pattern

## 10. PROP_MERKHET_BAY — the improvised palm-rib *bay* and *merkhet* (RESERVE: not in the current screenplay)

**Real (07 §F; 11):**
- The **bay** is a palm rib with a V-slit at one end, used as a sight. The **merkhet** ("instrument of knowing") is a bar with a nub from which a plumb line hangs.
