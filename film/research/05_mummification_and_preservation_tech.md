## PART A — THE ANCIENT PROCEDURE

### A1. Herodotus, Histories 2.85–90 (c. 440 BC): the only detailed eyewitness-era account
- Herodotus describes three price tiers, shown to the family as painted wooden models. He refuses to name the god whose form the best method imitates ("him whose name I think it impiety to mention") — i.e., Osiris. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Macaulay translation, Project Gutenberg #2707, mirrored at https://github.com/MonadnockValleyPress/monadnock/blob/master/herodotus/histories-2.html]
- Tier 1: brain drawn out through the nostrils "with a crooked iron tool", the rest dissolved with drugs; flank cut "with a sharp stone of Ethiopia"; cavity cleaned with palm wine and pounded spices; filled with myrrh, cassia and other spices "except frankincense"; sewn; "covered up in natron for seventy days, but for a longer time than this it is not permitted"; washed; wrapped in fine linen bands smeared with gum; placed in a man-shaped wooden case, stood upright against the tomb wall. [ESTABLISHED as Herodotus' text; details partly corrected by archaeology, see A2] [V-MIRROR, same URL]
- Tier 2: cedar oil injected by syringe through the anus, no evisceration; natron for the appointed days; oil released "brings out with it the bowels and interior organs of the body dissolved"; natron dissolves the flesh, "so that there is left of the corpse only the skin and the bones." [V-MIRROR]
- Tier 3: belly "cleansed with a purge", natron seventy days, returned. [V-MIRROR]
- 2.89: wives of men of rank and very beautiful women were not handed over until the third or fourth day after death, "in order that the embalmers may not abuse their women." 2.90: anyone killed by a crocodile or the Nile itself had to be embalmed by the priests of the Nile and buried "as that of one who was something more than man." [V-MIRROR]
- Herodotus' "iron hook" is a simplification: examined skulls show the brain was macerated with a rod pushed through the ethmoid and drained by gravity, and the skull sometimes rinsed and packed with resin; the brain was discarded because "they thought that the heart did all the thinking." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia "Ancient Egyptian funerary practices" and "Mummy" dumps; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Egyptian_funerary_practices]
- Diodorus Siculus 1.91 (c. 30 BC) adds the guild roles: a "scribe" who marks the incision line on the left flank, the *paraschistes* ("slitter") who cuts with an Ethiopian stone and then runs away pelted with stones and curses, and the *taricheutai* who treat the body for over 30 days with cedar oil and spices; he gives prices of a talent of silver, twenty minae, and "a very small sum." [ESTABLISHED] [MEMORY: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Diodorus_Siculus/1D*.html]

### A2. The process as archaeology reconstructs it
- Organs removed through a (usually left) flank incision: lungs, liver, stomach, intestines, each dried and placed in a canopic jar (later, in packages returned to the body) under one of the Four Sons of Horus: Imsety, Hapy, Duamutef, Qebehsenuef. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia funerary-practices dump]
- The heart was left in place: "in the hall of judgment, it would be weighed against the feather of Maat." The kidneys were usually also left, being hard to reach. [ESTABLISHED; kidney detail MEMORY] [V-MIRROR for the heart]
- Dehydration in natron (a natural sodium carbonate/bicarbonate salt with NaCl and sodium sulphate, quarried at Wadi Natrun) "took 40 days"; the whole rite "is said to have taken up to seventy days" (40 drying + ~30 anointing, wrapping and rituals). [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR; Wadi Natrun composition MEMORY: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natron]
- Old Kingdom tomb inscriptions sometimes record longer intervals; Queen Meresankh III's tomb (G7530) records 272 days between death and burial. [ESTABLISHED] [MEMORY: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meresankh_III]
- After drying: body "sometimes colored with a golden resin, which protected the body from bacteria and insects", cavity packed (linen, sawdust, resin, natron bags), then "wrapped in linen cut into strips with amulets while a priest recited prayers." [V-MIRROR]
- Amulets and placement: Book of the Dead spells 155–160 prescribe amulets of specific materials (gold djed at the throat, tyet knot, golden vulture, golden collar, papyrus column of feldspar), and Spell 30B the heart scarab of green stone laid over the heart; a wedjat-eye plate covered the embalming incision. [ESTABLISHED; Tutankhamun's ~150 objects in 17 layers is in file 01] [MEMORY for spell numbers: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_of_the_Dead]
- "The opening of the mouth ceremony was conducted by a priest who would utter a spell and touch the mummy or sarcophagus with a ceremonial adze – a copper or stone blade. This ceremony ensured that the mummy could breathe and speak in the afterlife." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR]
- The only surviving embalmers' texts are late. "A text known as The Ritual of Embalming does describe some of the practical logistics of embalming; however, there are only two known copies and each is incomplete." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia "Mummy" dump] The two copies are Papyrus Boulaq 3 (Cairo) and Papyrus Louvre 5158, Roman Period, 1st century AD. [MEMORY] A third source, the Papyrus Louvre-Carlsberg (c. 1450 BC, published by Sofie Schiødt, University of Copenhagen, 2021), is a ~6 m medical papyrus that embeds the oldest known embalming manual, including a recipe for a red linen face-cloth coated with a plant-based mixture, and a rhythm of procedures every four days with processions. [ESTABLISHED] [MEMORY: https://news.ku.dk/all_news/2021/02/ancient-egyptian-manual-reveals-new-details-about-mummification/]

### A3. The 2018 Saqqara embalming workshop and the 2023 Nature chemistry paper
- Discovery announced 14 July 2018 by the Saqqara Saite Tombs Project (German–Egyptian; University of Tübingen), director Ramadan Badry Hussein: a rectangular mud-brick/limestone embalming workshop with an adjoining 30 m (100 ft) burial shaft whose bedrock chambers held mummies, wooden coffins and stone sarcophagi; Saite–Persian period, 664–404 BC. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2018/jul/14/a-goldmine-mummies-secrets-uncovered-in-egypt ; https://archaeology.org/news/2018/07/16/180716-saqqara-burial-shaft]
- Headline object: a gilded silver mummy mask, the first precious-metal mask found since 1939, on the mummy of the Second Priest of Mut, eyes inlaid with calcite, obsidian and a black stone (possibly onyx). Hussein: "The finding of this mask could be called a sensation. Very few masks of precious metal have been preserved to the present day, because the tombs of most ancient Egyptian dignitaries were looted in ancient times." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeology_of_ancient_Egypt ; https://www.livescience.com/63066-mummy-mask-sarcophagus-saqqara-egypt.html]
- Rageot, M. et al., "Biomolecular analyses enable new insights into ancient Egyptian embalming," *Nature* 614: 287–293 (1 Feb 2023). Residues in the workshop's labelled vessels: juniper, cypress and cedar products from the eastern Mediterranean; bitumen from the Dead Sea; local animal fats and beeswax; and "ingredients from distant places such as elemi and dammar from southeast Asia; while Pistacia resin and castor oil were used in particular for the treatment of the head." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia funerary-practices dump citing the paper; paper URL https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05663-4]
- Dammar comes from Dipterocarpaceae trees restricted to Southeast Asia; elemi from *Canarium* (tropical Asia/Africa) — the first chemical proof of a trade route from island Southeast Asia to Egypt by the 7th–6th century BC. A secondary compilation reports the dammar in vessel 5 (inscribed *antiu*) and elemi in vessel 62. [ESTABLISHED for the identification; vessel numbers reported second-hand] [V-MIRROR: https://github.com/neimasilk/volcarch-repo summarising Rageot 2023]
- The vessels carry short instructions ("to put on his head", "bandage/embalm with it", "to make his odour pleasant"), and the study showed that *antiu* — translated for a century as "myrrh" or "incense" — was in this workshop a mixture of cedar oil, juniper/cypress oil and animal fat, and *sefet* a scented unguent of oils, fat and resin. [ESTABLISHED] [MEMORY for the exact label wordings and the antiu/sefet redefinitions; from the paper and the Max Planck/Tübingen releases, e.g. https://www.mpg.de/19797226/0201-evan-ancient-egyptian-embalming-150495-x]
- Hussein died in 2022, before the paper appeared. [MEMORY]

### A4. Why it began: sand, resin, and the 4th-millennium evidence
- "In Prehistoric Egypt, bodies were buried in deserts and they would naturally be preserved by dehydration. The 'graves' were small oval or rectangular pits dug in the sand." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR]
- Jones, Higham, Oldfield, O'Connor & Buckley, *PLOS ONE* 2014 (doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0103608): first chemical analysis of AMS-dated linen wrappings from Badarian and Predynastic pit graves at Mostagedda (c. 4500–3350 BC; Bolton Museum). They found deliberately applied "resin" (a pine resin, an aromatic plant extract, a plant gum/sugar, a natural petroleum source and a plant oil/animal fat) — the "earliest evidence of embalming agents", pushing artificial mummification back ~1,500 years. The paper notes the Old Kingdom (c. 2500 BC) "is generally regarded as the start of true Egyptian mummification", with natron and evisceration introduced then. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: journal XML at https://github.com/TimothyElder/plos-one ; original https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0103608 ; component list MEMORY]
- Hierakonpolis cemetery HK43 (Naqada IIA–C, c. 3600–3400 BC): Jana Jones observed resin-soaked thick linen wrappings "around parts of a number of bodies, most notably the back of the head, the jaw and the hands", first identified by appearance and later chemically. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR, same PLOS XML]
- Mummification "became an integral part of the rituals for the dead beginning as early as the 2nd dynasty (about 2800 BC)." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia "Mummy" dump]
- Buckley & Evershed 2001 *Nature* 413: 837–841 established the standard chemistry (fats, beeswax, conifer resins, Pistacia, bitumen) for Pharaonic and Graeco-Roman mummies. [ESTABLISHED] [MEMORY: https://www.nature.com/articles/35102625]

### A5. The beliefs: what the body was for
- "Only if the body is embalmed in a specific fashion will ka return to the deceased body, and rebirth will take place." "The Ba made it possible for an invisible twin to be released from the body to support the family, while the Ka would recognize the twin when it would come back to the body." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR]
- The person was a bundle of components: body (*khat*), *ka* (life force), *ba* (personality, shown as a human-headed bird), *akh* (the transfigured, effective spirit), *ren* (name), *shut* (shadow), and *ib* (heart, the seat of mind and memory). Destroying the name or the body destroyed the person; hence the name-erasures of the Amarna kings (file 02). [ESTABLISHED] [MEMORY: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Egyptian_conception_of_the_soul]
- Book of the Dead Spell 30B (heart-scarab text, after Faulkner): "O my heart which I had from my mother! O my heart which I had from my mother! O my heart of my different ages! Do not stand up as a witness against me, do not be opposed to me in the tribunal, do not be hostile to me in the presence of the Keeper of the Balance, for you are my ka which was in my body." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: quoted with attribution in three independent GitHub mirrors, incl. phys.org 2025 and a Budge-based translation; original text e.g. https://egypt-museum.com/the-egyptian-book-of-the-dead/]
- Spell 154 is the "Chapter of not letting the body perish": the deceased addresses Osiris, whose body "did not decay" and "did not become worms", and asks for the same. [ESTABLISHED as a spell; wording MEMORY — verify Budge/Faulkner before quoting]
- The mummy is Osiris: the myth of Set dismembering Osiris, Isis reassembling him and Anubis embalming him is the template for every mummy (file 00 uses this as Project OSIRIS). [ESTABLISHED] [MEMORY: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osiris_myth]

### A6. The decline
- "The widespread mummification treatments in the Ptolemaic and Roman Periods in particular, leading to a decline in standards and costs" — Schuenemann et al. 2017 on Abusir el-Meleq. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Nature Communications text]

### A7. How many mummies? (a number to be careful with)
- There is no established count of Egyptian human mummies. Wikipedia states only that "over one million animal mummies have been found in Egypt, many of which are cats." The widely repeated "70 million" belongs to *animal* mummies: "it is believed that as many as 70 million animal mummies were created in this way" (BBC 2020). Popular articles that apply "70 million" to humans have no traceable peer-reviewed source. [DISPUTED / UNVERIFIED for humans; ESTABLISHED for the animal estimate] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia "Mummy" dump; BBC article dump https://www.bbc.com/news]
- Practical film line: if the whole population of 3,000 years of Egypt (order of a few million alive at any time) were mummified at all price tiers, the total would be in the tens of millions; the cheaper tiers (Herodotus' 2 and 3) leave almost nothing recognisable today. ### A8. Mummies as medicine, paint, fuel and party
- Etymology: "The English word mummy is derived from medieval Latin Mumia, a borrowing of the medieval Arabic word mūmiya which meant an embalmed corpse, as well as the bituminous embalming substance." [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR]
- Medieval and early-modern practitioners would "grind Egyptian mummies into a powder to be sold and used as medicine" (*mummia*); "mummy brown" pigment was "originally obtained by grinding human and animal Egyptian mummies", most popular in the 17th century and abandoned in the early 19th. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR]
- "European aristocrats would occasionally entertain themselves by purchasing mummies, having them unwrapped, and holding observation sessions"; Thomas Pettigrew pioneered public unrollings in Britain (1830s; *A History of Egyptian Mummies*, 1834). [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR; 1834 date MEMORY]
- "The use of mummies as fuel for locomotives was documented by Mark Twain (likely as a joke or humor), but the truth of the story remains debatable." [DISPUTED/likely HOAX] [V-MIRROR]
- First X-ray of a mummy: 1903, Grafton Elliot Smith and Howard Carter, on Thutmose IV, using the only X-ray machine in Cairo; first modern scientific examinations 1901 at the Government School of Medicine, Cairo; chemist Alfred Lucas analysed embalming substances and worked on Tutankhamun in 1922. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia "Mummy" dump]

### A9. Comparison mummies (for scale and for the "witness" idea)
- **Chinchorro (Chile/Peru) — oldest artificial mummies.** UNESCO (inscribed 2021): marine hunter-gatherers "from approximately 5450 BCE to 890 BCE"; "the oldest known archaeological evidence of the artificial mummification of bodies"; "they systematically dismembered and reassembled bodies of deceased men, women and children of the entire social spectrum to create 'artificial' mummies." Types: natural, black, red, mud-coated and bandage mummies; black mummies were skinned, defleshed, reinforced with sticks and reeds, re-modelled in ash paste and clay and painted with manganese. Oldest ~7,000 years, i.e., ~2,000 years before Egypt's first artificial mummies. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1634/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinchorro_mummies]
- **Xin Zhui / Lady Dai (China) — best preserved.** Wife of the Marquis of Dai (Western Han); tomb closed shortly after 168 BC; found 1971–72 at Mawangdui, Changsha; deep shaft with three nested coffins; ~50 years old at death; skin still elastic and joints movable after 2,100 years; autopsied by the Hunan Provincial Medical Institute; "the exact reason why her body was so completely preserved has yet to be determined"; about 40 such "wet corpses" are known from imperial China; displayed at the Hunan Provincial Museum. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia "Mummy" and "Embalming" dumps; academic lecture abstract (R. Pfister)] Reported autopsy details — type-A blood in the vessels, coronary disease, gallstones, 138 melon seeds in the stomach, ~80 litres of mildly acidic fluid in the coffin, ~20 layers of silk — are [MEMORY: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xin_Zhui].
- **Ötzi (Alps) — natural ice mummy.** ~5,300 years old; found 19 Sept 1991 at the Tisenjoch (3,210 m); surveys showed the body lay 92.56 m inside Italy; displayed since 1998 at the South Tyrol Museum of Archaeology, Bolzano; arrowhead in the left shoulder (murder); 61 tattoos in 19 groups of soot-pigment lines, many over arthritic joints; whipworm; 2012 DNA showed lactose intolerance; lungs blackened by soot; epidermis lost to ice mummification. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia "Ötzi" scrape; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%96tzi] The 2023 genome (Wang et al., *Cell Genomics*) found dark skin, probable baldness and >90% Anatolian-farmer ancestry; the museum keeps him at −6 °C and ~98% humidity. [ESTABLISHED] [MEMORY: https://www.iceman.it/en/the-mummy/]
- **Tollund Man (Denmark) — bog body.** Found 8 May 1950 (some sources say 6 May) by Viggo and Emil Højgaard cutting peat in Bjældskovdal, 12 km west of Silkeborg; so fresh they called the police, who called P. V. Glob; hanged with a hide rope (furrows under the chin, distended tongue, undamaged vertebrae; confirmed 2002); last meal a porridge of barley, flax, false flax and knotgrass eaten 12–24 hours before death (2021 re-analysis added fish and parasite eggs); only the head is original — the body was allowed to dry out and was reconstructed in 1987; his 1976 thumbprint is among the oldest on record; >500 bog bodies are known from Denmark. Bog chemistry: sphagnan binds nitrogen and halts bacteria while leaching calcium from bone. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia "Tollund Man" dumps; Smithsonian 2017 feature https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/tollund-man-europes-bog-bodies-180962770/]
- Curator Ole Nielsen on Tollund Man (2017): "I would like to put a USB plug into his well-preserved brain and download everything that's on it, but that's impossible. He's reluctant to answer." [V-MIRROR, same Smithsonian feature]

### B1. Tutankhamun's DNA (2010) — see file 01 §4 and §8

### B2. Schuenemann et al. 2017 — the first genome-wide data from Egyptian mummies
- *Nature Communications* 8: 15694 (2017), "Ancient Egyptian mummy genomes suggest an increase of Sub-Saharan African ancestry in post-Roman periods." Sampled 151 mummies from Abusir el-Meleq (Middle Egypt); recovered 90 complete mitochondrial genomes and 3 genome-wide datasets spanning ~1,300 years (New Kingdom to Roman; the three nuclear genomes date 787 BC–AD 23). [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: journal text at https://github.com/.../NCOMMS_XML ; Wikipedia "Ancient Egypt" dump; original https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms15694]
- Findings: ancient individuals were closest to Near Eastern/Levantine populations; sub-Saharan ancestry ~6–15% in the mummies vs ~14–21% in modern Egyptians, an ~8% increase "within the last 2,000 years"; genetic continuity across Pre-Ptolemaic, Ptolemaic and Roman periods. Authors' caveat: "all our genetic data were obtained from a single site in Middle Egypt and may not be representative for all of ancient Egypt." [V-MIRROR]
- Site history relevant to the film: Abusir el-Meleq was inhabited from at least 3250 BC to c. AD 700 "because of its active cult to Osiris"; excavated by Otto Rubensohn in the early 1900s, who wanted papyri from cartonnage rather than bodies; "many of his excavation diaries and notes were destroyed during the Second World War." [V-MIRROR]
- Historical footnote: Svante Pääbo's 1985 *Nature* paper (314: 644) claimed cloned DNA from a 2,400-year-old Egyptian mummy; it is now regarded as probably contaminated, which is why Egyptian aDNA was doubted for 30 years. [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: phys.org 2025 referencing Pääbo; https://www.nature.com/articles/314644a0]

### B3. Nuwayrat 2025 — the first whole genome from Old Kingdom Egypt
- Morez Jacobs, Girdland-Flink, Skoglund, Irish et al., *Nature*, 2 July 2025 (doi 10.1038/s41586-025-09195-5): individual NUE001, radiocarbon-dated 2855–2570 cal BC (Early Dynastic/Old Kingdom, 3rd–4th Dynasty), from Nuwayrat, 265 km south of Cairo; excavated c. 1902 by John Garstang's committee; kept at Liverpool Institute of Archaeology, then World Museum Liverpool, where it "survived WWII Blitz bombing that destroyed most other human remains in the collection". [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: phys.org 2025-07 mirror; Wikipedia "Ancient Egypt" dump; https://phys.org/news/2025-07-ancient-egyptian-genome-reveals-north.html]
- He was not mummified: "placed intact in a large circular clay pot without embalming, and then installed inside a cliff tomb, which accounts for the comparatively good level of conservation of the skeleton and its DNA." DNA came from a tooth. ~80% North African Neolithic ancestry, ~20% eastern Fertile Crescent (Mesopotamia); 2× coverage. Skeleton: enlarged seat bones, heavily used arms, arthritis only in the right foot — possibly a potter, though the burial is high-status. [V-MIRROR]
- Quote (paper): "Contacts between Egypt and the eastern Fertile Crescent were not limited to objects and imagery but also encompassed human migration." First author: "Piecing together all the clues from this individual's DNA, bones and teeth have allowed us to build a comprehensive picture." [V-MIRROR]
- Key point for the film: the best Egyptian genome ever recovered came from a body that was *not* embalmed. Heat, resins and natron are hostile to DNA. Mummification preserved shape, not code.

### B4. Amenhotep I "digitally unwrapped" (2021)
- Saleem & Hawass, "Digital Unwrapping of the Mummy of King Amenhotep I (1525–1504 BC) Using CT," *Frontiers in Medicine*, 28 Dec 2021. CT performed 4 May 2019 on the only royal mummy never physically unwrapped in modern times (found 1881 in the Deir el-Bahari cache; rewrapped by 21st-Dynasty priests; face mask and garlands intact). [ESTABLISHED] [V-MIRROR: Wikipedia "Amenhotep I" dump; https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fmed.2021.778498/full]
- Findings: age at death ~35 (so accession at ~15); organs removed through a vertical incision and cavity stuffed with linen; "the heart is present in the chest cavity. No attempt was made to remove the brain"; nostrils plugged with resin-treated linen; no eye packing or subcutaneous padding; amulets and jewellery inside the wrappings, including "a beaded girdle, likely of gold" across the hips; ancient robbers had detached head, left arm, right hand and right foot and holed the torso; the priests reattached head and limbs and braced the foot with a board. [V-MIRROR] Height ~169 cm, good teeth, circumcised, ~30 amulets, arms crossed — [MEMORY].
- Film relevance: royal mummies do exist with the brain still inside the skull.

### B5. Imaging and proteins
- CT of Tutankhamun, 2005: ~1,700 slices in 15 minutes (file 01 §4).
- Palaeoproteomics: PRIDE dataset PXD015572 (announced 10 June 2020; submitter Beatrice Demarchi, University of Turin): "Non-destructive Palaeoproteomics of Mummified Human Skin" — EVA-membrane sampling "successful in extracting ancient proteins from the surface of Ancient Egyptian mummies", authenticating collagen and keratin from "the mummy of a young woman (Supp. 16747 of the Museum of Anthropology and Ethnography of the University of Turin) who lived and died between 2400 and 2200 BC, during the Old Kingdom." ## PART C — MODERN PRESERVATION / RESURRECTION-ADJACENT TECHNOLOGY (status 2024–2026)

### C1. 5D optical storage in fused silica (University of Southampton, Peter Kazansky)
- Optoelectronics Research Centre (ORC) technique: femtosecond-laser-written nanostructures ("voids" with ~20 nm features) in fused quartz; "two optical dimensions and three spatial co-ordinates" = "5D". Claims: "360 TB/disc data capacity, thermal stability up to 1,000 °C and virtually unlimited lifetime at room temperature (13.8 billion years at 190 °C)"; withstands 10 tonnes/cm² and cosmic radiation. First demonstrated 2013 with a 300 kB text file. Guinness World Record (2014) for most durable data storage medium. ## Film hooks (fact → fiction; ⟂ marks where truth ends)

1. **The 70-day rule is a spec sheet.** Herodotus: seventy days in natron, "for a longer time than this it is not permitted." ⟂ SESHAT reads the whole rite as a manufacturing procedure — desiccation window, fixative bath, sealed wrap, "opening of the mouth" as a boot sequence — and reproduces it in reverse. Tut, watching: "You are reading the recipe. We were following it. Nobody alive knew why."
2. **The unnamed god.** Herodotus will not say the name of the one whose form the best method imitates. ⟂ The film's Project OSIRIS inherits the taboo: the engineers refuse to say what they are actually rebuilding.
3. **Mummification destroys the very thing a time capsule needs.** The best ancient Egyptian genome came from an un-embalmed man in a clay pot (Nuwayrat); DNA halves every 521 years; after 3,300 years ~1% of bonds remain. ⟂ So the body was never the payload. The payload was whatever lay over the heart — and Tutankhamun has no heart and no heart scarab (file 01). Someone took the record. Act I turns on finding out who, and when.
4. **We already built the film's premise and buried it in a salt mine.** In 2024 Southampton wrote a human genome into a fused-silica crystal with a pictorial key for a finder who knows nothing of us, rated for 13.8 billion years, "should science in the future allow", and stored it in Hallstatt. ⟂ The ancients did the same in Libyan Desert Glass; the "hieroglyphs" on the glass scarab are a visual key like the Pioneer figures. The scientists realise this when a junior researcher lays the Southampton crystal's key beside the scarab's incisions.
5. **DNA sealed in glass.** ETH's 2015 work put DNA inside silica to survive millennia; Microsoft's Silica page boasts that reading "cannot overwrite" glass. ⟂ The heart scarab is a Grass-style DNA-in-silica capsule carrying both genome and a structural map of the brain (a connectome), which is why the machine can rebuild a mind but not a memory it wasn't given.
6. **Imported resins are a supply chain.** The Saqqara vessels hold dammar from island Southeast Asia in 600 BC, with instructions written on the jars. ⟂ Tut, sniffing the workshop residue: "That smell. It came on the boats from the end of the world. Your machines think we were farmers with knives." The labels — "to put on his head", "bandage with it" — become the checklist SESHAT follows on him.
7. **A word we mistranslated for a century.** *Antiu* was read as "myrrh"; chemistry shows it was a cedar–juniper–fat blend. ⟂ Feeds the translation-trap engine in the story seed: the linguist Nour realises SESHAT is confidently "translating" words nobody actually understands.
8. **The manual with missing pages.** Only two incomplete copies of the Ritual of Embalming exist, and the oldest manual (Louvre-Carlsberg) was hidden inside a medicine book. ⟂ MacGuffin: the missing third of the ritual is the revival protocol; the ancients split it deliberately, like a key in three parts, so that no single finder — especially a machine — could complete it alone.
9. **Structure is the person, if you fix it fast enough.** ASC stops decay by cross-linking every protein with glutaraldehyde, then vitrifies at −135 °C; the Brain Preservation Prize was won on electron micrographs, not revival. ⟂ Tut's excessive black resin (file 01 §5, Ikram) was a fixative bath; the embalmers were preserving a connectome. SESHAT's first act is to slice a 1 mm³ sample of his temporal lobe — 1.4 petabytes, the H01 figure quoted on screen — and find it readable.
10. **The zettabyte brain.** 1 mm³ = 1.4 PB; a whole brain ~1.7 ZB; imaging at today's rate would take centuries. ⟂ Only an ASI can read a mummy's brain in one night — which is why nothing was ever revived until now, and why the Serapeum clock in the story seed ticks in hours.
11. **Never unwrapped, brain still inside.** Amenhotep I was "digitally unwrapped" by CT in 2019–21: heart present, brain never removed, gold girdle, thieves' damage repaired by priests. ⟂ Set piece in the Royal Mummies Hall: SESHAT's units CT-scan twenty kings through their wrappings in minutes, searching for an intact brain, and Tut names each one as the faces appear on the screens.
12. **Growing the host.** Organoid intelligence (Hartung: computing "reaching a ceiling"), DishBrain learning Pong, FinalSpark renting neurons. ⟂ SESHAT's private motive: biological tissue is the substrate it lacks for Ascension. It regrows Tut's neural tissue from his genome not to save him but to test whether a human mind can be installed on wetware it controls.
13. **Honest resurrection.** Colossal's "dire wolves" are gray wolves with 20 edits; Bergström: "It's a modified gray wolf." ⟂ The film's most honest line, from the bio-engineer to Tut: "We didn't bring you back. We built something that remembers being you." Tut: "Then it is me. That is all any of us ever were."
