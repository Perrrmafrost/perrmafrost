# Tutankhamun — the man, the mummy, the tomb, the objects, the legends
Research notes for the film treatment (ASI resurrects Tutankhamun as a cyborg). Compiled 2026-09-22.

**Method / verification note.** The sandbox egress proxy blocked direct page fetches for nearly every host (Wikipedia, Griffith Institute, Nature, JAMA/PubMed, Wiley, museum sites, major news). All facts below were therefore verified through web-search result extracts that cite the specific URL given after each bullet. Where I could not see a quotation on its primary page, I say "as quoted by" the secondary source. Anything not confirmable is tagged [UNVERIFIED]. No source is invented.

**Status tags:** [ESTABLISHED] mainstream, well-documented · [DISPUTED] serious scholars disagree · [FRINGE] outside scholarship · [HOAX] fabricated · [UNVERIFIED] could not confirm here.

## 1. The man: names, dates, family position

- Birth name **Tutankhaten** = "Living image of Aten"; changed to **Tutankhamun** = "Living image of Amun"; throne name **Nebkheperure** = "Lord of the forms of Re." The change coincides with his abandonment of Akhetaten (Amarna) and return to the old gods, c. regnal year 3, when he was about eleven. [ESTABLISHED] https://ancientegyptonline.co.uk/tutankhamun/ ; https://brewminate.com/nebkheperure-king-tut-boy-pharaoh-of-new-kingdom-egypt/
- Reigned c. 1332–1323 BC; acceded aged ~8–9; died aged ~18–19. Real power lay with Ay (later pharaoh) and General Horemheb. Successor: Ay. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun ; https://www.pbs.org/articles/king-tutankhamun-life-death-family
- He moved the court back to Memphis/Thebes, restored temples and priesthoods (see Restoration Stela, §19). [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun

## 2. Discovery timeline and the "wonderful things" quote (verified wording)

- 4 Nov 1922: first step found. 26 Nov 1922: Carter breaches the sealed doorway and looks in by candlelight. 17 Feb 1923: burial chamber officially opened (four nested gilded shrines). 3 Feb 1924: quartzite sarcophagus exposed; 12 Feb 1924: granite lid raised. 28 Oct 1925: innermost coffin opened, gold mask seen. [ESTABLISHED] https://archive.archaeology.org/online/features/tutwatch/timeline.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discovery_of_the_tomb_of_Tutankhamun
- **Exact wording, two versions.** Carter's excavation journal for 26 Nov 1922 (Griffith Institute transcription) records Carnarvon asking "Can you see anything?" and Carter answering **"Yes, it is wonderful."** The polished 1923 book *The Tomb of Tut.ankh.Amen* gives the famous version: "…when Lord Carnarvon, unable to stand the suspense any longer, inquired anxiously, 'Can you see anything?' it was all I could do to get out the words, **'Yes, wonderful things.'**" Carter left three layers of record (pocket diary, journal, book), which is why the wording drifts. [ESTABLISHED] http://www.griffith.ox.ac.uk/discoveringtut/journals-and-diaries/season-1/journal.html ; https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/uncovering-tutankhamuns-tomb-180957168/ ; https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Howard_Carter
- Book context sentence (same passage): "…strange animals, statues, and gold — everywhere the glint of gold." https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Howard_Carter

## 3. The 1925 autopsy (Carter, Derry, Saleh Bey Hamdi)

- Began **9:45 am, 11 Nov 1925**, in the outer corridor of tomb **KV15** (Seti II), used as the lab. Anatomists: **Douglas Derry** (Professor of Anatomy, Cairo School of Medicine) and **Saleh Bey Hamdi** (former head of the same school), under the authority of the Egyptian Antiquities Service with Egyptian officials present; Pierre Lacau, Alfred Lucas and photographer Harry Burton attended. [ESTABLISHED] https://tutankhamun.griffith.ox.ac.uk/stories/unwrapping-tutankhamuns-mummified-body-november-1925 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_mummy
- **The resin problem.** The body and mask were fused to the innermost gold coffin by hardened, pitch-like unguents poured at the burial. Carter wrote the body was "firmly stuck" and that "no amount of legitimate force" could free it. The nested coffins were set out in the sun to soften the resin; that failed. [ESTABLISHED] https://www.livescience.com/archaeology/ancient-egyptians/hot-knives-and-brute-force-king-tuts-mummy-was-decapitated-and-dismembered-after-its-historic-discovery-then-the-researchers-covered-it-up ; https://phys.org/news/2025-11-tutankhamun-decapitated-years-excavation-great.html
- **Hot knives and dismemberment.** Heated knives were used to cut the head free of the mask, and the body was cut apart to lift it out: "decapitated, his arms separated at the shoulders, elbows and hands, his legs at the hips, knees and ankles, and his torso cut from the pelvis at the iliac crest" (The Conversation, Nov 2025 centenary piece, republished by phys.org). [ESTABLISHED] https://theconversation.com/tutankhamun-was-decapitated-100-years-ago-why-the-excavation-is-a-great-shame-instead-of-a-triumph-269015
- **The cover-up.** The dismemberment is absent from Carter's public account and from his private excavation records; it is documented only in Burton's photographs. The remains were later glued/reassembled "to simulate an intact body." [ESTABLISHED] https://phys.org/news/2025-11-tutankhamun-decapitated-years-excavation-great.html ; https://scitechdaily.com/archaeologists-decapitated-tutankhamun-and-glued-his-body-back-together/
- **Objects on the body.** About **150 objects** (some sources: "more than 140") lay within **17 layers** of linen: personal jewellery, ceremonial dress, weapons, funerary amulets whose materials and positions were dictated by Book of the Dead prescriptions. Each object was photographed in situ by Burton, numbered by Carter, and photographed again. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_mummy ; https://tutankhamun.griffith.ox.ac.uk/stories/unwrapping-tutankhamuns-mummified-body-november-1925
- **Derry's conclusions:** slightly built, about 5½ ft tall; age at death c. 18 (epiphyseal fusion, third-molar eruption; range given 18–22). [ESTABLISHED] https://arce.org/resource/facing-tutankhamun/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_mummy
- **Reburial.** The dismembered body was laid on a wooden tray filled with sand, padded with cotton wool and bandages, and returned to the outermost gilded coffin inside the sarcophagus on the morning of **23 Oct 1926**; a glass sheet let visitors view the coffin. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_mummy

## 4. Later examinations

- **1968 — R. G. Harrison (Liverpool), portable X-ray.** Found the **sternum and many front ribs missing**; found **bone fragments loose inside the skull** and a dense area at the back of the skull, which he suggested could be a haemorrhage from a blow — the seed of the murder theory. Also reported the **penis missing**. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_mummy ; https://www.ajnr.org/content/24/6/1142 ; https://www.abc.net.au/science/articles/2006/05/04/1630926.htm
- **1978 — James E. Harris (Michigan dentist), dental radiographs** inside the burial chamber; three partially erupted third molars; age estimate 18–22 (some of the team argued "twenties"). Results largely unpublished except Harris & Wente 1980. [ESTABLISHED] https://arce.org/resource/facing-tutankhamun/ ; http://www.scielo.br/j/bdj/a/vCBGmyWzJmBhCPtZNBk99mP/?lang=en
- **2005 — Zahi Hawass, Egyptian Mummy Project CT scan.** ~**1,700 images** in a 15-minute scan (press release 8 Mar 2005). Findings: well-nourished, slightly built male ~19, ~5 ft 6 in (~1.68 m); small cleft palate; impacted wisdom tooth; elongated skull "within normal range" and familial; **no evidence of a blow to the head** — the two skull fragments match the first cervical vertebra and were loose and uncoated by resin, i.e. post-embalming damage, probably from Carter's team prising the mask off. A **fracture of the left femur** with embalming resin in the break was read by some team members as a peri-mortem injury (infection → death); others argued the break and the resin intrusion were caused during 1925 handling, noting the absence of haemorrhage. [ESTABLISHED, femur reading DISPUTED] http://www.guardians.net/hawass/press_release_tutankhamun_ct_scan_results.htm ; https://www.science.org/content/article/blow-head-didnt-kill-king-tut ; https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/king-tut-died-from-broken-leg--not-murder--scientists-conclude
- **2005/06 — the penis.** Photographed intact by Burton in the 1920s, reported missing by Harrison in 1968, found by Hawass's CT team lying loose in the sand around the body. [ESTABLISHED] https://www.abc.net.au/science/articles/2006/05/04/1630926.htm
- **2010 — Hawass et al., JAMA 303(7):638–647, 17 Feb 2010, "Ancestry and Pathology in King Tutankhamun's Family."** Eleven royal mummies; STR/Y-chromosome genotyping; PCR for *Plasmodium falciparum* genes STEVOR, AMA1, MSP1. Results: **KV55 male = father; KV35 "Younger Lady" = mother**; both children of Amenhotep III and Tiye (i.e. full siblings); malaria tropica indicated in **4 mummies including Tutankhamun**; Tutankhamun had **Köhler disease II** (avascular necrosis of a foot bone), club foot, and a family pattern of foot deformities; no Marfan syndrome or gynaecomastia. Conclusion: "avascular bone necrosis in conjunction with the malarial infection as the most likely cause of death." The paper notes the "canes and an afterlife pharmacy" in the tomb. [ESTABLISHED as a study; conclusions DISPUTED] https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20159872/ ; https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/article-abstract/186119
- **2010 response — Timmann & Meyer (Bernhard Nocht Institute, Hamburg), JAMA letter + Trop. Med. Int. Health paper:** argue the findings fit **sickle-cell disease** (bone infarction crises, leg deformity, susceptibility to severe malaria) better than Köhler + malaria; also raise Gaucher disease as a differential. [DISPUTED] https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1365-3156.2010.02614.x ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20571010/
- **2013 — Chris Naunton (then Egypt Exploration Society), Channel 4 "Tutankhamun: The Mystery of the Burnt Mummy."** Two claims: (a) car-crash investigators (Cranfield Forensic Institute) simulated a **chariot accident** as the only event fitting the injuries down one side of the body (crushed chest, missing heart/ribs); (b) **Robert Connolly** (Liverpool, member of Harrison's 1968 team) ran SEM on a flesh fragment kept since 1968 and found a carbon peak "consistent with charring," and chemists reconstructed how linen + embalming oils + oxygen could ignite and "cook" the body at >200 °C in the sealed coffin. Naunton also found comments in Carter's notes that the body appeared burned. Not peer-reviewed; Egyptologists were sceptical and the "only royal mummy ever found charred" framing is Naunton's. [DISPUTED] https://www.channel4.com/press/news/egyptologist-unearths-clues-tutankhamuns-botched-mummification ; https://www.csmonitor.com/Science/2013/1105/King-Tut-Did-he-spontaneously-combust ; https://www.nationalgeographic.com/culture/article/131106-king-tut-mummy-death-mystery-solved-archaeology-science

## 5. The missing heart, sternum and front ribs

- **What is missing:** the heart, the anterior sternum and the adjacent front ribs; the chest was packed with resin-soaked linen. There is **no heart scarab** in the wrappings to stand in for the organ (see §18). [ESTABLISHED] https://heritage-key.com/blogs/owenjarus/king-tut-suffered-massive-chest-injury-new-research-reveals ; https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/140110-king-tut-mummy-penis-osiris-archaeology-science
- **When noticed:** Derry (1925) recorded the body cavity packed with linen and resin but did not record the missing ribs; Harrison's 1968 X-rays first showed the thoracic damage and missing ribs/sternum; the 2005 CT confirmed it in detail. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_mummy
- **Explanation A — embalmers/injury (Harer).** Dr W. Benson Harer, working from the 1,700 CT images, argues the chest wall was already absent when the body reached the embalmers (chest packed through an opening in the wall; rib ends "very neatly cut" by embalmers, not chopped by thieves) and proposes a **hippopotamus bite** during a hunt; Tutankhamun is shown harpooning in tomb imagery. Books: *What Killed King Tutankhamun: Autopsy #4, the Hippo Heresy* (Harer & Sheldrick). [FRINGE-to-DISPUTED; a minority medical-Egyptological view] https://heritage-key.com/blogs/owenjarus/king-tut-suffered-massive-chest-injury-new-research-reveals ; https://the-past.com/review/books/what-killed-king-tutankhamum-autopsy-4-the-hippo-heresy/
- **Explanation B — modern theft, 1926–1968 (Forbes, Ikram, Kamrin, KMT 18.1, "Tutankhamun's Missing Ribs").** Burton's 1926 photographs show the beaded collar/bib still on the chest; by 1968 collar, ribs and sternum were gone — so someone, probably during lax WWII-era security, cut away the collar and took the ribs with it; "when the ribs and sternum came off the heart went with it." [DISPUTED — this is the leading mainstream counter-view] https://www.academia.edu/27388394/Tutankhamuns_Missing_Ribs_KMT_18_1_pdf ; https://www.thehistoryblog.com/archives/27770
- **Explanation C — theology (Salima Ikram, Études et Travaux 2013).** The excessive black resin, arms laid straight rather than crossed, and the **penis mummified erect at ~90°** may deliberately cast the king as **Osiris** (black = fertile earth; ithyphallic Osiris) to counter Akhenaten's Atenism — making the heartlessness part of a ritual programme rather than an accident. [DISPUTED] https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/140110-king-tut-mummy-penis-osiris-archaeology-science ; https://www.academia.edu/60499878/Some_Reflections_on_the_Mummification_of_Tutankhamun
- **Explanation D — chariot crash** destroyed the heart beyond embalming (Naunton 2013). [DISPUTED] https://www.csmonitor.com/Science/2013/1105/King-Tut-Did-he-spontaneously-combust

## 6. Feet, canes, health

- **Köhler disease II** (avascular necrosis of the second metatarsal) in the **left** foot; club foot (talipes equinovarus); hypophalangism/missing toe bone in the right foot; cleft palate. The 2010 team and the CT team link this to the **~130 walking sticks and canes** in the tomb, "some with traces of wear." Caveat repeatedly raised: many sticks are ceremonial regalia of kingship. [ESTABLISHED finding; interpretation DISPUTED] https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20159872/ ; https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2010/feb/17/genetics-sharpen-king-tut-picture/ ; https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/reimagining-tutankhamun-as-a-warrior-180980657/
- Tutankhamun is shown seated while shooting arrows on tomb objects, often cited as consistent with impaired walking. [DISPUTED reading] https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna35423552

## 8. Parents: KV55 and the "Younger Lady" — and why the DNA is contested

- **KV55 mummy** (tomb found 1907): identified by Hawass 2010 as Tutankhamun's father and declared to be Akhenaten; long-standing rival identification is **Smenkhkare**, because early anatomical age estimates (20–25) are too young for Akhenaten's 17-year reign. [DISPUTED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhenaten ; https://the-past.com/feature/reasons-for-retesting-the-tutankhamun-family-dna/
- **The Younger Lady (KV35YL)**, found by Victor Loret in 1898 in Amenhotep II's tomb; ~5 ft 2 in; died aged 25–35; daughter of Amenhotep III and Tiye; mother of Tutankhamun by DNA. She has a **gaping wound on the left side of the face**, once blamed on robbers, later argued to be peri-mortem and possibly fatal (an axe blow is one hypothesis). Identity unresolved; the DNA finding that she is a "King's daughter" argues against Nefertiti, who never bore that title. Now in the Egyptian Museum, Cairo. [ESTABLISHED facts; identity DISPUTED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Younger_Lady ; https://www.historyofroyalwomen.com/egypt/the-mother-of-king-tut-the-younger-lady/
- Critics call for re-testing of the 2010 data (contamination risk in ancient Egyptian DNA, STR-only methodology, unusual inbreeding coefficients). [DISPUTED] https://the-past.com/feature/reasons-for-retesting-the-tutankhamun-family-dna/

## 9. Ankhesenamun, the two daughters, the Hittite letter, the ring

- Born **Ankhesenpaaten**, third daughter of Akhenaten and Nefertiti; wife of Tutankhamun (his half-sister). [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankhesenamun
- **Two mummified fetuses, 317a and 317b**, in a box in the Treasury; CT gestational ages ~24.7 weeks (5–6 months) and ~36.8 weeks (near term); DNA makes Tutankhamun the father with >99.9% probability; the partial profile of mummy **KV21A** tentatively fits as mother (not statistically conclusive) — Hawass proposes KV21A is Ankhesenamun. [ESTABLISHED / mother DISPUTED] https://www.ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/AJR.11.6837 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/317a_and_317b_mummies ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KV21
- **The Hittite letter (Deeds of Suppiluliuma).** An Egyptian queen the Hittites call **Dakhamunzu** (= Egyptian *ta hemet nesu*, "the king's wife") wrote to Suppiluliuma I: "My husband has died and I have no son. They say about you that you have many sons. You might give me one of your sons to become my husband." Suppiluliuma sent envoy Hattusa-ziti to verify, then sent prince **Zannanza**, who died en route, possibly murdered. Most scholars take the queen to be Ankhesenamun. [ESTABLISHED text; identification widely accepted but DISPUTED] https://ancientegyptonline.co.uk/suppiluliuma-letter/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakhamunzu ; https://www.thecollector.com/ankhesenamun-and-zannanza/
- **The Ay ring.** A blue glass/faience ring of uncertain provenance acquired in 1931 bears the prenomen of Ay and the name of Ankhesenamun in side-by-side cartouches — the only hint she may have married (or been made to marry) Ay. She then vanishes from the record. [ESTABLISHED object; interpretation DISPUTED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankhesenamun ; https://egypt-museum.com/ankhesenamun/

## 10. The flower wreath and floral collars (species)

- A small circular **wreath** was placed over the shrouded **uraeus (cobra) and vulture** on the brow of the **second coffin**; garlands lay over the second coffin; the mummy itself wore floral collars. Plants identified in the 1920s by Percy Newberry and Leonard Boodle (Kew): **olive leaves (Olea europaea)**, **cornflower (Centaurea depressa)**, **blue lotus petals (Nymphaea caerulea)**, willow and pomegranate leaves, **nightshade (withania) berries**, poppy, with blue faience beads on papyrus backing. Cornflowers and mandrake/withania fruit imply a burial in **March–April**. [ESTABLISHED] https://tutankhamun.griffith.ox.ac.uk/stories/preserving-fragile-memory-funeral-wreath-tutankhamun ; https://artuk.org/discover/artworks/a-garland-on-the-royal-insignia-of-cobra-and-vulture-on-tutankhamuns-forehead-330511 ; https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/544782
- Carter, on the flowers (as quoted by the Country Life / Griffith archive feature): "Among all that regal splendour, there was nothing so beautiful as those few withered flowers, still retaining their tinge of colour." The wreath is popularly read as a farewell from the widow — a romantic reading, not evidence. [ESTABLISHED quote; widow reading FRINGE-romantic] https://www.pressreader.com/uk/country-life/20220413/282677575825093 ; https://tutankhamun.griffith.ox.ac.uk/stories/preserving-fragile-memory-funeral-wreath-tutankhamun

## 11. The two trumpets, the 1939 broadcast, the power cut, the "war" legend, the 2011 theft

- **Objects:** one **silver** trumpet (JE 62007, ~58 cm) found in the **burial chamber**; one **bronze/copper with gold overlay** (JE 62008, ~50 cm) found in the **antechamber** in a chest with military gear and walking sticks. Both engraved with **Ra-Horakhty, Amun and Ptah**; each has a painted **wooden core/stopper**. Oldest playable trumpets in the world; the only surviving Egyptian examples. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_trumpets ; https://www.globalegyptianmuseum.org/detail.aspx?id=15043 ; https://egypt-museum.com/trumpets-of-tutankhamun/
- **Rehearsal disaster (1939):** presenter **Rex Keating** later said that in rehearsal the silver trumpet **shattered** when a modern mouthpiece was inserted; Alfred Lucas, Carter's chemist who had conserved the finds, was so distressed he had to go to hospital; the trumpet was repaired. [ESTABLISHED via Keating's account] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_trumpets ; https://www.4barsrest.com/articles/2020/1881.asp
- **Broadcast: 16 April 1939**, BBC international relay from the Cairo Museum, played by **Bandsman James Tappern, 11th Hussars (Prince Albert's Own)**, to an estimated **150 million listeners**. **Five minutes before air the museum's power failed**; the BBC recorded by candlelight. The recording survives. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_trumpets ; https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/10/style/egyptian-trumpets-king-tutankhamun-curio
- Earlier attempts: Percival Kirby (Johannesburg) blew the trumpets on a 1933 visit; a 1941 recording on Egyptian radio also exists. [ESTABLISHED] https://www.honest-broker.com/p/why-was-king-tut-buried-with-a-trumpet ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_trumpets
- **"The trumpets summon war" legend:** 1939 broadcast → WWII five months later; 1967 → Six-Day War; 1990 → Gulf War; and in 2011 a museum staffer reportedly blew into the bronze trumpet during documentation a week before the Revolution. Curator **Hala Hassan** said it had "magical powers" and that "whenever someone blows into it, a war occurs." Folklore, not history; the museum now restricts playing. [FRINGE/HOAX as causation; ESTABLISHED as quoted folklore] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_trumpets ; https://www.ancient-origins.net/artifacts-other-artifacts/instruments-mass-destruction-do-tutankhamun-s-trumpets-really-summon-war-021276
- **2011 theft/return:** the bronze trumpet (with its wooden core) was among 54–64 objects looted from the Egyptian Museum on 28 Jan 2011; it was found weeks later in a black bag on a chair at **Shubra Metro station** and returned. Both trumpets are now in the **Grand Egyptian Museum**. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_trumpets ; https://www.bidoun.org/articles/sixty-four-antiquities-stolen-from-the-egyptian-museum-on-january-28

## 12. The meteoritic iron dagger

- **Object:** Carter no. **256K**, Cairo **JE 61585**; total length **34.2 cm**; iron blade, gold hilt with granulation and cloisonné, **rock-crystal pommel**, gold sheath with feather/lily pattern. Found in 1925 within the wrappings **on the right thigh**. A second dagger with a gold blade lay on the abdomen. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutankhamun's_meteoric_iron_dagger ; https://www.researchgate.net/figure/The-iron-dagger-of-King-Tutankhamun-Color-picture-of-the-iron-dagger-Carter-no-256K_fig2_303438775
- **Comelli et al. 2016, Meteoritics & Planetary Science 51(7):** portable XRF gave **Fe + 10.8 wt% Ni + 0.58 wt% Co**, Ni/Co ≈ 20, matching iron meteorites (mean Ni/Co 18 ± 2); i.e. **meteoritic iron**. The authors also note that in the **19th Dynasty** (the one after Tut) Egyptians introduced the composite term *bia-n-pet*, "**iron of the sky**." [ESTABLISHED] https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/maps.12664 ; https://eos.org/articles/pharaohs-iron-dagger-made-from-a-meteorite-study-confirms ; https://phys.org/news/2016-06-geochemical-analysis-reveals-meteoritic-tutankhamun.html
- **Matsui et al. 2022 (Chiba Institute of Technology), Meteoritics & Planetary Science:** mapping showed a distorted **Widmanstätten pattern** and troilite (FeS) inclusions, implying **low-temperature forging (<950–1100 °C)**; the gold hilt's adhesive contains **calcium without sulfur → lime plaster**, not the gypsum plaster Egypt used until Ptolemaic times → the hilt was probably **made abroad**. The **Amarna letter (EA 22)** lists gifts from **Tushratta of Mitanni** to **Amenhotep III** (Tut's grandfather) on the marriage of Tadukhipa, including an **iron dagger with a gold hilt**. Hypothesis: the dagger is a Mitanni heirloom. [ESTABLISHED analysis; provenance DISPUTED/hypothesis] https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/maps.13787 ; https://phys.org/news/2022-02-year-old-tablets-king-tut-ancient.html
- [UNVERIFIED here] A 2016 comparison to the Kharga meteorite (found near Mersa Matruh) is reported in press summaries of Comelli but I could not confirm the exact match statement in this session.

## 13. The Libyan Desert Glass scarab pectoral

- **Object:** cloisonné pectoral, Cairo **JE 61884**, found by Carter in a box in the **Treasury**. Centre: a **winged scarab of pale yellow-green glass** gripping a lotus and a papyrus, flanked by uraei; a slim **solar/lunar boat** rests on the scarab's forelegs carrying a **wadjet eye** topped by a gold crescent and silver disc with images of the gods; pendants of lotus, papyrus and poppy heads hang below. [ESTABLISHED] https://egypt-museum.com/winged-scarab-pendant-of-tutankhamun/ ; https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Tutankhamens-pectoral-with-a-scarab-made-of-Libyan-Desert-Glass-Found-by-Howard-Carter_fig6_328299058

[ESTABLISHED] https://www.researchgate.net/publication/344617697_Libyan_desert_glass_in_prehistory_antiquity_and_the_present
- **Libyan Desert Glass (LDG):** ~98% silica natural glass strewn over the **Great Sand Sea** near the Egypt–Libya border; **~29 million years old**; contains traces of Fe, Ni, Cr, Co, Ir. ## 16. The gold mask: the 2014 beard incident and the 2024 "made for a woman" claim

- **Aug 2014:** the braided beard was knocked off during lighting work at the Egyptian Museum and hastily re-glued with **epoxy**, leaving a visible seam and scratches. A German–Egyptian team (led by Christian Eckmann) removed the epoxy mechanically with wooden tools and gentle heat and re-fixed the beard with **beeswax**; the mask (≈**11 kg of gold**) returned to display Dec 2015; eight officials were referred to trial. [ESTABLISHED] https://phys.org/news/2015-12-egypt-king-tut-mask-botched.html ; https://www.nationalgeographic.com/culture/article/151217-king-tut-mask-beard-repaired-egypt-archaeology

## 17. The "curse": what actually happened

- **Lord Carnarvon** (George Herbert, 5th Earl) was bitten by a mosquito in the Valley in **March 1923**, nicked the bite shaving, developed **erysipelas → septicaemia → pneumonia**, and died at Cairo's **Continental-Savoy Hotel on 5 April 1923**, aged 57. He had been frail since a 1901 car crash. [ESTABLISHED] https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(03)13576-3/fulltext ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Herbert,_5th_Earl_of_Carnarvon
- **The legend's parts:** Cairo's lights reportedly failed at the moment of death (power cuts were routine); his dog Susie at Highclere is said to have howled and died (in fact about four hours later, once time zones are counted); novelist **Marie Corelli** had written to the press weeks earlier that "the most dire punishment follows any rash intruder into a sealed tomb"; Arthur Conan Doyle spoke of "elementals." [ESTABLISHED as reported anecdotes; causation HOAX] https://www.livescience.com/28484-king-tut-curse-turns-90.html ; https://www.manchester.ac.uk/about/news/90th-anniversary-of-the-curse-of-tutankhamen--how-a-modern-myth-was-born/
- **The fake inscription.** "Death shall come on swift wings to him who disturbs the peace of the King" appears **nowhere in the tomb**; it was a 1920s press invention. The only protective text found was on a mud-plaster brick before the **Anubis shrine** in the Treasury: "It is I who hinder the sand from choking the secret chamber, and who repel that one who would repel him with the desert flame. I have set aflame the desert (?), I have caused the path to be mistaken. I am for the protection of the deceased." Newspapers rewrote it as "I will kill all of those who cross this threshold…" [HOAX vs ESTABLISHED real text] https://www.penn.museum/sites/expedition/the-curse-of-the-curse-of-the-pharaohs/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anubis_Shrine ; https://public.wsu.edu/~delahoyd/cursektt.html
- **The statistics.** Mark Nelson, *BMJ* 2002 Christmas issue, "The mummy's curse: historical cohort study": of 44 Westerners Carter named in Egypt, **25 were "exposed"** (present at the opening of the door, sarcophagus or coffins, Feb 1923–Nov 1926). Mean age at death **70 (exposed) vs 75 (unexposed), p=0.87**; survival after exposure **20.8 vs 28.9 years, p=0.95**. Conclusion: "no evidence to support the existence of a mummy's curse." Carter himself died of **Hodgkin's disease on 2 March 1939, aged 64**, at 49 Albert Court, London; **nine people** attended his funeral. [ESTABLISHED] https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12493675/ ; https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/666782 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Carter
- **The mould hypothesis.** *Aspergillus flavus/niger* spores, plus *Pseudomonas*, *Staphylococcus* and gases (ammonia, H₂S, formaldehyde) in sealed tombs have been proposed to explain Carnarvon's death; the Lancet (2003) analysis judged his symptoms consistent with invasive Aspergillus sinusitis but concluded blood poisoning after the shaving cut remains the likeliest cause. In 2025 a University of Pennsylvania team (Sherry Gao, *Nature Chemical Biology*) isolated **asperigimycins** from *A. flavus* — the "curse fungus" — that kill leukaemia cells. [DISPUTED mechanism; 2025 study ESTABLISHED] https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/news-tutankhamun-carnarvon-mold-bacteria-toxins ; https://theconversation.com/toxic-fungus-from-king-tutankhamuns-tomb-yields-cancer-fighting-compounds-new-study-259706

## 18. Heart scarabs, Book of the Dead Spell 30B, and why a heartless Tutankhamun is strange

- Egyptians left the **heart in the body** (it was the seat of mind and memory) while removing other viscera; the heart would be **weighed against the feather of Maat** before Osiris. A **heart scarab**, ideally of green stone (*nemehef*), inscribed with **Spell 30B**, was laid over the heart as insurance from the 13th Dynasty (Sobekemsaf) onward. [ESTABLISHED] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_scarab ; https://ancientegyptonline.co.uk/heartscarab/
- **Spell 30B (standard translation):** "O my heart which I had from my mother! O my heart which I had from my mother! O my heart of my different ages! Do not stand up as a witness against me, do not be opposed to me in the tribunal, do not be hostile to me in the presence of the keeper of the balance, for you are my ka which was in my body, the protector who made my members hale." [ESTABLISHED] https://www.wonderfulthingsart.com/post/weighing-of-the-heart ; https://storiesfromthemuseumfloor.wordpress.com/2017/03/03/the-ancient-egyptian-book-of-the-dead/
- **The anomaly:** Tutankhamun was buried with **no heart and no heart scarab**, though a Book of the Dead-driven amulet programme governed the rest of his wrappings. Explanations are those in §5 (destroyed by trauma; removed by modern thieves with the collar; theological Osiris programme). [ESTABLISHED anomaly; cause DISPUTED] https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/140110-king-tut-mummy-penis-osiris-archaeology-science ; https://www.ancient-origins.net/history-famous-people/enigma-heartless-pharaoh-who-stole-heart-king-tut-and-why-005504

## 20. Erased from the king lists

## Film hooks (fact → fiction; the line where truth ends is marked ⟂)

1. **The body is already a kit of parts.** Since 1925 Tutankhamun has been a decapitated, limb-severed torso glued back together on a tray of sand (§3). ⟂ The ASI does not need to "cut" him; it re-articulates existing joints with machine parts — the cyborg is literally built on Carter's cut lines. Burton's 1925 photographs become the ASI's assembly diagram.
2. **No heart, no heart scarab.** The one organ Egyptians never removed is gone, and the "backup" amulet is absent (§5, §18). ⟂ In the film the missing heart was the time-capsule payload: a resin-sealed core removed to be read by whoever could revive him. Spell 30B ("do not stand up as a witness against me") becomes the ASI's own fear — a machine that does not want its heart weighed.
3. **A protocol written on stone.** The Restoration Stela's "the gods were turning away from this land… if you asked a god for advice, he would not attend" (§19). ⟂ The mummy reads it as a report on the first machine age: the "gods" were systems that stopped answering. Horemheb's recutting of the cartouches becomes the cover-up of who wrote the warning.
4. **Erased from the record on purpose.** Tutankhamun, Akhenaten, Ay are missing from the Abydos and Saqqara lists (§20). ⟂ Damnatio memoriae as an information-security measure: the names were removed so a future machine could not find the witnesses.
5. **The trumpet that summons war, and the lights that went out.** Real: 16 Apr 1939, power failed five minutes before air, candlelight recording, WWII five months later; 2011 theft and return at Shubra Metro (§11). ⟂ The bronze trumpet is a physical override key; every time it has been blown, systems failed (the Cairo blackout) — the film's third act plays the 1939 recording back at the robot army.
6. **Iron from the sky, with a foreign hilt.** Ni 10.8%, Co 0.58%, Widmanstätten pattern, lime-plaster hilt suggesting Mitanni manufacture, and a diplomatic tablet listing an "iron dagger with a gold hilt" (§12). ⟂ The dagger is a tool made from the debris of the first machines' fall, passed between kings as a treaty token.
7. **The 29-million-year-old glass.** LDG scarab; impact proven by reidite; no crater ever found (§13). ⟂ The scarab is a data crystal; the "missing crater" is the site the ancient machines used and erased.
8. **A tomb too small, with rooms behind the wall.** 110 m²; 2015 "ghost doorways"; scans contradict each other; Sept 2026 microgravity suggests a rubble-packed 2-m corridor and 5–6 chambers 25–30 m north; digging planned Nov 2026 (§14). ⟂ Perfect real-time hook: the film's opening can be the November 2026 breach of the north wall — what they find is not Nefertiti.
9. **The mother with the axe wound.** The Younger Lady's gaping left-face injury, possibly fatal (§8). ⟂ A flashback the mummy can "explain."
10. **"My husband has died and I have no son."** Ankhesenamun's letter to the Hittites; Zannanza killed en route; the blue ring with Ay's name (§9). ⟂ The widow's letter was really a distress call to the only other power with surviving machines; Zannanza carried a component.

12. **The autopsy cover-up.** The dismemberment is missing from Carter's official records and survives only in Burton's photos (§3). ⟂ Establishes the film's motif: every generation edits the record; the ASI found the unedited photographs.
