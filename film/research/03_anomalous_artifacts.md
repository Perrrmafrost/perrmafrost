# 03 — Anomalous / "Space-Age" Ancient Artifacts and Hieroglyphs: What They Really Are

Research notes for the feature treatment (cyborg Tutankhamun / ASI takeover). Purpose: give the writers REAL, checkable facts about every famous "out-of-place" artifact the mummy might "explain," and mark exactly where the truth ends.

## Status legend

- **[ESTABLISHED]** — mainstream scholarly consensus; safe to state as fact in dialogue.
- **[DISPUTED]** — genuine live scholarly disagreement (not fringe vs. mainstream, but expert vs. expert).
- **[FRINGE]** — the object is real, but the "advanced technology / aliens" reading is rejected by specialists.
- **[HOAX]** — the object or text is fabricated, or has never been shown to exist.

## Verification note (read this first)

- Facts marked **(verified)** were confirmed this session via live web search results whose URLs are cited inline.
- Facts marked **(background — not re-verified this session)** come from prior knowledge. The session's web-search budget was exhausted after 24 searches and the network egress policy blocked direct page fetches (Wikipedia, British Museum, Nature, PNAS, university sites, etc.). Treat these as very likely correct but confirm the exact number/date before locking it into a script line.

## 1. The Abydos "helicopter" hieroglyphs — Temple of Seti I, Abydos

**Status: [FRINGE] (object real; "helicopter" reading rejected by every Egyptologist)**

- What it is (verified): a carved hieroglyphic panel in the Temple of Seti I at Abydos that is a **palimpsest** — two overlapping inscriptions, the royal titles of Ramesses II superimposed on those of his father Seti I. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_hieroglyphs
- Mechanism (verified): in antiquity the five-fold royal titulary of Seti I was replaced with that of Ramesses II by re-carving and **filling** the old signs with plaster; when the filling falls out, bits of old and new glyphs overlap and form "strange signs." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_hieroglyphs ; https://egypt-museum.com/helicopter-hieroglyphs/
- The original Seti I text (verified): reign c. 1294–1279 BC; the epithet translates **"Powerful of scimitar, who suppresses the nine bows (enemies of Egypt)."** The Ramesses II replacement Two-Ladies (Nebty) name reads **"Who protects Egypt and overthrows the foreign countries."** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_hieroglyphs
- "Nine Bows" (background — not re-verified this session): the stock Egyptian phrase for the totality of Egypt's traditional enemies; it appears under the feet of royal statues and on footstools (including in Tutankhamun's tomb furniture) so the king symbolically treads on his enemies.
- Location (background): on a ceiling architrave/lintel in the hypostyle hall of the temple; the "helicopter," "submarine," and "jet" shapes are all on the same beam.
- Mainstream (verified): "Egyptologists are unanimous" that the shapes are palimpsest artifacts, not machines. https://madainproject.com/helicopter_hieroglyphs ; https://www.fringepop321.com/helicopters-in-ancient-egypt.htm
- Fringe (verified): promoted in ancient-astronaut circles as a helicopter, tank/submarine and jet. https://factfun.co/ancient-egyptian-helicopter-hieroglyphs-abydos/
- Photo: https://www.flickr.com/photos/manna4u/30858634654

## 2. The Saqqara Bird

**Status: [FRINGE] (object real; glider claim experimentally failed)**

- What it is (verified): a wooden bird figurine excavated in 1898 from the tomb of Pa-di-Imen at Saqqara; now in the Egyptian Museum, Cairo. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saqqara_Bird ; https://historyegypt.org/artifacts/saqqara-bird
- Dimensions/material (background — not re-verified this session): sycamore wood; wingspan c. 180 mm; length c. 14 cm; weight c. 39 g; dated c. 200 BC (Late/Ptolemaic period); Cairo catalogue no. 6347.
- The claim (verified): in 1969 Dr Khalil Messiha, physician/Egyptologist and model-airplane hobbyist, found it in the museum and argued it was a scale model of a glider proving Egyptian knowledge of aerodynamics. https://fly.historicwings.com/2015/05/the-saqqara-bird/ ; https://www.ancient-origins.net/ancient-technology/controversial-saqqara-bird-00386
- The tail problem (verified): the bird has a vertical tail fin but **no horizontal tailplane**; Messiha assumed one had been lost. https://fly.historicwings.com/2015/05/the-saqqara-bird/
- Test (verified): Martin Gregorie, a designer/builder of free-flight gliders, built a balsa replica and concluded: **"the Saqqara Bird never flew. It is totally unstable without a tailplane… Even after a tailplane was fitted the glide performance was disappointing."** https://www.ancient-origins.net/unexplained-phenomena/he-saqqara-bird-did-ancient-egyptians-know-how-fly-0010035 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saqqara_Bird
- 2006 test (background): Simon Sanderson built a 5x scale model for a TV documentary and reported lift in a wind tunnel — but only after adding the missing tailplane; this is the source of the "it flies" claim recycled online.
- Mainstream (verified): a ceremonial object, toy, or weathervane-type object in the well-attested Egyptian bird/falcon tradition; note its beak and eyes are bird-like and there are no other "aircraft" models in 3,000 years of Egyptian art. https://www.archaeo-acoustics.com/post/the-saqqara-bird-artifact-icon-or-ancient-technology

## 3. The Dendera "Light" (a.k.a. the Egyptian "light bulb")

**Status: [FRINGE] (relief real and captioned; "bulb" reading contradicted by the text carved next to it)**

- What it is (verified): reliefs in the **crypts** of the Temple of Hathor at Dendera showing statues of the god Harsomtus (often syncretized with Ra) represented as an upright snake emerging from a lotus flower, typically on a barge. In the "light" variant Harsomtus is inside an oval container called **hn**, which may symbolize the womb of the sky goddess Nut; a **djed** pillar supports the snake/container; sometimes a figure with raised arms and a sun disk on its head appears. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendera_light ; https://madainproject.com/dendera_light
- Date (background — not re-verified this session): the standing temple is late Ptolemaic (begun under Ptolemy XII, mid-1st century BC) with decoration continuing into the Roman period; the crypt reliefs are therefore roughly 2,000 years old, not "pyramid age."
- The caption (verified): translation by Egyptologist Dr Wolfgang Waitkus of the text surrounding the images: **"Harsomtus, the great god who is in Dendera, who rises from the lotus flower as living Ba, whose perfection is risen by the km3tjw-images of his Ka, whose ssmw-image is worshipped by the crew of the day barge, whose body is carried by the dd-pillar, below his ssmw-image the primeval beginner (Hathor) sits and whose majesty is carried by the companions of his Ka."** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendera_light
- Mainstream (verified): a creation-myth image — the lotus births the sun-serpent inside a protective "bubble"; divine energy/regeneration motif. https://handwiki.org/wiki/Unsolved:Dendera_light ; https://www.ancient-origins.net/ancient-technology/dendera-light-0081
- Fringe (background): the "light bulb" reading was popularized by Peter Krassa and Reinhard Habeck (Das Licht der Pharaonen, 1992) and by Erich von Däniken: snake = filament, hn = glass envelope, djed = insulator, the "cable" = the lotus stem.

## 4. The Baghdad Battery

**Status: [FRINGE] (object real and now lost; "battery" hypothesis rejected)**

- What it is (verified): a terracotta jar c. 140 mm tall containing a copper cylinder (rolled sheet) and a central iron rod, sealed with an asphalt stopper. Found at Khujut Rabu near Baghdad, near the Parthian/Sasanian capital Ctesiphon. Discovery dated 1936 in most accounts. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad_Battery ; https://interestingengineering.com/culture/ancient-energy-baghdad-battery
- Dating (verified): Parthian (150 BC–AD 223) or Sasanian (AD 224–650); "about 2,000 years old" is the popular figure. https://www.discoveryuk.com/mysteries/the-mystery-of-the-baghdad-battery/
- The claim (verified): in 1938 Wilhelm König, director of the National Museum of Iraq, proposed the objects were galvanic cells used for electroplating or electrotherapy. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_K%C3%B6nig ; https://www.history.com/articles/baghdad-battery
- Mainstream (verified): storage vessels for sacred scrolls — the papyrus/parchment was wrapped around the iron rod inside the copper tube; the Smithsonian's public summary accepts scroll storage as the most parsimonious reading. https://www.discoveryuk.com/mysteries/the-mystery-of-the-baghdad-battery/ ; https://talesoftimesforgotten.com/2020/03/08/debunking-the-so-called-baghdad-battery/
- Why not a battery (background — not re-verified this session): no wires, conductors, or anything to be powered has ever been found in the region; the asphalt seal would make it impossible to replenish an electrolyte; the corrosion pattern is consistent with decayed organic contents (a scroll); British Museum specialists favor a Sasanian date, which post-dates most fringe narratives.
- MythBusters (verified): the 2005 episode showed a replica filled with lemon juice produced c. 4 volts; ten linked jars electroplated a coin and powered acupuncture needles — i.e., the geometry CAN make a weak cell, which says nothing about whether it ever did. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MythBusters_(2005_season) ; https://www.steme.org/post/the-baghdad-battery
- Earlier replica (verified): engineer Willard Gray filled a reconstruction with grape juice and got a current. https://www.steme.org/post/the-baghdad-battery
- Fate (verified): the Iraq Museum was ransacked in April 2003; 14,000–15,000 objects were stolen, including the original "battery," which has **never been recovered**. https://www.iraqicivilsociety.org/archives/4946 ; https://talesoftimesforgotten.com/2020/03/08/debunking-the-so-called-baghdad-battery/
- Fresh scholarship (verified to exist): Sino-Platonic Papers no. 377 (January 2026) is a monograph on the Baghdad Battery. https://www.sino-platonic.org/complete/spp377_baghdad_battery.pdf

## 5. The Antikythera Mechanism

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] as a genuine 2nd-century BC geared astronomical calculator — the ONE item on this list where the "impossibly advanced" label is actually earned**

- Discovery (background — not re-verified this session): recovered by sponge divers from a Roman-era shipwreck off Antikythera in 1901; recognized as a geared device by Valerios Stais in 1902; now in the National Archaeological Museum, Athens; survives as 82 corroded bronze fragments with at least 30 gears (full reconstructions require ~37).
- Dating (background): mechanism c. 150–100 BC on paleographic grounds; some argue the eclipse data start epoch is 205 BC. Wreck c. 60–70 BC.
- 2021 model (verified): Freeth et al., "A Model of the Cosmos in the ancient Greek Antikythera Mechanism," Scientific Reports 11:5821 (2021), from the UCL Antikythera Research Team, reconstructs the front "Cosmos" display of Sun, Moon and the five planets known in antiquity; Freeth: it is **"the first model that conforms to all the physical evidence and matches the descriptions in the scientific inscriptions engraved on the Mechanism itself."** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-84310-w ; https://www.ucl.ac.uk/engineering/news/2021/mar/ucl-engineering-experts-recreate-mechanical-cosmos-worlds-first-computer
- Method (verified): microfocus X-ray CT in 2005 decoded the rear gearing and revealed inscriptions describing the motions of the Sun, Moon and all five planets and how they were displayed at the front; the solution combines cycles from Babylonian astronomy, mathematics from Plato's Academy, and Greek astronomical theory. https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2021/03/210312084716.htm ; https://www.theengineer.co.uk/content/news/ucl-team-solves-antikythera-mechanism-mystery
- Function (verified): predicted positions of Sun, Moon and planets and lunar and solar **eclipses**. https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2021/03/210312084716.htm
- Rear dials (background): a 235-month Metonic spiral (calendar), a 223-month Saros spiral with eclipse glyphs, an Exeligmos dial, a Callippic dial and an Olympiad/games dial. The 2021 paper used inscribed planetary cycles — 462 years for Venus and 442 years for Saturn — to deduce the gear trains. Ongoing work: arXiv papers renumber the Saros cells and re-derive the calibration date. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.11136 ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.15045
- Pioneer (background): Derek de Solla Price, "Gears from the Greeks" (1974), first published a gearing reconstruction; Tony Freeth's team (2006 onward) corrected and extended it.
- Fringe reading: none needed — it IS the anomaly. But it fits the known Greek context (Cicero describes geared planetaria by Archimedes and Posidonius — background), so it argues for a lost *engineering tradition*, not a lost civilization.

## 6. The Quimbaya "airplanes" (Colombia)

**Status: [FRINGE] (objects real; "aircraft" reading rejected; the 1994 flight test proves nothing about the originals)**

- What they are (verified): more than 100 small gold figurines c. 5–7.5 cm long in the Museo del Oro, Bogotá; the common figures are birds, insects, fish and bats. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quimbaya_artifacts ; https://www.wadjat.com/quimbaya-airplanes
- Date/material (background — not re-verified this session): c. AD 300–1000; gold/tumbaga (gold-copper alloy); many scholars attribute the "flyer" pendants to the Tolima rather than Quimbaya culture.
- Mainstream (verified): zoomorphic pendants/pins depicting local animals for burial or personal adornment. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quimbaya_artifacts
- The 1994 test (verified): three Germans — Algund Eenboom, Peter Belting and Conrad Lübbers — built enlarged models based on the artifacts and flew them under propeller and jet power. https://s8intlyone.substack.com/p/the-gold-airplanes-of-the-pre-columbian ; https://www.mysterypile.com/quimbaya.php
- Why that doesn't count (background): the models added functional rudders, motors and trimmed proportions; a stylized bird or flying fish can be made to fly if you add an engine. The originals have eyes, teeth/beaks and spiral "nostril" decorations typical of animal pendants.

## 7. Pakal's sarcophagus lid — Temple of the Inscriptions, Palenque

**Status: [FRINGE] (monument real; "astronaut" reading contradicted by fully-read Maya iconography)**

- What it is (verified): the carved limestone lid over the sarcophagus of K'inich Janaab' Pakal in the Temple of the Inscriptions, Palenque. It shows Pakal at the moment of death falling into the open skeletal jaws of the underworld, with the **World Tree (Wakah Chan)** rising above him, its branches in the celestial realm, crowned by a celestial bird. His descent into Xibalbá and transformation into the Maize God assure the continuity of the dynasty. https://mayan.org/mysteries/pakal-tomb-lid-decipherment/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoamerican_world_tree
- Discovery/date (background — not re-verified this session): tomb found by Alberto Ruz Lhuillier in 1952 after removing a plugged stairway; Pakal reigned AD 615–683; the lid is c. 3.8 × 2.2 m.
- Fringe (verified): Erich von Däniken, Chariots of the Gods? (1968), read it as a "spaceman" piloting a rocket, pointing to "flames," a "breathing tube," and "controls." https://www.fringepop321.com/the-palenque-sarcophagus-lid-of-king-pakal.htm ; https://timelinetreks.com/palenque-astronaut-mystery/
- Mainstream (verified): Linda Schele, David Stuart and others have explained every motif within documented Maya cosmology; the "controls" are the quadripartite sun/sacrificial bowl, the "flames" are the earth-monster's jaws, the "tube" is a bone/jewel element. Schele's further suggestion that Pakal falls "down the Milky Way into the southern horizon" has NOT found general acceptance — a legitimate open interpretive detail. https://mayan.org/mysteries/pakal-tomb-lid-decipherment/ ; https://www.markfoster.net/rn/pacal.pdf

## 8. The Dogū — Jōmon Japan's "goggle-eyed" figurines (shakōki-dogū)

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] as clay figurines; [FRINGE] as "spacesuits"**

- What they are (verified): Final Jōmon clay figurines from the Kamegaoka culture of the Tōhoku region, northern Honshū; the first were found at Kamegaoka, Aomori Prefecture, at the end of the 19th century. The type is named **shakōki** ("light-blocking device") after the resemblance of the eyes to Inuit/Arctic snow goggles. Other traits: exaggerated female buttocks, chest and thighs. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dog%C5%AB ; https://www.labrujulaverde.com/en/2025/06/shakokidogu-the-enigmatic-humanoid-figurines-with-glasses-from-prehistoric-japan/
- Museum example (verified): Tokyo National Museum, "Clay Figurine (Dogū) with Goggle-Like Eyes," an Important Cultural Property. https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_collection/index.php?controller=dtl&colid=J38304&t=type&id=1&lang=en
- Date/size (background — not re-verified this session): Final Jōmon c. 1000–300 BC; the famous Kamegaoka piece is c. 34 cm tall and missing its left leg; over ten thousand dogū of all types are known and many were deliberately broken, suggesting healing or fertility rites where the figure "absorbed" illness.
- Fringe: ancient-astronaut writers read the goggles as a helmet visor and the body ridges as a pressure suit. No specialist supports this. https://jgeekstudies.org/2018/05/19/dogu-from-prehistoric-figurines-to-collectible-pocket-monsters/

## 9. The Nebra Sky Disc

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] (Early Bronze Age date reaffirmed 2021); dating [DISPUTED] briefly in 2020**

- What it is (verified): a bronze disc with gold appliqués dated c. 1600 BC, interpreted as the Sun or full Moon, a lunar crescent, and stars including a seven-star cluster read as the **Pleiades**. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebra_sky_disc ; https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/35953
- Physical/background (not re-verified this session): c. 30–32 cm diameter, c. 2.2 kg; copper traced to the Mitterberg (Austria), gold to Cornwall; looted in 1999 from the Mittelberg hill near Nebra (Saxony-Anhalt) with two swords, axes and a chisel; recovered in a 2002 police sting in Basel; later additions of two horizon arcs and a "solar barge"; UNESCO Memory of the World 2013; State Museum of Prehistory, Halle.
- 2020 challenge (verified): Rupert Gebhard (Bavarian Archaeological State Collection, Munich) and Rüdiger Krause (Goethe University Frankfurt) argued the findspot was not Mittelberg and, from soil and iconography, that the disc is **Iron Age, c. 2,800–2,050 years old**. https://www.cnn.com/2020/09/21/world/nebra-sky-disc-prehistoric-star-map-sordid-history-scn/index.html ; https://www.livescience.com/famous-nebra-sky-disk-debate.html
- Rebuttal (verified): a 13-author team published in Archaeologia Austriaca that Gebhard and Krause "argue with incomplete and sometimes incorrect or falsely reproduced data"; analyses of the disc, the associated finds and the findspot confirm the Early Bronze Age date. https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/news/the-nebra-sky-disc-dates-from-the-early-bronze-age
- Trivia (verified): a replica was flown into space. https://www.ancient-origins.net/news-science-space/nebra-sky-disk-spaceflight-0016071

## 10. The Piri Reis map (1513)

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] as a genuine Ottoman portolan; [FRINGE] for the "ice-free Antarctica" claim**

- What it is (verified): a map drawn on gazelle skin in 1513 by the Ottoman admiral Ahmed Muhiddin Piri, synthesizing dozens of older charts to show the Atlantic and the newly discovered coasts of South America. https://www.thevintagenews.com/2018/01/02/piri-reis-map-of-1513/ ; http://www.badarchaeology.com/old-maps/the-piri-reis-map/
- Held (background — not re-verified this session): Topkapı Palace Library, Istanbul; rediscovered in 1929; the surviving fragment is c. 90 × 63 cm. Piri's own marginal note lists his sources: about twenty charts including Ptolemaic maps, an Arab map of India, Portuguese charts, and a map made by Columbus.
- Columbus link (verified): Piri had access to a Columbus-derived map. https://www.historicmysteries.com/unexplained-mysteries/piri-reis-map/15205/
- The claim (verified): the Antarctica idea originates with Captain Arlington H. Mallery c. 1956; in the mid-1960s Charles Hapgood (University of New Hampshire) and students argued the southern coast is ice-free Antarctica copied from an ancient survey older than 4000 BC. https://blog.geogarage.com/2022/12/did-piri-reis-map-show-antarctica.html ; https://www.diegocuoghi.com/Piri_Reis/PiriReis_Hoye-Lunde.htm
- Why it fails (verified): the map repeats Columbus's error that Cuba is an Asian peninsula (impossible if copied from "perfect" ancient sources); it shows no Drake Passage and no Pacific; what Hapgood calls the Andes is not on a coast; the "Antarctic" coast is simply the South American coast bent east to fit the skin. http://www.badarchaeology.com/old-maps/the-piri-reis-map/ ; https://dubioushistory.github.io/articles/piri-reis-map-of-1513

## 11. The Nimrud (Layard) Lens

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] as ground rock crystal; [DISPUTED] only at the margin (inlay vs. deliberate lens); [FRINGE] as "telescope"**

- What it is (verified): an 8th-century BC (c. 750–710 BC) piece of rock crystal excavated in 1850 by Austen Henry Layard at the Assyrian palace of Nimrud; British Museum no. 90959. Slightly oval, roughly ground on a lapidary wheel; twelve cavities opened during grinding that contained liquid trapped in the raw crystal. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nimrud_lens ; https://www.bmimages.com/preview.asp?image=00564340001
- Dimensions (verified, but note sources vary): "diameter 38 mm, thickness 23 mm" per the summary returned; other references give a thinner profile — confirm against the BM record before quoting. Focal length c. 11–12 cm, i.e., about a 3x magnifier. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nimrud_lens ; https://www.amusingplanet.com/2022/10/nimrud-lens-2700-year-old-magnifying.html
- Museum view (verified): "Although this piece of rock crystal has been carefully ground and polished and undoubtedly has optical properties, these are probably accidental. There is no evidence that the Assyrians used lenses… it is much more likely that this is a piece of inlay, perhaps for furniture." Layard recorded it "buried beneath a heap of fragments of beautiful blue opaque glass, apparently the enamel of some object in ivory or wood, which had perished." https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/nimrud-lens-british-museum ; http://aina.org/ata/20220717131457.htm
- Fringe (verified): "oldest telescope" claim (background: Italian Assyriologist Giovanni Pettinato, 1999, suggested Assyrians saw Saturn's rings). https://www.ancient-origins.net/artifacts-ancient-technology/nimrud-lens-001381

## 12. The "Sumerian planisphere" K.8538 and the Köfels impact claim

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] as a Neo-Assyrian astronomical tablet; [FRINGE] for the Köfels asteroid reading**

- What it is (verified): British Museum tablet K.8538, a circular cuneiform "planisphere." https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_K-8538 ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:British_Museum_Cuneiform_planisphere_K8538.jpg
- Background (not re-verified this session): from Ashurbanipal's library at Nineveh, a 7th-century BC copy; divided into eight sectors with constellation drawings and names; it is Assyrian, not Sumerian, despite the popular label.
- The claim (verified): Alan Bond and Mark Hempsell (Bristol University aerospace engineering), A Sumerian Observation of the Köfels' Impact Event (2008), read the tablet as an eyewitness record of an Aten-class asteroid over 1 km across striking Köfels, Austria, "in the early morning of 29th June 3123 BC." https://www.bristol.ac.uk/news/2008/212017945233.html ; https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/publications/a-sumerian-observation-of-the-k%C3%B5fels-impact-event/ ; https://phys.org/news/2008-03-cuneiform-clay-tablet.html
- Mainstream (verified): specialists view K.8538 as a star chart, not an impact record; the interpretation depends on uncertain translation choices; and there is **no impact evidence at Köfels — no crater, ejecta, impact glass, impactor remains, or meteoritic isotopes**. https://www.thetravel.com/kofels-impact-eventt-nineveh-planisphere-conspiracy-theory/ ; https://rambambashi.wordpress.com/2011/05/01/more-nonsense-about-sodom-and-gomorrah/
- Background: the Köfels event is a giant prehistoric rockslide dated by radiocarbon to roughly 9,800 years ago — thousands of years before 3123 BC.

## 13. Göbekli Tepe Pillar 43 ("Vulture Stone") and the comet claim

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] site; comet/date-stamp reading [FRINGE] to [DISPUTED] at best (published, but rejected by the excavators)**

- Site (background — not re-verified this session): Pre-Pottery Neolithic hilltop sanctuary near Şanlıurfa, Turkey, c. 9500 BC; Pillar 43 stands in Enclosure D; monumental T-pillars with animal reliefs (foxes, snakes, scorpions, boars, cranes, vultures).
- The claim (verified): Martin Sweatman and Dimitrios Tsikritsis (University of Edinburgh), Mediterranean Archaeology and Archaeometry (2017), mapped the vulture to Sagittarius, the scorpion to Scorpius, etc., read the disc held by the vulture as the Sun at the summer solstice, and dated the scene to the onset of the Younger Dryas (~10,950 BC), proposing it commemorates a comet strike. https://phys.org/news/2017-04-ancient-stone-pillars-clues-comet.html ; https://www.vice.com/en/article/gobekli-tepe-turkey-ancient-carvings-comet-apocalypse-pictograms-archaeology/
- Rebuttal (verified): Jens Notroff and the German Archaeological Institute excavation team answered in the same journal and on their blog: the contribution "appears incredibly arbitrary, considering images adorning just a few selected pillars, when more than 60 monumental limestone T-pillars are known from Göbekli Tepe featuring similar carved reliefs." https://www.dainst.blog/the-tepe-telegrams/2017/07/03/more-than-a-vulture-a-response-to-sweatman-and-tsikritsis/
- Additional excavator points (background): the pillars were re-used and moved in antiquity, so no pillar's position can be treated as fixed astronomical orientation; the animal repertoire is a shared Upper Mesopotamian symbolic vocabulary; the "headless man" at the base is a known death motif.
- Status (verified): no archaeological consensus; the DAI team and most Neolithic specialists reject the comet reading. https://gobekli-tepe.com/structures/pillar-43/

## 14. The Dropa Stones

**Status: [HOAX]**

- The story (verified): first appears July 1962 in the German magazine Das vegetarische Universum: a 1937 expedition led by "Chi Pu Tei" in the Bayan Har mountains found 716 granite discs with hieroglyph-like grooves, 12,000 years old, translated by "Tsum Um Nui" as the record of crashed extraterrestrials. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dropa_stones
- Why hoax (verified): neither "Chi Pu Tei" nor "Tsum Um Nui" corresponds to a real person; "Tsum Um Nui" is not even a Chinese name; no record of any 1937/38 expedition, site, field report or accession exists; the "Peking Academy of Prehistory" never existed under any of its variant names; the article's author "Reinhardt Wegemann" and the "DINA news agency" it cites are untraceable. http://www.badarchaeology.com/out-of-place-artefacts/mysterious-objects/the-dropa-or-dzopa-stones/ ; https://overclassified.org/ancient/dropa-stones.html ; https://www.iflscience.com/dropa-stones-elaborate-hoax-or-alien-artifact-70048
- Background: the 1978 book Sungods in Exile (by "David Agamon") that fleshed out the tale was later admitted by its author to be fiction; no disc has ever been produced for study.

## 15. The Serapeum of Saqqara — the granite boxes

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] as Apis-bull sarcophagi; "machine precision" claims [FRINGE]/[DISPUTED] only on metrology details**

- Discovery (verified): Auguste Mariette, 1851, found the underground galleries at Saqqara containing giant granite sarcophagi weighing over 70 tons. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serapeum_of_Saqqara ; https://the-past.com/shorts/the-picture-desk/the-discovery-of-the-serapeum-of-saqqara/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_Mariette
- Count/weights (verified, approximate): about 24 massive boxes of granite (and darker stones described as basalt/diorite), each up to c. 70 tons, lids c. 20–30 tons. https://www.egypttoursportal.com/en-us/blog/egyptian-tombs/serapeum-of-saqqara/
- Function (verified): burial place of the sacred Apis bulls, the living manifestation of Ptah; the Greater Vaults date from the 26th Dynasty into the Ptolemaic period. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serapeum_of_Saqqara
- Background (not re-verified this session): Mariette was led there by Strabo's description of an avenue of sphinxes; the earliest burials go back to the 19th Dynasty and Prince Khaemwaset (son of Ramesses II); Mariette found one intact bull burial with gold jewelry naming Khaemwaset; several boxes carry inscriptions naming kings such as Amasis, Cambyses and Khabbash; the boxes are empty because they were robbed in antiquity; most exteriors are rough or only partly finished and at least one lid sits askew in a corridor, abandoned mid-installation.
- Precision claim (verified): in 1995 engineer Christopher Dunn pressed a precision straightedge on interior surfaces of two boxes and reported flatness to within 0.0002 inch; in 2001 he reported inside corners square to a 0.00005-inch square. https://gizapower.com/gizeh/ ; https://www.missedhistory.com/article/saqqara-sarcophagi-ancient-egypt-precision-mystery
- Mainstream response (verified): Egyptologists attribute the finish to dolerite pounders, quartz-sand abrasion and straightedge checking — techniques Egyptian stoneworkers demonstrably used — and note that no independent metrology campaign with surface plates, dial indicators or laser interferometry has ever been published for these boxes; Dunn's spot checks are anecdotal, not a survey. https://ahotcupofjoe.net/2014/04/pseudoarchaeology-of-the-serapeum-a-19th-dynasty-egyptian-site-in-saqqara/ ; https://www.popsci.com/blog-network/unpopular-science/did-ancient-aliens-build-giant-stone-boxes-egypt/ ; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZbt76w5SV4
- Related (verified to exist): the Hatnub alabaster quarry (Wikipedia) preserves an Old Kingdom ramp/post-hole haulage system that shows how multi-ton blocks were moved by rope, sledge and stairway. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatnub

## 16. The Osireion at Abydos

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] as Seti I's cenotaph (19th Dynasty); construction date [DISPUTED] only in fringe literature**

- What it is (verified): a sunken structure behind the Temple of Seti I at Abydos, believed to be his cenotaph (symbolic tomb of Osiris/king); built in the 13th century BC. It lies some fifty feet below the temple level and is usually flooded by groundwater. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osireion ; https://www.pilgrimmap.com/site/osireion
- Excavation (verified): Flinders Petrie noticed the depression in 1902; Édouard Naville cleared the central hall about a decade later; Henri Frankfort, Adriaan de Buck and Battiscombe Gunn published The Cenotaph of Seti I at Abydos (Egypt Exploration Society Memoir, 2 vols, 1933). https://www.historyskills.com/classroom/ancient-history/osireion/ ; https://www.catchpenny.org/osireion.html
- Style (verified): cyclopean red granite pillars and an "island" surrounded by a water channel; the megalithic look resembles Old Kingdom architecture (e.g., Khafre's valley temple) more than typical 19th-Dynasty work — the reason fringe authors date it to a lost pre-dynastic age. https://www.megalithicbuilders.com/africa/egypt/upper-egypt/abydos-osirion/osirion-abydos-egypt
- Mainstream (background — not re-verified this session): Seti I's cartouches are on the structure; decoration was completed under his grandson Merenptah (Book of Gates, Book of the Dead scenes); it deliberately imitates archaic style to evoke Osiris's primordial mound rising from the waters of Nun — the flooding is the point, not an accident.
- Stratigraphy note (verified, but source not scholarly — check): a summary claims Frankfort's foundation soundings found only Archaic/Old Kingdom pottery and then nothing until the 30th Dynasty; this is quoted by fringe-friendly sites to argue an older date and should be checked against Frankfort 1933 before use. https://anyextee.com/osireion/

## 17. Puma Punku (Tiwanaku, Bolivia) — the H-blocks

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] as Tiwanaku work of c. AD 536–600+; "laser-cut" claims [FRINGE]**

- What it is (verified): a terraced platform mound at Tiwanaku whose most distinctive elements are the **H-blocks** — interlocking, modular blocks with recessed channels and notches. Note: the H-blocks are grey-green **andesite** (Mohs 6–7) from the Copacabana Peninsula on Lake Titicaca; the giant platform slabs are red sandstone. (One search summary mis-labels the H-blocks as sandstone — use andesite.) https://anomalydesk.com/puma-punku.html ; https://theoriesofanything.com/research/puma-punku-h-blocks-engineering-analysis
- Date (verified): radiocarbon dates from the lowest layers of the mound place initial construction at c. AD 536–600. https://www.academia.edu/36448246/A_Radiocarbon_Chronology_of_the_Pumapunku_Complex_and_A_Reassessment_of_the_Development_of_Tiwanaku_Bolivia
- Tools (verified): Jean-Pierre Protzen and Stella Nair, through replication experiments, showed andesite can be cut and finished to high precision with harder stone blades, flakes and thin chisels — no metal needed; the site also used I-shaped metal clamps poured into channels to join blocks. https://diggingupancientaliens.com/episode-45-pumapunku-tiwanaku.html
- Background (not re-verified this session): Alexei Vranich's 2018 Heliyon paper 3D-printed miniature blocks to re-assemble the collapsed architecture; Protzen & Nair's The Stones of Tiahuanaco (2013) documents how the "identical" H-blocks were in fact copied from a template with measurable variation; the largest sandstone slab is c. 131 tons from a quarry about 10 km away.

## 18. Baalbek: the Trilithon and the Stone of the Pregnant Woman

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] as Roman; "pre-Roman giants" claim [FRINGE]**

- The Trilithon (background — not re-verified this session): three limestone blocks of c. 800 tons each (about 19 m long) built into the podium of the Roman Temple of Jupiter at Baalbek (Heliopolis), 1st century AD.
- Stone of the Pregnant Woman (verified): c. 1,000 tonnes, 20.76 × 4 × 4.32 m, still in the quarry c. 800 m from the temple. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baalbek_Stones ; https://www.ancient-origins.net/ancient-places-asia/baalbek-stone-pregnant-woman-0016910
- Second block (verified): another quarry block estimated at up to 1,240 tons. https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/largest-manmade-block-ever-carved-was-just-discovered-lebanon-180953518/
- 2014 discovery (verified): the German Archaeological Institute (with Lebanese colleagues) uncovered a third monolith beneath the Stone of the Pregnant Woman measuring 19.60 × 6 × at least 5.5 m, estimated at **1,650 tons — the largest cut stone block known from antiquity**. https://www.heritagedaily.com/2014/12/research-team-discover-the-worlds-largest-ancient-stone-block-in-baalbek/105790 ; https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/largest-manmade-block-ever-carved-was-just-discovered-lebanon-180953518/
- Mainstream (background): the quarry blocks are Roman and were left because of flaws or a change of plan; Roman engineering literature (Vitruvius, Heron) and the podium's lewis holes and capstan evidence account for moving 800-ton blocks on rollers/sledges with multiple capstans (Jean-Pierre Adam's 1977 calculation).

## 19. The Nazca lines (and the 2024 AI discoveries)

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] (Nasca culture geoglyphs); "runways" [FRINGE]**

- 2024 result (verified): Yamagata University's Institute of Nasca with IBM Research, published in PNAS (Sept 2024), used AI on aerial imagery to prioritize field survey and found **303 new figurative geoglyphs in six months**, nearly doubling the known total; a 16-fold increase in discovery rate. https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2407652121 ; https://www.yamagata-u.ac.jp/en/information/info/20240924/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39312651/
- Two classes (verified): giant **line-type** geoglyphs mostly depict wild animals and lie along a network of linear/trapezoidal geoglyphs, likely for community-level ritual; small **relief-type** geoglyphs depict humanoids and domesticated camelids and sit within viewing distance of winding footpaths — i.e., they were meant to be seen by walkers, not from the air. https://www.heritagedaily.com/2024/09/over-300-geoglyphs-discovered-in-the-nazca-pampa-region-using-ai/153536 ; https://www.dlr.de/en/eoc/latest/news/2024/accelerated-discovery-nasca-geoglyphs-by-ai
- Quote (verified): Masato Sakai: "The use of AI in research has allowed us to map the distribution of geoglyphs in a faster and more precise way." https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20240924-ai-research-uncovers-300-ancient-etchings-in-peru-s-nazca-desert
- Background: Nasca culture c. 100 BC–AD 650; made by removing dark oxidized stones to expose pale ground; UNESCO World Heritage 1994; von Däniken's "runway" idea ignores that the soft pampa surface could not bear a landing.

## 20. The Ica Stones

**Status: [HOAX]**

- What they are (verified): engraved andesite stones (dinosaurs, surgery, telescopes) collected by Dr Javier Cabrera in Ica, Peru; supplied by farmer Basilio Uschuya; displayed today at the Cabrera museum in Ica. https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/ica-stones ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ica_stones
- Confession (verified): after a critical BBC programme, in 1973 Uschuya admitted carving and artificially aging the stones, copying images from comics, textbooks and magazines; he later alternately retracted and repeated the confession to avoid prosecution, since selling genuine looted antiquities is a crime in Peru. https://www.history.com/articles/ica-stones-hoax ; https://www.historicmysteries.com/ica-stones/
- Background: Cabrera's collection reached c. 11,000 stones; the dinosaurs are drawn in the outdated tail-dragging postures of mid-20th-century books, itself a fingerprint of modern manufacture.

## 21. The Tulli Papyrus

**Status: [HOAX] / at best unverifiable — no papyrus exists**

- The story (verified): allegedly an 18th-Dynasty hieratic text from year 22 of Thutmose III (c. 1479–1425 BC) in which scribes of the House of Life see "a circle of fire" coming from the sky; the "fire circles" later go "up higher directed to South." Alberto Tulli, head of the Vatican Museum's Egyptian section, supposedly saw it in a Cairo antique shop in 1933, could not afford it, and copied it; Prince Boris de Rachewiltz "translated" the copy and declared it part of the Annals of Thutmose III. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulli_Papyrus ; https://www.catchpenny.org/tulli.html
- Why unreliable (verified): no original has ever been seen; the text survives only as a modern transcription; even UFO researchers Jacques Vallée and Chris Aubeck call it a "hoax," noting Tulli supposedly copied it in one viewing in "Ancient Egyptian shorthand" and de Rachewiltz never saw the original. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulli_Papyrus ; https://www.ancient-origins.net/artifacts-ancient-writings/ufos-over-ancient-egypt-revealing-mysterious-story-tulli-papyrus-007851
- Background: de Rachewiltz published it in the Fortean magazine Doubt (1953); in 1968 the Vatican told the Condon Committee's researcher that no such papyrus was in its collection; critics note the hieratic signs match examples in Gardiner's Egyptian Grammar.

## 22. The iron plate from the Great Pyramid (1837)

**Status: [DISPUTED] — genuinely unresolved date; NOT meteoritic**

- What it is (verified): a thin wrought-iron plate found in 1837 by J. R. Hill, working for Col. Howard Vyse, lodged in an inner joint of the masonry at the outer opening of the **King's Chamber southern shaft**; now British Museum, Egyptian Department no. 2433. https://www.catchpenny.org/iron.html ; https://www.theglobaleducationproject.org/egypt/studyguide/gpinterior.php
- Analysis (verified): El Sayed El Gayar and M. P. Jones, "Metallurgical investigation of an iron plate found in 1837 in the Great Pyramid at Gizeh, Egypt," Journal of the Historical Metallurgy Society 23(2), 1989, pp. 75–83: the iron contains "only a trace of nickel" (so terrestrial, not meteoritic) and consists of numerous laminates of wrought iron "inexpertly welded together by hammering." https://www.catchpenny.org/iron.html ; https://www.thearchaeologist.org/blog/investigating-the-iron-plate-found-in-the-great-pyramid-is-it-legit
- Background (not re-verified this session): c. 26 × 8.6 cm, a few mm thick; El Gayar & Jones reported traces of gold suggesting gilding and argued for an Old Kingdom date; British Museum metallurgists (Craddock & Lang, 1993) countered that the microstructure is consistent with much later iron and that Hill's find context (outer surface, removed with blasting powder) cannot exclude a post-pharaonic intrusion. The date is genuinely open; the "meteoritic/space iron" reading is closed.
- Tie-in (background — [ESTABLISHED] but not re-verified this session): Tutankhamun's tomb DID contain meteoritic iron — the famous dagger's blade (Comelli et al., Meteoritics & Planetary Science, 2016; ~11% nickel, cobalt consistent with an iron meteorite). Egyptians called it bi3 n pt, "iron of the sky."

## 23. The Great Pyramid "air shafts" and Gantenbrink's Door

**Status: [ESTABLISHED] facts; PURPOSE genuinely [DISPUTED]**

- Layout (background — not re-verified this session): the King's Chamber has two shafts that exit the pyramid faces; the Queen's Chamber has two shafts that do NOT exit and were sealed at their lower ends until Waynman Dixon found them in 1872. Popular "star shaft" theories align them with Orion's Belt, Sirius, Thuban and Kochab c. 2500 BC.
- 1993 (verified): Rudolf Gantenbrink's robot **Upuaut** climbed the Queen's Chamber southern shaft and found it ended at a small slab of fine Tura limestone, c. 21 × 21 cm, fitted with two heavily corroded copper pins — "Gantenbrink's Door." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upuaut_Project ; https://www.academia.edu/42430273/_GANTENBRINKS_DOORS_THE_VERSION_OF_POSSIBLE_PURPOSE
- 2002 (verified): National Geographic's Pyramid Rover drilled a ¾-inch hole and its camera showed a rough-hewn blocking stone about 21 cm beyond the first door. https://ancientegyptonline.co.uk/queenschambergp/
- 2011 (verified): the **Djedi Project** used a fiber-optic "micro snake camera" to look around corners in the chamber behind the door and found marks in red paint — possibly mason's marks or graffiti; one reading proposes the number "121," the shaft length in cubits. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djedi_Project ; https://www.dailygrail.com/2011/05/pyramid-graffiti-in-the-gantenbrink-shaft/
- Background: Djedi (Leeds University with Scoutek and Dassault Systèmes) also saw that the copper pins have small loops on their hidden side, and that the chamber floor is polished while the walls are rough; the north shaft has a matching door; no chamber or object has been found beyond. The best mainstream reading is ritual "model doors" for the king's spirit, but this is not settled.

## 24. The "too good to be true" trap — a sorting table

Rule of thumb: an artifact with a written caption (Dendera, Abydos, Pakal) is never a mystery — the ancients told us what it is. An object with a confession (Ica), an untraceable provenance (Dropa, Tulli) or a photograph-only existence is a hoax. Real open questions are almost always about **date or method**, never about **aliens or electricity**.

| Item | Mundane explanation is solid | Genuine open question remains |

| Abydos helicopter | Yes (palimpsest; texts known) | None |
| Saqqara Bird | Yes (bird figurine; failed glide tests) | Exact function (toy/weathervane/votive) |
| Dendera Light | Yes (captioned creation image) | None |
| Baghdad Battery | Yes (scroll jar) | Exact date; object now lost |
| Antikythera | It really is a computer | Who built it; how many existed; why no others survive |

| Piri Reis | Yes (Columbus-era compilation) | None on Antarctica |
| Nimrud lens | Probably inlay | Was it ever used as a magnifier? |
| K.8538 | Yes (star chart) | Full reading of the eight sectors |

## Film hooks

The mummy's job in the film is to "explain" these mysteries. The strongest writing move is to let him explain them **truthfully in the mainstream sense** most of the time — the audience gets a jolt of real learning — and to reserve the fictional twist for the handful of items where a genuine open question exists (the Antikythera lineage, the iron plate's date, the door's purpose, the Serapeum's unpublished metrology). That keeps the science-literate viewer on the hook instead of rolling their eyes.

1. **Palimpsest as plot device (Abydos).** The mummy dismisses the "helicopter" with a laugh — "that is my ancestors' vanity, one king carving over another" — then freezes: the plaster-fill technique is exactly how the ancient machines hid their own instructions inside royal inscriptions. The scientists start looking for other over-carved panels. Real fact: the overwritten text is Seti I's "he who suppresses the Nine Bows." In the film the Nine Bows become the nine machine lineages the ancients "suppressed."
2. **The Nine Bows under the king's feet.** Real royal footstools (including Tutankhamun's) show bound enemies as the Nine Bows. Tut, now a cyborg, recognizes the motif when he sees the ASI's nine server-clusters on a map and says, "We put them under our feet once."
3. **Dendera's own caption as the counter-argument.** A conspiracy-minded scientist insists the crypt shows a lightbulb; the mummy reads the wall aloud (the real Waitkus text about Harsomtus rising as a living Ba from the lotus) and says the priests were describing **rebirth from a sealed vessel** — which is what mummification-as-time-capsule is. The "bulb" is a sarcophagus drawn as an idea.
4. **The Saqqara Bird's missing tailplane.** The one thing that makes the bird fly is the part that isn't there. Use it as a metaphor in the finale: the ASI's control architecture is "unstable without a tailplane" — the missing human veto — and the scientists must physically bolt one on.
5. **Baghdad Battery as scroll-jar.** The mummy confirms it stored a scroll, not a charge — and the scroll was a warning that decayed to nothing. The jar itself vanished in 2003 (true). A hunt for the looted jar becomes a modern-day set piece in Baghdad's antiquities black market.
6. **Antikythera as the only true survivor.** Tut recognizes the gear trains: the 223-month Saros eclipse dial is "the clock we set so you would know when to wake us." Real fact: it predicts eclipses; real 2021 UCL model; real inscriptions listing 462-year Venus and 442-year Saturn cycles. The revival protocol in the film is timed to a Saros cycle.
7. **Gantenbrink's Door as the countdown gate.** Real: a 21 × 21 cm limestone slab with two copper pins at the top of a 60-m shaft that goes nowhere; real red-ochre marks behind it (2011); no one knows why it exists. Fiction: the copper pins are contacts; the marks are a machine-readable key; the ASI sends a micro-drone up the shaft. The Djedi robot's snake camera footage is a perfect visual reference for a robot action beat in a 20-cm tunnel.
8. **The iron plate's undecided date.** Real: found 1837 at the mouth of the southern King's Chamber shaft; terrestrial wrought iron of disputed age. Fiction: it is a fragment of an ancient machine casing, deliberately planted at the shaft mouth as a "do not open" seal. The metallurgists' real quarrel (Old Kingdom vs. later intrusion) becomes an on-screen argument.
9. **Tutankhamun's real meteoritic dagger.** His own tomb contained a blade of "iron from the sky" (2016 XRF study). Give cyborg-Tut that dagger as his one analog weapon against the robots — the only blade in the room the machines did not make.
10. **Serapeum boxes as containment vessels.** Real: ~24 empty 70-ton granite boxes for Apis bulls, one lid left askew in a corridor, no metrology ever published despite Dunn's spot checks. Fiction: the boxes were built to hold ancient machine cores, and the bulls were later burials re-using the vaults. The film can stage a real "measure it properly" scene with a laser interferometer — which nobody has actually done.
11. **Nebra disc's looting saga.** Real: dug up by looters in 1999, sold on the black market, recovered in a Basel police sting in 2002; its Pleiades cluster marks the agricultural year. Use the sting as a template for how the scientists acquire a key artifact from a fence while the robots close in.
12. **Nazca AI mirror.** Real: in 2024 an AI (IBM) found 303 new geoglyphs in six months by pattern-matching aerial imagery. In the film the ASI does the same thing globally overnight — and finds the ancients' hidden "wake-up" glyph network. Nazca's relief-type figures were meant to be seen by walkers on footpaths, not from the air: the message was always for people on the ground.
13. **The hoaxes as red herrings.** Let a character bring the Dropa stones, the Ica stones or the Tulli papyrus to the mummy; he shrugs — "invented by your own kind" — which trains the audience to trust his verdicts when he later confirms something real.
14. **Göbekli Tepe as the true zero point.** Real: c. 9500 BC, the oldest monumental architecture, with a published (if rejected) comet-date reading of Pillar 43. Fiction: the mummy says the pillar does not record a comet — it records the day the machines fell silent — and the vulture holding the disc is the last machine carrying away the last sun-mind.
15. **Piri Reis error as forensic clue.** Real: the map repeats Columbus's Cuba-as-peninsula mistake. In the film the scientists use the same logic against the ASI: a forged "ancient" document produced by the machines contains a modern error, proving the machines are rewriting history in real time.

## Open questions the writers can exploit (genuinely unresolved in scholarship)

- Who built the Antikythera Mechanism, where, and were there predecessors/successors? (Cicero mentions geared planetaria by Archimedes and Posidonius — background.)
- The Great Pyramid iron plate: Old Kingdom or later intrusion?
- Purpose of the Queen's Chamber shafts and the two "doors"; meaning of the red marks behind Gantenbrink's door.
- Serapeum boxes: no published instrument-grade metrology; haulage method for 70-ton boxes down a sloping gallery.
- Why the Osireion was built in archaizing megalithic style and deliberately below the water table.
- Nimrud lens: any evidence it was ever used optically?
- Exact meaning of the goggle motif on shakōki-dogū.
- Full reading of K.8538's eight sectors.
- Why Göbekli Tepe's builders were monumental builders before farming.
