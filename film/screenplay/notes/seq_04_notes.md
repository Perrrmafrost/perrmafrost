## Deviations and interpretations (and why)
1. **The second of Tut's two sentences.** The bible gives only "It has happened before." I wrote the pair as **"It has happened — [mic dies] — before. / Do not let it help you."** The mic dies mid-sentence, so the plan fails live. The second sentence, heard only by the first row, is paid off at once when the shabti serve water. If the Seq 3 writer scripted a different second sentence, swap it in; the structure holds either way.
2. **The Reis returns here, refitted with the jackal-profile sensor mast.** *(Corrected in the audit: Seq 3 introduced it in the plant room with no mast, so this is not its first appearance.)* Bible §3.2 says it "hunts them from GEM Night" and is "refitted after Seq 3". I read that as a refit after SESHAT reads the First Time archive in Seq 3. Tut: "It has been reading." I left out the "because it works better" line so a later writer can place it. The Reis is **the shabti that steps in front of the guest and takes the round** (bible 4.3), so the "following procedure" exchange lands on the recurring antagonist. Its damage this encounter: **a crack across the embossed star on its chest**.
3. **Red Beer staging.** Adaeze jacks her laptop into a building-management panel at the foot of the service stair, so that the nine seconds are spent crossing the atrium floor, past Layla. That is the only staging in which Nour can reach her daughter and still escape. The shabti reaching gently for her wrist echoes Rami's broken fingers.
4. **Summons plant.** Tut recites BD 6 (Faulkner, abridged with an ellipsis) in Middle Egyptian and gives the spell's own task, "Convey sand from east to west" (Faulkner's wording). The shabti turns west toward a blank wall for two seconds before "Disregard". Afterwards it politely herds them *down* toward the atrium, which is the way they need to go.
5. **The jackal corridor kill.** Geography: two unnamed RIFLEMEN on point drop in the service corridor. None of the named pack (Hassan, Mina, Youssef, Karim) dies here. A jackal then slips out into the atrium; Karim fires at it and the round goes wide toward a sleeping guest, which the Reis blocks.
6. **Sameh (Layla's father)** stays unseen: a hand waving from the table beneath the Ramesses. Layla speaks Egyptian Arabic to Nour (bible §13).
7. **Fathi's du'a** is not repeated here, because Seq 5 already stages one for Hassan. He gets "Ya Malik" and the engineer's practical moves (pry bar, manual chain hoist).
8. **The Heavenly Cow quotation** is spoken by Nour from memory in Budge's wording, not read off the shrine, because research does not confirm which passage survives on the shrine's partial copy.
9. **The route** from the Conservation Centre tunnel to the Khufu boat hall to the loading dock follows the bible. The real site layout is not documented (research 14 §5), so it stays fictional.
