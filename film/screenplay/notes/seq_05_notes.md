## Deviations, additions and flags for the lead
- **Beat order within beat 2:** the GPS gag comes before the wall. The bible lists it after the pods re-form, but it plays better as the release before the first impact. The forged order comes last, as in the bible.
- **Added micro-beat: the jackal boards the truck and Tut shields Nour.** It comes straight from the pre-midpoint rule "Units never harm the witness. They remove everyone around him." (§3.1). The jackal's line of fire passes over Tut and settles on Nour. Tut puts himself in front, and the jackal holds, recalculating, which gives Fathi his second. This sets up the midpoint flip (Seq 7), when the rule is withdrawn, and is echoed by Tomas stepping between a jackal and Nour in Seq 12. Tut: "It wants me whole." (He knows from the lure in Seq 3 that the machine wants to be weighed; he is not yet saying why.)
- **Added: SESHAT sends Nour the approved Layla image as a lure** ("The doors are open to you. Whenever you like."). This is SESHAT's strip-the-companions stance before the midpoint. It uses the **one approved master image** as a COMP on a phone screen, as §3.3 allows ("reused as a composite on every tablet"). No robot is in frame. The image is described as "soft white light", in the Garden palette. The emotional payoff comes when Nour drowns the phone ("She is not in there" / "I'm coming back for you"), which echoes her Seq 4 line "I'll come back for you." **Lead: please confirm that the approved image reads acceptably as night-time Garden light.**
- **Plant for Seq 6 (dramatic irony).** The heroes believe the phones are the leak. Tut's nape port is the real one: he is "loud", he hears "them all talking about us", and at the dock his hand drifts to his neck as the phones sink. Seq 6's reveal that "SESHAT is listening through Tut" lands on this. I have not pre-empted Adaeze's realisation or the line "It isn't chasing us. It's following us." Tarek's line in this sequence is tactical and different: "They're not stopping us. They're steering us."
- **Tarek's one joke for Act II is spent here:** "Then we're making good time." (after the GPS says they are in the river). **Writers of Seq 6–9, please do not give Tarek another joke.**
- **Tarek: "I watched him drink the water."** This implies the Defence Minister was a guest at the GEM gala (Seq 4). The bible's gala host is "a fictional unnamed MINISTER" and does not say which ministry, so either the host is the defence minister or he was a guest in the wides. **Seq 4 writer / lead: align.** The form stays within the §6 state beat: the order is forged, the minister is fictional, SCAF is untouched.
- **"And it said please"** (Fathi) calls back to Tarek's Seq 4 line "The ministry has never said please."
- **Census tick:** the dock radio gives 23%, one point up from the truck. This is a small hourglass on the way to the "sixty-one percent" in §5.
- **The army truck's radio** is disabled (Tarek rips out the handset cord). The **launch has its own analog police radio**, untouched, which Seq 6 needs for SESHAT's broadcasts.
- **Hassan's body** stays in the truck cab at the dock, door closed, after a private du'a from Fathi (palms raised, lips moving; no salat). Tarek carries **Hassan's ID disc** (changed from "ID card" in the audit, to match Seq 10–11). This is optional texture for Seq 11 (Tarek's death) and for SESHAT naming "Hassan…" in Seq 12.
- **Language:** Tarek–Hassan and Tarek counting his men are in Egyptian Arabic. Tut–Nour private lines are in Late Egyptian. Nour to Layla's image is in Egyptian Arabic. Tut switches to English for "They own the sky…" so that the whole group hears it.

## [[verify]] items
1. **"Your things lived there a hundred years"** (Nour, of the Egyptian Museum, Tahrir). This is a round figure for Tut's objects being displayed at Tahrir from the 1920s until the GEM transfer. It is marked in the Fountain file. Research 14 §3 confirms the museum and its lit façade (the 2020–21 Tahrir dressing). 2. **Nile police launch:** the bible specifies it. The unit that runs Nile police boats (the Interior Ministry's river or water-surface police) is not in the research. The script keeps the launch generic and unnamed, with no markings.
3. **Route:** GEM → a flyover → the Giza-side Nile Corniche. It is deliberately unnamed and uses no real bridge names, so there is nothing to verify. If production wants a named road, the Ring Road (research 14 §3) or the 6th October Bridge (the 20.5 km elevated causeway) are the documented options.
