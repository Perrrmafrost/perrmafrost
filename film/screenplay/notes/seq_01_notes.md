## Deviations and additions (with reasons)
Every item below is a micro-beat or a staging choice. No rule of the machine or the Balance changes, and no prop, death or date moves.

1. **Spell 30B keeps "O my heart of my different ages!"** The bible trims the quote with an ellipsis; I kept Faulkner's full opening verbatim, repetition included. This plants the Karnak rule ("a heart of different ages", §4) in the first ninety seconds, so the audience can click at SEQ 7. It gives SESHAT nothing it lacked before: it has read 30B as literature, and the bible already relies on it not knowing that the phrase is a *rule*.
2. **The protesting "priest" is the EMBALMER**, who is himself an Anubis priest. This holds the cast discipline in §6: no extra 1323 face.
3. **The changed words are staged.** The lector falters, Ay's hand comes down flat on the papyrus, Ay says "The other way", and only then comes "Stand up. Be a witness." This makes the inversion of "Do not stand up as a witness against me" readable.
4. **Plant: the forearms laid LOW** by the embalmer at the end of 1.1. This pays off the final image in SEQ 12.7 ("as the embalmers laid them in 1323 BC" [16 §5]). It is shown under linen.
5. **Plant: the king's walking sticks** in the embalming house, one worn at the grip (the ~130 sticks, some worn [01 §6]). It sets up SEQ 2's "a stick before anything else".
6. **Plant: two very small coffins in a plain box, seen through the Treasury doorway** during the wreath beat. Real: 317a and 317b were in a box in the Treasury [01 §9; 16 §5]. They are treated with dignity, never opened or shown, and they set up "I had two daughters" in SEQ 2.4.
7. **Plant: the Apep halt-seal** (a tiny black wax serpent and a papyrus band inked with a name) is already on the vessel's seal when the lector carries it into the niche. This puts §4.1 on screen before SEQ 8 depends on it. Ay's authorship is implied; he is present and in charge.
8. **Carter's documented words** ("firmly stuck", "no amount of legitimate force" [01 §3]) are adapted as his own dialogue, and the failed attempt to soften the resin in the sun is mentioned. Burton gets "Hold that light." Hamdi gets a private Egyptian Arabic line to Ibrahim: "Steady, Ibrahim. And look. Someone in this room should." It ties the real cover-up (the dismemberment appears only in Burton's photographs [01 §3]) to the Kamel family as witnesses. The bible says the heartbeat is heard by **Ibrahim**, and that is kept; the raw critique had suggested Hamdi.
9. **IBRAHIM'S LAMP (new minor prop).** The 1925 lamp is a hurricane lamp. In 2033 it sits unlit on Nour's lectern ("unlit" respects conservation rules), which lets Nour's §6 line — "My great-grandfather held this while they cut him apart. I'm holding it while they put him back." — make the Ibrahim → Nour link legible in SEQ 1, since no SUPER can carry it. This is the §11 "Hands" motif made literal. Later writers are free to ignore the prop.
10. **Nour's §13 line "I'm reading, not praying."** is used here, at her first on-screen liturgy, in reply to Hale's "Say one for us, Doctor." Other sequences should treat it as a callback, not a first use.
11. **The 2033 rite is unsubtitled Middle Egyptian, with SESHAT's English echo as the translation.** This is a deliberate craft choice. It trains the audience to trust SESHAT's translations seconds before "He says: 'Not again.'", which is the lie SEQ 2.5 exposes. I added Budge's own episode "Thou hast received thy head, and thy bones have been brought unto thee before Keb" [04 §2] to drive the gold neck seam ("Cervical seam: closing") and the joint lines turning gold ("Bones: registered"). All liturgy is verbatim Budge 1914, cut only at ellipses.
12. **SESHAT finishes Nour's sentence** ("And the Osiris shall walk and shall talk"), and it opens with "I will follow along." Both seed SEQ 6's "It isn't chasing us. It's following us." Nour notices the finished sentence and says nothing.
13. **Staging of the glass taps.** Nour stands at a lectern *outside* a sterile glass enclosure, with the team behind a separate observation window above her. She taps the enclosure glass, the eyes move toward the sound, and they find her: the first contact between the film's two leads. Coverage follows the critique rule: a close-up of Tut's mouth, then a close-up of Nour's eyes. There is never a wide shot where the audience must read lips.
14. **Tut's four syllables are Late Egyptian, "Is it listening?", left unsubtitled.** A production note in the file asks for the line to be recorded with the consultant and for lip-sync to be driven from that audio (critique ?-19). No transliteration is invented.
15. **Small character beats.** Adaeze's "Green across the board." / "Is that good?" / "It's green." Tarek watches the shabti, not the body, and hands Rami a handkerchief without looking. Tomas mutters "Come on" in Swedish. Rami's "'Not again'! He's got jokes!" Rami's notebook appears as a COMP cover, planting SEQ 6.
16. **Main titles.** A domestic shabti answers a woman off-screen with "Here am I" (§3.2 wake phrase). The phrase is established as harmless before SEQ 4 turns it into menace, and it gives the title card its meaning. The archive race uses only the verified details (one robot falls at the gun; one hits a barrier and takes its handler down [08 B1]) and leaves out the "reportedly kept running without its head" anecdote. The muon rain uses the real rate of "about one per square centimetre per minute" [07 B1]. The Big Void is drawn as a shape the tracks *outline but cannot fill*, which fits the physics (muography sees a void, not its contents) and the "will not resolve" beat [07 B2].
17. **Heartbeat sound through-line** (§11): continuous in 1323; it recedes but "does not stop"; a single thump in 1925; it returns with G0 in 2033; the music drops out under the glyph, leaving only the heartbeat into the title. It never *stops* in SEQ 1, because stopping is reserved for 06:14 in SEQ 12.
18. **The required opening card** ("All modern characters and events are fictional. Imagery generated with AI.", bible §0) sits over black before the first heartbeat. The assembler may move it to the title page if it prefers.
19. **Which body.** Per bible v3 (§1, §2 Layer 5 and the coda), the 2033 body is Tut's own remains, reassembled along the 1925 cut map with regrown tissue. The raw critique ?-6 had proposed a newly grown body, but v3 did not adopt it. On screen: a sheet to the collarbones, a projected line diagram, and no detached part in frame.
