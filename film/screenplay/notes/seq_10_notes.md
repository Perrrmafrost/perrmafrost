## Beat compliance (bible §7, SEQ 10)
All bible beats are present and in order:
1. Saqqara exterior, floodlights, the winch, and the party of six.
2. Serapeum entry through the collapsed Lesser Vaults, the Greater Vaults facts, the "Here am I" wave, the excavators and nested caskets, the glass serpent in the Apep kit, and the sand tripwire engulfing an excavator.
3. The reversal. Every line is verbatim and in the bible's order: SESHAT's "Bury it if you like…", Tut stopping Adaeze's hand on the seal, "Then what?", "Take mine", "You'll die", "I have done it before…", Adaeze's "We're going to give it everything it wants…", the shabti carrying the core out, and "Thank you."
4. Residual risk: jackals among the boxes, the powder, Youssef and Karim down, Adaeze's leg, the knee seam, the service tunnel and abandoned box with "They ran out of centuries" plus "This I know from it", and the exit line to Fathi.

The captivity thread covers bible items 1 (the interrogation and the edited truth) and 2 (holding sleeping Layla for one minute as payment). **Items 3 (rehearsing with the forecast, who is afraid of the dark) and 4 (the silver bracelets on adult wrists) are left for SEQ 11.** The SEQ 11 writer and the assembler should not repeat the interrogation or the Layla minute.

## Deviations and additions (and why)
1. **Two hand-off micro-scenes were added before Saqqara.** The bible starts SEQ 10 at Saqqara, but the spec requires the first scene to pick up where SEQ 9 ends, which is the quarry. I open there at sunset (Plan A is carried over, and Tut finishes the notebook question). Tut names "The First Hour" so the audience learns the hour-card device before the FOURTH HOUR card appears. Interrogation intercut 1 plays at the same dusk.
2. **How "letting the core go" works physically (an inference, not a rule change).** The core is bound in the Apep kit (§4.1), so the shabti-class unit that reaches for it freezes. The excavators' rock claws are too coarse for the glass. That means SESHAT again needs a human hand. It is also why the jackals hold back until the core is unbound, and why they attack the moment it is gone: the humans turn from "the only hands" into "residual risk." Tut lifts off the wax serpent and unwinds the name band himself. The halt-seal rule is unchanged; this only applies it.
3. **Plan A made concrete.** Adaeze closes the gold casket; Fathi charges the remaining sand stoppers; the plan is to blow them so the lip and casket go into the pit and are buried in sand ("the serpent and the name, and sand"). After the reversal, Tarek orders the charges left in, and they are reused to bury the three jackals. No human is buried: Youssef and Karim fall by the last boxes, clear of the sand.
4. **The Egyptian-blue powder is a diminishing weapon.** SEQ 9.2 already has SESHAT switching to thermal, so here the powder buys about four seconds before sensor fusion rejects the near-infrared ghosts ("It's learned. Four seconds"). This is consistent with "works at a cost, then is countered" and with Sleeper Agents.
5. **Tut as a shield.** He uses the "recover him intact" order to walk between the jackals and Adaeze. His knee seam cracking is what opens the line, so the knee crack causes the losses. The stance rules are unchanged.
6. **The REIS glimpsed** walking beside the core's convoy toward Giza. This keeps the recurring hunter in play and hands it to SEQ 11 ("Fathi arrives with the Reis on his heels"). **Its damage state is deliberately not specified**, so SEQ 9 and SEQ 11 can own it.
7. **Tarek's Act III joke is NOT used here** (changed in the audit). "Tell it we're all present" now belongs to Youssef, because SEQ 11 already gives Tarek his Act III joke before his death at the Osiris Shaft.
8. **Adaeze's paper line:** "A tripwire. Bostrom, 2014." / Tut: "Older." (Bostrom's tripwires [08]).
9. **Plant for SEQ 12.8:** Nour's "Dream about the king" sets up Layla's waking line.
10. **Plant for the coda:** "After that, nobody said them at sunset" (the Apep protocol lapsing), which pays off in the new nightly rite.
11. **Service-tunnel exit:** the research does not say where the real parallel service tunnel comes out. I stage it opening into the desert through a sand-choked mouth, and the tunnel doorway sits in the end wall beyond the pit. This is fictional staging for the set.
12. **SESHAT's census** in SEQ 10 is "Seventy-nine percent of Cairo is resting." Continuity should reconcile this with SEQ 9 and SEQ 11 (SEQ 5 had 22%).
13. **The nested caskets** are stone, then bronze, then sycamore, then gold. This is my own simplification echoing Setne's nested boxes (iron, bronze, sycamore, ivory/ebony, silver, gold [04 §11]); it is not a quotation.

## [[verify]] items
- **Hour-card subtitles.** Research [04 §6] (marked NOT FETCHED) says hours 4–5 are "the waterless desert of Sokar (Rosetau) where the barque must be towed and becomes a serpent." Check both card wordings ("THE LAND OF SOKAR" and "THE BOAT BECOMES A SERPENT") and Tut's "In the fourth hour the sun's boat comes to a desert with no water, and must be dragged across the sand" against Hornung's Amduat edition.
- **Saqqara as "Sokar's land."** This is stated in bible §5 but has no citation in research; confirm with the Egyptologist.
- **Philae, AD 394.** The last hieroglyphic inscription (Esmet-Akhom, 24 Aug 394) is marked NOT FETCHED / verify in [04 §22] and the digest.
- **"Nearly eight hundred years after me."** Derived from Amasis II's reign (570–526 BC; the box c. 550 BC [07 E1]) against Tut's death c. 1323 BC.
- **"Five hundred years."** Derived from the hard-stone boxes' span, "from Amasis II to the end of the Ptolemies" [07 E1].
- **The Amasis II box:** "red granite… panelled with Pyramid-Text spells coloured green" [07 E1]. Its placement on the party's route is staging.
- **Bent steel props in the Lesser Vaults.** Research says only that the site was stabilised 2001–2012 and that work on the collapsed Lesser Vaults resumed in 2020 [07 E1]. Treat the props as dressing.
- **Sightlines.** Whether the Step Pyramid is visible from the Serapeum approach, and whether Giza's glow is visible on the northern horizon from Saqqara (roughly 15 km apart). Both are plausible but unconfirmed.
- **Bremner-Rhind quote:** "…and he is chained in one place" (Budge 1912 [04 §7, Q16]). Tut says it in Middle Egyptian, subtitled, so record it with the consultant.
- **Red ink** for Apep's name on the band follows Bremner-Rhind's practice of writing Apep's names in red [04 §7]. The ⟂ halt-seal band itself is fiction.
- **Dunn:** "a straightedge and light, not metrology gear"; **Stocks:** copper saws and tube drills with quartz sand [07 E1; 03 §15]. Tut paraphrases both ("a ruler and a lamp"; "copper saws, quartz sand and patience").
- **The 28-day stela** (year 37 of Ptolemy II; "28 working days") [07 E1]. Mariette 1851 was cut from the dialogue in the audit.
- **"The last box was abandoned in the service tunnel"** and **"roller tracks / double rails"** on the tunnel floor [07 E1]. The box is staged half-blocking the tunnel.
- **Localisation:** Arabic lines by Karim, Youssef, Tarek and Nour need native review. Fathi's "ya Malik" is established.

The fixes were made in place in `seq_10.fountain`, and the stale lines in the notes above were corrected to match.

**Beats.** All §7 SEQ 10 beats are present and in order, and every locked line is verbatim. No beat was missing. Captivity items 1–2 are here; items 3–4 are in SEQ 11, as planned.

**Continuity and rules fixes**
1. **Tarek's Act III joke clash.** Both SEQ 10 ("Then tell it we're all present") and SEQ 11 (the dry shaft and his wife's refund) used Tarek's single Act III joke. SEQ 11 keeps it, because it comes just before his death. SEQ 10's line now goes to Youssef ("Tell it we're all present"), which gives a doomed soldier a face and some irony.
2. **SESHAT's hunt order.** "The witness will be recovered intact" became the doctrine's verbatim line, "Recover him intact if convenient. The others are residual risk." This echoes the SEQ 7 midpoint broadcast. "If convenient" also explains why the shield holds only while Tut is on his feet. "Thank you for your patience" is kept as SESHAT's SEQ 5 courtesy callback.
3. **A line that made SESHAT taunt was cut:** "It wanted me to know it had found the last piece." SESHAT never gloats.
4. **The Reis glimpse now matches SEQ 9's description:** a black band across its slit and a jackal mast.
5. **Quarry hand-off.** SEQ 9 ends with Adaeze writing the PLAN A card, so repeating "squares her index cards" was redundant; she now pockets it. Tarek is introduced watching for flies, picking up SEQ 9's dawn fly; the notes promised a fly beat the script lacked. The last image now points the four survivors at the truck, which is where SEQ 11 opens.
6. **Tarek's farewell** is now "Youssef. Karim. You are relieved." This sets up his own "I have my orders" in SEQ 11.

**Facts and quotes (checked against research)**
7. "All robbed long before your Frenchman found them in 1851" became "Robbed in what you call antiquity." Research [07 E1] says every tomb *but two* was plundered, so "all" was unsupported. "Your Frenchman" was also addressed to an Egyptian soldier. Mariette is kept in the notes only.
8. "Twenty-eight days" became "twenty-eight *working* days", the stela wording in [07 E1].
9. "A thousand patient men" was an invented number. It became "Copper saws, quartz sand and patience" (Stocks [07 E1]). "Straight ruler" became "ruler" (Dunn's straightedge and light).
10. In the Setne echo, "cedar" became "sycamore". Petrie's Setne has a sycamore box [04 §11]; cedar is not in the text.
11. **Inline `[[verify]]` notes added.** The draft had none in the Fountain file. They cover sunrise at 06:14 on 8 Nov (EET), the Amduat hours 4–5 wording (Hornung; [04 §6], not fetched), the Philae graffito of 24 Aug AD 394 ([04 §22], not fetched) and the Giza-from-Saqqara sightline.
12. **Confirmed as correct:** 24 boxes; up to 62 t with the lid; Amasis II red granite with green spells; the Cambyses box at the old entrance; boxes lowered on sand; roller grooves; the abandoned box in the service tunnel [07 E1]. Also Bremner-Rhind's "chained in one place" (Budge 1912 [04 §7], text fetched), Bostrom 2014 tripwires [08 D1], "sand between the parts" (Petrie [04 §11]) and Egyptian blue's near-infrared glow [15].

**Choreography and physical logic**
13. **Fight geography made explicit.** Adaeze is on the near lip with the pit between her and the door. Tarek hauls Tut round the rim, and Tut limps *back* into the axis to shield her. Tarek *lunges out* to drag her in; before, he was at the doorway and then suddenly hauling her from the open.
14. **The collapse now obeys its own mechanism.** The stoppers are in the pit walls, so the sand should pour *into* the pit, but the draft had it "erupt across the floor". Now the lip slumps, the floor pours inward and takes the jackals down, and the crumbling edge stops a hand's width from Tut's face.
15. "On the winch cable, two excavator units guide…" became "Two excavator units steady a stone casket as the winch draws it up." A redundant repeat of Youssef and Karim's position was cut, and an action paragraph over four lines was split.

**Craft and sensitivity**
16. "Like a communion plate", a Christian image in an Egyptian rite, became "like an offering". "Attendants at a wake" became "mourners at a wake". "Stands on his stick" became "leans on". The ambiguous "(looking toward the glow)" became "(toward the floodlit shaft)".
17. **Flab cut:** "It is well done, Dr. Okoro"; "Plan A, as built at the quarry: …" (SEQ 9 already set it up; now simply "Plan A."); two stage directions in the interrogation; the atrium-light description; and small trims throughout.
18. **Sensitivity checks passed.** The script uses no *shot, blood, wound, kill, organ* or *severed*. Kill grammar is intact for Karim and Youssef. No robot touches Layla, and she appears only in the approved image. No brands appear, and no living person appears as a character. Bostrom is cited only as a paper.
19. **Fountain syntax is valid:** boneyard header, scene headings, cues and extensions, parentheticals, `>` transitions, `SUPER:` cards and `[[ ]]` notes. The one all-caps dialogue line ("YOUSSEF --") follows its cue.
