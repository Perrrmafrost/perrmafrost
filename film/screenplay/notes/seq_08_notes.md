## Real facts used (with source)
- Carter's journal: Carnarvon "Can you see anything?" / "Yes, it is wonderful." [01 §2, Q1]. The book's "Wonderful things" is saved for Seq 12.
- KV9 (Ramesses VI) "was built over the top of" KV62 [13 §3].
- The burial chamber north wall: three scenes read right to left; Ay in the sem-priest's leopard skin performs the Opening of the Mouth on the mummiform king; Nut; the king with his ka embraced by Osiris; the only royal tomb showing the rite on the king [04 §2; 09 §5.2].
- Quartzite sarcophagus [01 §2]. Since 2007 the mummy has lain in a climate-controlled glass case in the antechamber [01 §15]; the empty case is ⟂ the film's OSIRIS move.
- The Sept 2026 survey: a rubble-packed corridor ~2 m wide; 5–6 chambers, 25–30 m north (disputed) [01 §14]. On screen: "two metres wide", empty rooms off both sides.
- The Anubis-shrine brick: "It is I who hinder the sand from choking the secret chamber" [01 Q5]. The fake curse, "Death shall come on swift wings…", was never in the tomb [01 Q6, §17]. The brick is hieratic scratched in mud, found before the Anubis shrine at the Treasury (store-room) entrance [09 §5.1].
- Forbes, Ikram and Kamrin, "Tutankhamun's Missing Ribs" (KMT 18.1): Burton's 1926 photographs show the collar on the chest; by 1968 the collar, ribs and sternum were gone; "when the ribs and sternum came off the heart went with it" [01 §5].
- KV21A: a tentative, statistically inconclusive match as the mother of 317a/317b [01 §9]. SESHAT's "sixty percent" is its own estimate.
- Egyptian blue: near-infrared emission λmax = 910 nm (Accorsi et al. 2009). VIL imaging uses red-rich visible light and a camera with the IR-cut filter removed and a visible-blocking filter fitted; the grains glow white on black [15 §5.2]. **⟂ The pigment's glow is real; writing meant for it is fiction [15 §5].**
- The Apep ritual (Bremner-Rhind, BM EA 10188): a wax image, the name on a new sheet of papyrus, recited so the sun would rise; Apep's names written in red ink [04 §7]. ⟂ The halt-seal.
