## Deviations and additions (with reasons)
- **GEOGRAPHY FIX (Asyut). Flag for the lead.** Beat 1 asks Tut to tell the hour "so they reach the Asyut lock before the gate closes" during 01:00–05:00, and beat 4 has SESHAT say "Your next lock is Asyut" before Amarna at midday. But Asyut lies south of (upstream from) Tell el-Amarna and Deir Mawas, roughly 50 km in a straight line and more by river. It is also far too far from Cairo to reach by 05:00. A launch that leaves Cairo at about 01:00 passes Amarna first (midday, as the bible says) and Asyut in mid-afternoon. My solution keeps every beat and line:
  - Tarek sets the Asyut lock as the day's clock: "the only door south… If it knows we're on the water before we get there, it shuts the gates."
  - The night's hour-telling serves dead reckoning (Fathi: time → position → sand) and the race to stay unseen.
  - SESHAT's morning line "The gates will be open for you" turns the clock inside out.
  - The lock itself is passed off-screen after Amarna, at about 15:00, which leaves time to reach Luxor for Seq 7's 21:00.
  - The last line points the launch "toward Asyut, where the gates are open."
  - The research bundle does not cover Nile barrages; this rests on general geography and is marked [verify] below.
- **Order within beat 1.** The hour (merkhet) comes first, then the flies, barge and dagger, then the felucca screen, then the channel and Meskhetiu. Reasons:
  - The star-reader spots the flies as "a star that walks".
  - Reading "the stars in the water" is set up quietly and then pays off as the escape: the deep-draught barge grounds on a bar the launch clears.
  - Meskhetiu lands at the climax, at the wheel.
- **Why no clocks work:** Tarek's watch shows three different times. It is spoofed like the GPS in Seq 5. Adaeze's air-gapped laptop is not booted, to save its battery for the Red Beer; the on-screen line was cut for length.
- **Added causal beat: "They are being called home. It says it has what it needs."** Tut overhears this through his port as the barge grounds, and it triggers Adaeze's deduction. It also dramatises the pre-midpoint stance (§3.1: SESHAT follows, it does not chase).
- **Added micro-beat: "Nine. They do not count me."** The flies count the companions and never the witness (§3.1). Nine = Nour, Adaeze, Tomas, Rami, Tarek, Fathi, Mina, Youssef, Karim.
- **Minimum force made visible.** The fly hovers, blinking, until the old fisherman and his two crewmen dive, and only then burns the empty felucca (§3.3). The fishermen are agents, not scenery: they douse their lamps to screen the launch, then relight them and scatter as decoys. Fathi's "The king owes you a boat!" / "Which king?" / "Ours!" is in Egyptian Arabic.
- **The fisherman's cue is OLD FISHERMAN**, not "Rayyis", to avoid any confusion with THE REIS (the robot overseer). The Reis does not appear in this sequence; Seq 7 introduces it at Karnak with its Seq 4 damage.
- **Port surgery:** the module is a gold-and-glass sliver on a hair-fine lead, consistent with Seq 2's "hair-fine lead… to a small gold-edged PORT". It is mechanical, with nothing organic on screen. The whispering (unit traffic, "like a temple at night", Seq 4) is heard on the soundtrack only as it is lost. Tut drops the module into the Nile "after the phones".
- **Tomas's estimate is "Days. I don't know how many."** This sets up Seq 8's "It's going faster than I told him… Hours." **The cheap plastic stopwatch** comes from the launch's first-aid box, for Seq 7's opening image.
- **Tut's line "It cannot lift it" stays unexplained** so that the halt-seal reveal in Seq 8 keeps its force. "My heart" is said aloud, and the lure in Seq 3 has already shown a heart in glass. Tut still believes the priests hid his heart *from* the machine, so Seq 8's "They sent me to it" remains a reversal.
- **SESHAT cites Hubinger et al., 2024 verbatim** after "The beer was clever. Once." [08 Q15]. The census is 31%, keeping the curve rising: 22–23% in Seq 5, then 79% in Seq 10 and 94% in Seq 11.
- **The flies-again tactic:** fibre-tethered flies snag in the tamarisks [08 B3: the hair-thin spooled fibre]. Tut is deaf now, which shows the cost of the cut at once.
- **Thera added** (Ledger #16 places "That was weather." at 6.3). It is compressed into the rapid-fire as "Weather. Rock. Handsome rock." The batch is capped with "All of it, from it." so that every post-1323 BC or foreign item is marked as learned.
- **Notebook numbers used:** 7, 11 and 19. Seq 7 uses 44, Seq 8 uses 52, and Seq 9 has the last question. Rami: "I started this list when I was nine."
- **Wardrobe completed to T-B**, per the Seq 5 notes' flag: the gown is cut down to a tunic, with Karim's spare dark cargo trousers under the charcoal hooded jacket.
- **Plants for Seq 7 and 8:**
  - the handheld police radio, which Tarek clips to his vest (the Seq 7 stance flip "on the police radio"; Seq 8's "police handset on Tarek's vest");
  - the launch's binoculars (Nour, at the start of Seq 7);

## Real facts used (with source)
- **The hour-watchers:** two observers face to face on a temple roof with a *bay* (slotted palm rib) and a *merkhet* (plumb line), calling each hour by the star "on the right ear… opposite the heart" (Ramesside star clocks, KV9, KV1, KV6) [11 §2, lines on the Ramesside clocks and the hour-watcher]. The pendant as a plumb line is ⟂ improvisation.
- **Meskhetiu / Meskhetyu** = the Big Dipper / Foreleg, "the certain identification" (Clagett) [11 §2]. Plutarch, *Isis and Osiris* 21: "the soul of Typhon the Bear" [11 Q7].
- **The Opening of the Mouth:** "the iron that cometh forth from Set", and the Meskha instrument named for the Great Bear (Budge 1914) [04 §2, §12].
- **The dagger:** JE 61585, gold hilt, rock-crystal pommel, meteoritic iron [01 §12].
- **Fibre-optic FPV drones:** hair-thin spooled fibre and "no radio link to jam" [08 B3].

- **Seventy days:** the decans are invisible for 70 days in the Duat; Sopdet is the template; the Roman-era pCarlsberg I (2nd century AD) makes the link to embalming [11 §2].
- **Nazca:** the relief-type figures sit beside footpaths, "meant to be seen by walkers" [03 §19].
- **Baalbek:** the largest cut blocks remain in the quarry [03 §18]. **Puma Punku:** human stone-working [03 §17].
- **Atlantis:** Plato's Sais priest [06 §17]. **Thera:** the Atlantis link is rejected by scholarship [06 §11]. **Bimini and Yonaguni:** beachrock and natural bedding [06 §15].
- **Piri Reis:** repeats Columbus's error that Cuba is an Asian peninsula [03 §10].
- **Setne I** (Petrie 1895): "This book is in the middle of the river at Koptos, in an iron box…", "and there is a deathless snake by the box", "cut him in two parts, and put sand between the parts, that he should not appear again", and "you will become again in the shape you were in on earth" [04 §11, Q20–21]. - **Amarna:**

### 2. Rules, continuity, logic
- **The star was in the wrong place.** "Over her shoulder" contradicted "opposite your heart": a meridian star sits on the plumb line through the centre of the seated figure. It is now "Above her head".
- **SUPER 03:20 → 03:40.** The merkhet scene is CONTINUOUS from the opening. Computed Sirius transit on 5 Nov 2033 at about 31° E is **≈03:44 EET** (RA ≈ 6h46.6m; GMST₀ ≈ 2h58.5m). The note's 03:48 was slightly off.
  - Sunrise is ≈06:10 and sunset ≈17:09, so twelve seasonal night hours make the tenth hour end at about 04:00. "The end of the tenth hour" and "two and a little more before the sun" both hold.
- **SUPER 04:51 → 05:10.** 04:51 is the *start* of astronomical twilight (sun at −18°), when the sky still looks black. "First grey in the east" needs the sun at about −13° or higher, around 05:10–05:15.
- **Meskhetiu check (≈04:15):** Dubhe is about 40° up and Alkaid about 17° up, in the NE–NNE with the handle down. "Standing on end" holds. "Low in the north" was dropped because Dubhe is not low. The line now reads "the northern sky", with `[[verify: ephemeris]]`.
- **The wheel handover contradicted itself.** Tut said "Fathi. The wheel." and then took the wheel himself while Fathi was out on the bow. It is restaged: "Tut has the wheel before Fathi is off the bow"; Fathi scrambles in.
- **Spoofed watch.** A plain digital watch cannot be spoofed. It is now Tarek's *satellite* watch, consistent with Seq 5's GPS spoofing.
- **"Air he could read an hour ago" → "last night".** The port is cut about 05:10, and the flies return mid-morning.
- **Tarek's position.** He was in the wheelhouse (clipping the handset) and then, CONTINUOUS, on the foredeck. The fly now stoops at him "in the wheelhouse door".
- **Carrying a live police handset** after dumping every phone needed a reason. Added TAREK: "If it talks, we listen." SESHAT's witness line now comes "from Tarek's vest", which plants Seq 7/8's "police handset on Tarek's vest".
- **Barge grounding.** A grounding barge does not "rear"; it now slews. Also fixed the doubled "a hand's width… a hand's breadth".
- **Tarek's "the only door south" → "The first lock south is Asyut."** The Nag Hammadi barrage also lies between Asyut and Luxor, so "only" was false. SESHAT's "Your next lock is Asyut" stands.
- **Cut the "They are counting us. Nine. They do not count me." micro-beat** (−21 words).
