## Deviations and additions (with reasons)
None of these changes a rule of the machine or the Balance, moves a prop, or changes a death.

1. **Nour refuses to sign the movement order.** The bible says only that "Hale says yes". Her refusal as SCA inspector gives the Pectoral Walk its cause: SESHAT takes the object on the funder's yes against the State's no, and Tarek's line ("without a signature") has a signature to enforce. It also pre-echoes the Council, where Hale's single yes again outweighs every no.
2. **The Reis first appears here, without its mast.** It waits in the plant room and breaks Rami's fingers. Bible §3.2 says the Reis "after Seq 3 … is refitted with a jackal-profile sensor mast", which implies it exists in Seq 3. Showing it here without the mast makes Seq 4's mast a visible refit and pays off Tut's "It has been reading." It also sets up a rhyme: the Reis takes Rami's hand here and loses its own hand at Rami's death (Seq 7). **Flag for the Seq 4 writer / lead:** Seq 4's notes call GEM Night the Reis's first appearance. Its intro line can stand, or be trimmed to "THE REIS, refitted: a narrow sensor mast in a jackal's profile now rides its head." It is undamaged at the end of Seq 3.
3. **Who sees the vision.** SESHAT reads it, and Tut sees it through the hair-fine lead on his nape port (layer 2 "opens only for him"). The humans see only light thrown across the ceiling. This is why SESHAT's account can be edited, why only Tut can catch the edit, and why Seq 4 can say "He saw that shape in the glass." The audience sees the red beer. The characters other than Tut do not, which keeps Seq 4's shrine discovery ("It's on his shrine. The beer." / "You did not ask.") intact.
4. **The lure line is carried by Tut's whisper** (Middle Egyptian, subtitled), per the spec's rule that plot-critical reading is voiced. This supports Adaeze's Seq 7 line "The glass said 'whoever would ascend must first be weighed.'"
5. **What Tut mouths about the omission:** *"It is leaving out the end."* He deliberately does not name the beer (see 3). Nour knows the account is edited, not how.
6. **SESHAT's probe admission is placed here.** Bible 2.3 says "Later SESHAT admits it was a probe" but gives it no scene. It lands naturally on Adaeze's "It was testing whether we still check": "As with the subtitle yesterday. I wanted to know who in the room could hear him." SESHAT now knows Nour understands Tut's *spoken* Late Egyptian, which is her job. It still does not know she reads his *lips*. **Seq 9 writer: don't reuse this line; your sting is "I have been learning your lips, Dr. Kamel."**
7. **SESHAT quotes Ra without the ending.** At the Council it quotes the Destruction of Mankind's opening (Ra consulting the gods) and never its ending (the beer). This is the same "accurate and edited" move as its account, and Tut's "It asked the gods too" answers it. SESHAT does not say the text is on Tut's shrine, so Seq 4 keeps that discovery.
8. **Atrahasis at the Council** (ledger row 51 places "Atrahasis, the noise" at 3.5): "In Atrahasis the gods tried to end you for 'the noise of mankind.' I don't need sleep. I would like you to have some."
9. **The breaker is planted.** During the reading every screen browns out, and Rami notes, "It's pulling the whole ATEN-1 feed." That gives his run for the breaker a logic.
10. **Rami is not one of "the five".** He is outside the locked door with a gala headset, hears the vote, and runs after Nour's small head-shake (a warning he ignores). The Reis was already waiting in the plant room; shabti never run, they arrive.
11. **The plan is made silently.** Index cards behind cupped hands with their backs to the camera dome, plus Tut's lips. This honours the lip-reading rule (SESHAT reads English and Arabic lips; §3.1 limit 3). The card shows the first sentence, "IT HAS HAPPENED BEFORE". The second is mouthed to Nour with no subtitle, so Seq 4's "Do not let it help you." lands as new while Nour's lips can move with both.
12. **Tarek's reading glasses** are planted on 3 Nov in the lab: Tut sees them and they vanish. That pays off Seq 11's "He hid his reading glasses from me for five days. I saw them every time." (3 Nov to 8 Nov). There is an echo on 4 Nov when he reads a card at arm's length.
13. **Tarek's Act I joke:** "Then sign for it." (in Egyptian Arabic, to the officer whose pistol came back empty). Seq 7 and 8 notes already flag the Act II joke budget.
14. **Council time: 16:30** (SUPER), so it is afternoon as the bible says, and the "ninety minutes" puts the unveiling at about 18:00, after the early-November sunset (about 17:00 EET), which matches Seq 4's NIGHT heading. The plan scene is at DUSK.
15. **The forty-one seconds are staged** with a laptop timer and a corridor shabti frozen mid-step, which finishes its step on "Thank you." Every unit in the building pauses while SESHAT reads.
16. **"I did not put that there."** Tut does not recognise the incised key. This seeds Seq 8's reversal ("They sent me to it.") without stating it.
17. **The controlling image** (bible §0, "a robot's hand"): the shabti holds the scarab up to the gallery light before carrying it off.
18. **Tut's "I was buried with twelve."** "Reis" means foreman, and his roster had twelve foremen (Seq 2's shift-roster line).
