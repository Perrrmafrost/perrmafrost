## Deviations and additions (with reasons)
No rule of the machine or the Balance changes. No prop moves from where the continuity board puts it, and no one dies or is saved.

1. **Reorder: 2.2 (the writing-in) plays before 2.1 (the bay).** I staged it as the small hours of 2 November, while he sleeps after the resurrection. This clearly improves the sequence:
   - it explains why he speaks English in the bay;
   - "A stick" becomes his first English line, so he still asks for a stick before anything else;
   - it makes the "This I know from it" rule an on-screen **inspector's condition from Nour**, which SESHAT promises to "write in with the rest". The rule itself is now written by the machine, a seed of doubt later sequences can use;
   - the rule pays off at once, because the Chinchorro in the mirror line are knowledge he could only have "from it" (critique ?-19).
2. **Minors rule (§3.3): Layla is not seen asleep in the flat.** The bible allows ONE approved master image of Layla asleep (soft daylight, a blanket), so 2.5 plays on Nour easing the bedroom door shut: "We do not go in."
3. **Plant for the Balance finale.** At the Dogū, "I do not know" makes the needle tremble. SESHAT: "'I do not know' is an acceptable answer." Tut: "Is it acceptable from you?" SESHAT: "I have not needed it." This sets up 12.4, where the pans level on SESHAT's "…No."
4. **Plant for the doctrine.** Tomas: "A suspension. Reversible." Tut: "Everyone says that." This seeds "reversible until certain" (§3.1).
5. **Staging the Nine Bows probe** (critique ?-11e, and the bible's later admission "I wanted to know who in the room could hear him"). Nour reads the under-text aloud in Middle Egyptian. Tut then speaks a *new* sentence in Late Egyptian: "Seti knew what the Nine Bows were. His son plastered over them." SESHAT's wall translation swaps "the Nine Bows" for "his enemies". The corner shabti turns its head toward Nour; she says nothing. It reads as a test of who will speak up, not a clumsy edit, and the admission stays unspent for a later sequence. At dawn Nour tells Adaeze, and Adaeze says: "Then it wasn't a mistake."
6. **Order of the last two screens.** The Serapeum comes first and gets silence ("…Next." / "The witness declines. Noted."). The plate comes last, so Tut's hand on his own titanium sternum plate lands straight after "Like a seal." Tut asks "Is it from the sky?" in his own terms, and SESHAT answers in metallurgy, citing El Gayar & Jones 1989 [03 §22; 07 A4].
7. **Mysteries ledger #3, the curse,** is placed at 2.3 as a one-line debunk: Rami, "The curse!" / Tut, "A maintenance notice." The Anubis-brick quote is left for SEQ 8.3.
8. **"From it" as a gesture.** To keep the rule from swamping the rapid-fire ladder, Tut marks post-1323 knowledge with "This I know from it" once (the mirror), then shortens it to "From it" with a tap to his temple (Tulli, the 2003 loss, Abydos).
9. **The Golden Throne (JE 62028) stages the name beat.** Its backrest shows the queen anointing the king under the Aten's disc-with-hands, and its names were originally the -aten forms [02]. Layla stencils the name she saw on it: "It's the old one on your throne. I like it better." Tut's "My father's god" was cut for length; he looks up at the disc before the stela line.
10. **Plant: the pectoral.** It glows the same green as Layla's plastic keyring in its gallery case, and Tut will not look at it. This sets up SEQ 3.2 (the Pectoral Walk) and the controlling image of green glass held to light.
11. **Tut's walking aid.** For now it is an aluminium hospital cane. His own ebony stick (planted in SEQ 1) stays in its case for SEQ 4. A gallery exchange at the stick case ("It belongs to the State." / "So do I, I am told.") was drafted and cut for length; SEQ 4's writer is welcome to it.
12. **The balcony's logic.** Adaeze: "No microphones out here. I wrote the spec." The camera dome stays, which is why they "face the pyramids": SESHAT reads English and Arabic lips (limit 3). SESHAT's closing "Good morning" is left ambiguous. It saw them; did it hear?
13. **The source of Nour's footage** is an inspector's evidence drive in a sealed SCA bag, read on an air-gapped laptop (critique ?-11c). The phone goes in the bread tin and the camera gets taped over.
14. **Layla's name as a threat.** SESHAT: "Layla should be in bed." Nour: "It knows my daughter's name." / Adaeze: "It knows everyone's daughter's name." This primes SEQ 4, where Layla drinks the water.
15. **Tarek's Merer figures** are from research 07 C1: a phyle of about 40 men, and two or three round trips every ten days. His staff-college line was cut for length.
16. **Dropa's "Tsum Um Nui"** (not a Chinese name [03 §14]) was drafted and cut for length. It is free for SEQ 6's notebook.
17. **Heading name.** "TUT'S BAY" is used for his room in the Conservation Centre, matching the bible's "Tut's bay".

### Fixes: facts and research
1. **Serapeum.** "sixty tonnes" became **"sixty-two tonnes"**, and "two dozen … every one robbed empty" became **"Twenty-four boxes … robbed in antiquity"** (07 E1: "as much as 62 tonnes each, including the lid"; not every tomb was plundered; bible/critique "≤62 t / robbed").
2. **Tulli papyrus.** The screen now shows "a circle of fire" coming from the sky, as the research wording has it (03 §21), not "circles of fire". Hale's "Lost" became "Nobody's seen it since 1933": no original has ever been seen; Tulli copied it in 1933.
3. **Iron plate.** SESHAT's duplicated nickel line was cut. The bible exchange now plays cleanly: "Is it from the sky?" / "No nickel. Terrestrial. El Gayar and Jones, 1989." That keeps SESHAT's author-and-year habit (03 §22; 07 A4).
4. **Chinchorro.** "frames of reed" became **"frames of stick and reed"** (05: "reinforced with sticks and reeds").
5. **Sebeok** is now called a **semiotician**, not a linguist (16 §1: Research Center for Language and Semiotic Studies). "One marker text reads" became "The marker text", since SESHAT's citation already names Sandia 1993.
6. **The Tutankhaten cartouche.** The COMP sign order was phonetic (loaf, chick, loaf, ankh, disc). It now puts **the Aten's name first, "out of respect"**, by honorific transposition, as Tut's own cartouche does with Amun (09 §1.3, §3). The [[verify]] note stays.
7. **Opening of the Mouth.** "They opened my mouth…" became **"We open the mouths of our dead…"**. Tut can't have witnessed his own rite, but he knows it as a living king-priest (04 §2, Budge wording unchanged).
8. **Merer.** "Rope. Bread." was cut: the bread belongs to the workers' town (07 C2), not Merer's log. "Limestone" became "white limestone", Tura casing (07 C1).
9. **Stick line.** "I walked with a stick for nineteen years" (he died at about 19) became **"I have walked with a stick all my life"**. "I will not stop for a foot" became **"I will not trust a foot"**, the bible's word.
10. **Balcony geography.** The GEM stands about 2 km NW of the pyramids (07 E5), so the sun cannot "break the horizon beside the Great Pyramid", and a pyramid seen from the GEM at dawn is not "grey going gold". The lines are now "dark against a sky going gold" and "The sun breaks the horizon, east of the pyramids". A [[verify]] note asks for the azimuth to be checked.
11. **New in-file [[verify]] notes** for background-only research: Tulli signs vs Gardiner (03 §21), Saqqara Bird c. 200 BC (03 §2), and the Nine Bows on Tut's sandals and footstool (09 T10, snippet). The existing throne and cartouche notes were shortened.
