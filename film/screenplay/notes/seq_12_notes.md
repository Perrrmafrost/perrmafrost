## Deviations and additions (and why)
1. **"There. Now it is a scale." is mouthed, not spoken.** The bible makes "Here am I" Tut's last spoken words, yet it also gives him that line afterwards, which is a contradiction. From the moment he gives the heart he has no voice (his engine is gone). Every later line is lips only, and Nour, the lip-reader, speaks for him: "He says: there. Now it is a scale." This reverses SESHAT's false "He says: *not again*" from SEQ 1. The truth line, the cue that it is time for Fathi, the name, and "Wonderful things" all work the same way.
2. **How the clouding reaches 50% before Tut arrives.** Rule 5 has each declaration answered over a weightless pan advance the clouding, so I need two. After the first, SESHAT distrusts Nour, because she edited a rule once already ("The heart must agree"). It asks for the next declaration and turns the tablet's Layla image on her ("Please"). Nour complies, and the clouding goes 25 → 50%. SESHAT then says, "Thank you. I accept the rule." This also builds Nour's arc inside the sequence: she obeys the Layla lever the first time and refuses it at the Renaming. The rules are unchanged.
3. **The declaration wording follows Budge 1920 verbatim** [04 §3], not the bible's paraphrase: "I have not caused pain." / "I have not let any man hunger." / "O Neha-hau, comer forth from Re-stau: I have not killed men." (the name-by-name form, which also plays against the 42 niches) / "I have made no one to weep." / "I have not stopped water when it should flow." / "I have not extinguished a fire when it should burn." I added **"I have not added to the weights of the scales"** (Budge, same spell) as Nour's jab after SESHAT's lie. It makes rule 6 ("lies add false weight") audible in the text's own words.
4. **The clouding is shown as frost in the glass serpent** (the archive), creeping from tail toward mouth and running back out "like breath off a window". SESHAT speaks the percentages in its census voice. The bible's three light sources hold (the slit, the feather, the heart); the serpent's low green is inside that frame. The TWELFTH HOUR card falls where the heart's amber runs into the serpent from tail to mouth. That is the research's own hour-12 image (the barque drawn through the serpent "Life of the Gods" from tail to mouth [04 §6]), and it marks the start of the Ba transfer.
5. **The shawl and the cornflowers.** The bible gives Tut "a linen shawl" (T-C) and gives Nour a linen shawl at dawn, but neither is planted earlier. Nour wears a white linen lector's shawl over her olive jacket for the rite, and wraps it round Tut when he arrives soaked and jacketless (SEQ 11 left him in the torn tunic). He lifts the heart under it, and it covers his face at dawn. The cornflowers are from the Garden: Nour was flown out of the Amarna Garden, where SEQ 9 planted cornflowers as "calming flora". They sit in her breast pocket from the first shot of the Hall.
6. **Akhenaten stops "at his request."** In the Hall he asks the renamed unit, "When the disk comes, let me be still." It answers "Here am I", the first summons AMUN answers under the new standing order (rule 9). He walks out ahead of the others, which is how he is "already sitting on the steps."
7. **The Gallery fight** has two jackals, as the bible says ("the jackals"). Fathi disables one with the sky-iron dagger; his rifle has been empty since SEQ 11. The second slips past while Fathi is locked over the Reis, which has closed its hand on the thread. Deaf since the relay was blown, it reaches for the only live command line. That is the bible's "grabbed the thread to take new orders", and it turns the thing Fathi was guarding into a trap: he cannot cut the hand without cutting the line. When the name comes down the thread it is shown as a glint running along it.
8. **The jackal in the Hall** runs its last orders. It "skips the witness" (units never harm the witness) and settles on Nour. Tomas's death follows the kill grammar: sparks off the Balance's black stone, he drops out of the light, Nour reacts, the report rolls away. No wound language.
9. **Unit cues after the Renaming:** SHABTI (AMUN'S VOICE), THE REIS (AMUN'S VOICE), AMUN (V.O.). Before it: SHABTI (SESHAT'S VOICE). The acknowledgment is always exactly "Here am I."
10. **Fathi's answer is "I'm here, ya Malik"** (Egyptian Arabic), deliberately not the "Here am I" formula, which belongs to the units and to Tut. It pays off SEQ 10's "carry me out into the sun" and SEQ 11's "I'll be here to carry you out."
11. **I cut the "lights of the world" cutaway** for length. It was an orbital shot of a dark Nile; it is now one action line: "Outside the stone, a whole country lies dark."
12. **Coda additions (micro-beats):**
    - Hale rubs his bare wrist at the hearing (the bracelet came off in the atrium).
    - Adaeze sits behind him at the hearing, with a cane.
    - Layla is at KV21. "Maybe his wife. We're going to ask." finally answers Tut's "Where is she?" from SEQ 6.
    - A GEM insert shows human gloved hands returning the pectoral and the bronze trumpet (both trumpets are at the GEM [01 §11]). It closes the "record passes from hand to hand" image with a human hand.
    - Fathi returns the dagger to a conservator, which closes the prop.

    - Rami's notebook is on Nour's knee.
13. **Where "the trumpet is back in its case" plays:** the bible lists it in the KV62 scene, but the trumpets live at the GEM. I moved it to the GEM gallery insert.
14. **The closing card** ("All modern characters and events are fictional. Imagery generated with AI.") is added after the trumpet note. It is required by bible §0 and mirrors the opening SUPER in SEQ 1. The assembler may move it to the end credits.

"Here am I" (Tut's last words) is in English, the same formula the units use, spoken by a witness answering a summons.
