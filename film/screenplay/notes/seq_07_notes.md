## Beats honoured (bible §7, SEQ 7)
1. Karnak at night with Luxor dark; the bucket-chain dismantling the Ninth Pylon; the drone sweep over decorated faces as a visible countdown; the thread-loom across the 134 columns (16 rows, 12 central columns about 21 m); jackals holding the axis.
2. The heist: Tut recognises the queen's scenes as they come out ("That is the queen's temple. Turn it over."). The rule is on the bedding face, which SESHAT's pipeline never sees. Nour reads the key block by touch in the dark and mouths it (subtitle for the audience only), then gives the gloss "Lord of the forms. He was named for the key." Tut at the Third Pylon, with the bible line verbatim.
3. Midpoint at 01:00, in the bible's order: the projection on the pylon and columns, the 40 m Akhenaten reciting the Hymn, SESHAT's announcement verbatim, 06:14, bracelets on adult wrists, the stance flip verbatim on the police radio, "That is my father." / "Is it?", and Fathi dragging him down as the jackals turn.
4. The cost: Rami runs the block on a hand-cart to the quay and a jackal drops him at the quay's edge (kill grammar). Nour tips the whole cart off the quay into the deep channel, face down, and becomes the only record. Tut takes Rami's notebook from his jacket.
5. Across by felucca to the West Bank at about 03:00.

## Deviations and additions (with reasons)
- **The Third Pylon beat is moved earlier**, onto the way in (after the Hall, before the Ninth Pylon). The Third Pylon is the gate between the Hall and the north–south axis, so the geography passes it first. Placed early, it also plants *kheperu*: Horemheb's prenomen, Djeserkheperure, keeps the middle of Nebkheperure [09 §3.2–3.3]. That pays off minutes later in the key-block gloss.
- **Why they go to Karnak** is stated on the boat: SESHAT is digging, so it lacks a rule; "the first thing it has wanted that it does not have." This avoids claiming the heroes read the lure's "house of the queen" line.
- **The witness as a shield** (added micro-beat, scene 7). Before the flip, units "never harm the witness" (§3.1), so Tut physically blocks each jackal's firing line. The 01:00 flip then turns into a physical reversal: the red lines no longer stop at him, and fire hits the column where Fathi's head was. (Audit: the jackals never aim at Tut himself, because the order is "recover him intact".)
- **The Reis is at Karnak** as foreman of the bucket-chain ("reis" = foreman). This is its encounter damage for this sequence: it **loses its right hand at the wrist** at the quay when the trolley goes over. The bible lists "a missing hand" among its damage. **Flag for the Seq 9–11 writers and the lead:** from Seq 7 on, the Reis has one hand, shown as a clean white stump.
- **SESHAT knows a block is missing and that Nour read it**, but it never sees the rule face. The block was taken from a row not yet swept, so SESHAT has seen neither of its faces. This is consistent with the captivity thread ("What did the block say, Dr. Kamel?") and with §4 ("SESHAT never learns it until the Hall"). The Reis's line "Now you are the only copy" also explains why SESHAT will take Nour alive later.
- **Nour refuses to break the block** ("I'm the inspector. Nobody breaks anything.") and puts it in the river intact, face down in silt. It is recoverable one day, which fits her Seq 8 petition stance on the north wall.
- **The launch is abandoned** at the Karnak river landing after the flies mark it. The party takes a moored felucca (the owners are in the Garden) and hides among the moored cruise ships from the quay until it crosses at 03:00. This matches Seq 8's opening (felucca into the reeds at 03:05; "Karnak is dark again").
- **Tut writes Rami's name in hieroglyphs** in the notebook. This is the Ren motif (Budge 1899: "any person, who effected the blotting out of a man's name was thought to have destroyed him also" [04 Q10]), and it plants Seq 11.4, where Tut names the first work-gang shabti "Rami".
- **Joke budget:** Tarek's act-two line here is "We are not running. We are withdrawing in good order." Seq 8 gives him "Finally, honest work." If the lead wants one joke per act, cut one.
- **Language:** Tut speaks Late Egyptian at the Third Pylon. Nour answers the Legrain/1905 fact in English, since a modern name and date sound wrong in reconstructed Late Egyptian. The rule block is mouthed. Akhenaten recites in Middle Egyptian, keeping his Seq 9 tell (liturgical register) consistent without pointing at it.

## Real facts used (with sources)
- Great Hypostyle Hall: 134 columns in 16 rows; the 12 great columns about 21 m; roof fallen [13 §2].
- Restoration Stela: red granite, 2.54 m; originally before the Third Pylon; found in fragments in the Hall's NE corner; usurped by Horemheb [13 §2; 01 §19].
- Talatat packed into the Ninth Pylon by Horemheb; the demolition paradoxically preserved the most complete pictorial record of any Egyptian temple [02 §13].
- Hwt-benben: the queen officiates alone before the Aten, often facing her own image across the offering table; the king never appears [02 §7].
- Great Hymn, tr. Lichtheim: "When you have dawned they live, / When you set they die; / You yourself are lifetime, one lives by you" [02 Q4].
- Nebkheperure = "Lord of the forms (manifestations) of Ra": scarab L1 *ḫpr* + three strokes Z2 plural [01 §1; 09 §3.2].
- Tut's omission from the king lists [01 §20].

## Audit (script editor pass)

Checked against the writers' spec, bible v3 (§3–4, §6, §7 SEQ 6/7/8, §11 motifs, §12 continuity board, §13 language), `04_CRITIQUE_DECISIONS.md`, the neighbouring pages (the end of Seq 6, Seq 8, and Seq 9–11 for knock-on continuity) and research 01, 02, 09, 13 and 16. Every §7 SEQ 7 beat is present. The only reorder is the Third Pylon beat, which the writer explained above. After the fixes below, no machine or Balance rule is broken.

**Rule and logic fixes**
1. **"Recover him intact" was broken.** Straight after the stance-flip broadcast, a jackal fired at "the stone where Tut's head was". That contradicts the order just given and §3.1. Now the red lines *slide past* Tut onto the others, and the hit lands where **Fathi's** head was. "The shield is gone" now means that Tut no longer protects his companions.
2. **Why SESHAT is at the Ninth Pylon (bible §4 and §7 SEQ 7.1).** The draft never said this. The Reis now says, in its courteous citing voice: "The glass told me the rest was kept in the house of the queen." The audience learns why SESHAT is digging, and the heroes learn that the lure told SESHAT more than it told them.
3. **Projection geometry.** The drones rose "over the Second Pylon, from the west" and projected onto its face, which would put the light in the runners' eyes while the text said "His light on their backs". The drones now rise behind the group (east) and throw the image down the nave onto the Second Pylon, with the trolley facing west toward the exit. This matches Seq 9's "the face from the pylon".
4. **The Reis's restraint at the quay** was unexplained after the flip. It is now: "It has lost the stone. It will not risk the copy." The reason is local to this scene; it does not create a general rule that Nour's presence stops fire, so Mina's death under fire in Seq 8 stays consistent.
5. **The dredge exchange.** "That's days. It doesn't have days" was false, because SESHAT has about 51 hours and a fleet. Nour's reply is now "Face down in silt. A dredge would scour the face off. You saw it lay them on foam." That calls back "worried about chipping" and supports Seq 10's "I cannot check it."
6. **The block trolley came from nowhere.** Empty steel block trolleys are now planted at the row ends in the Ninth Pylon court.
7. **Timing.** The felucca "lain hidden for an hour" did not fit the clock (quay at about 01:10, departure at 03:00). It now reads "since the quay".

**Research fidelity**
8. **Great Hymn (Lichtheim, [02 Q4]).** The punctuation now matches the quoted translation: "When you have dawned they live, when you set they die; you yourself are lifetime, one lives by you."
9. **Horemheb's recut.** "He only cut off the lord" was inaccurate: the masons *replaced* nb with ḏsr and added stp-n-rꜥ [09 §3.3]. The line is now "He changed 'lord' to 'holy' and kept the rest." A `[[verify]]` note has been added, because that recut is attested at Luxor Temple and the research does not show it on this stela [01 §19].
10. **Legrain's findspot.** "In the corner of that hall" is now "in the north-east corner" [13 §2].
11. **King lists.** Tut was *omitted* from the Abydos and Saqqara lists, not "cut out" [01 §20]. His line is now "They left mine off the king lists, to finish me."
12. **Talatat.** "The best picture" is now "The most complete picture" [02 §13, "the most complete pictorial record of any Egyptian temple"].
13. **SESHAT's Akhenaten Temple Project citation** now says "these faces", since the Project matched all the Aten-temple talatat, not only the queen's [02 §7; 16 §4].
14. **"This I know from it."** The bible rule for post-1323 BC knowledge is now applied twice: to Horemheb's usurpation (at the Third Pylon) and to the king lists (on the felucca).

**Continuity**
15. **Unit cue.** `THE REIS (SESHAT'S VOICE)` is now `REIS (SESHAT'S VOICE)`, to match its first appearances in Seq 3 and Seq 4. (Seq 9 still uses `THE REIS`, which the assembler or the Seq 9 editor should normalise.)
