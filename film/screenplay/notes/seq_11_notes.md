## Deviations and micro-beats added (with reasons)

- **Opening bridge scene (the truck).** Seq 10 ends on foot at the service-tunnel mouth at about 22:30, and Seq 11 opens at Giza at midnight. A short drive scene joins the two. Fathi already answers "carry me out into the sun" in Seq 10 ("Until then, you walk"), so I did not repeat that exchange. Seq 10's "Until then, I walk" now pays off when he walks without the stick.
- **Tut knows the crawlway because he crawled it at nine** (the 1332 BC Weighing). ⟂ This is a micro-beat that justifies his confidence in a route no one has explored. The Osiris Shaft itself is Late Period, so Tut says "a tomb someone built on top of a door." It does not conflict with Seq 9's Amarna memory (b).
- **The work-gang graffito appears in the ⟂ crawlway.** The real text is from the Relieving Chambers [07 A6]; placing it in the crawlway is fiction.
- **One pump, not "the pumps".** A single big dewatering pump with a grille low in the moat keeps the choreography readable (rifle into the impeller). The mechanism of the jam: the rifle stalls the impeller, and Tarek must hold it under water so the pump cannot shake it loose. The water stops a hand's width below the tunnel lip. That gap is what his death buys, because Adaeze could not survive eight flooded metres on one leg. Tut offers to take his place ("I do not need the air"). Tarek refuses: "You need the room upstairs." This closes the obvious plot hole that Tut, who does not breathe, could have held the rifle himself.
- **The relay versus the thread** is stated on screen (Adaeze) before the split: blowing the relay means no live override inside, so the summons works, but the thread must stay whole. Fathi physically lifts the thread aside before the charge. This sets up Seq 12, where the Reis grabs the thread to take new orders.
- **The Reis does not answer Tut's summons** ("It has a jackal's head now. It does not hear me."). This is my inference from the bible's refit note, a jackal-profile mast, and it explains why Tut cannot simply command it. It stays compatible with Seq 12: the Reis obeys only the centre, via the thread, when AMUN's name arrives.
- **Tut shields Adaeze and Fathi with his own body up the Gallery** ("Units never harm the witness"). This uses the §3.1 rule as tactics and lets the jackal fire only when he is torn out of the line.
- **Tarek's charcoal jacket (T-B) is lost to the Reis in the same grab that takes the stick.** Tut climbs into the stone in his white linen tunic. The board shows T-B for S11 and T-C for S12, and this gives Seq 12 an on-screen reason for T-C. He has no shawl yet; the linen shawl of T-C is Seq 12's to supply.
- **Rami's notebook moves into a waterproof map case (Tarek's) tucked into Tut's tunic** before the flood. It survives both the water and the loss of the jacket, per the board (Tut keeps it in S12).
- **The conservation kit is lost in the flood.** Rami's converted camera sinks. Seq 10 already spent the last jar of Egyptian blue, and the board shows the kit gone by S12.
- **Nour reads "Bridge the two"**, not Tut or SESHAT. SESHAT asks for "a human voice on the record", in keeping with its fear of voiding the Weighing (the §4 reason it chose a human lector). This keeps plot-critical reading in a character's voice and gives Tut and Nour their one silent look down the Gallery.
- **Adaeze stops Fathi cutting the inch-worm's tether** ("Leave it. It's opening the door for us."). This line answers the audience question of why the heroes don't stop the door.
- **Seeds for Seq 12:** "They know what they are for" (Tut, of the work gang), with Adaeze looking at him a moment too long. This seeds her forty-third declaration, *"I know what I am for."* → "…No." "A servant with a name can be called" seeds the Renaming and rule 9.
- **Nour's preamble line** ("…I know thee, and I know thy name…") drifts down at the end at 05:20. It is the preamble, not a declaration, so Seq 12 keeps the first declaration and the unmoving pan.
